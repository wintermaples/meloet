# SOUND — fixed in the second pass (2026-11-25)

- crates/meloet-sound/src/synth/poly.rs:535 — a held note's `filter_env` is multiplied once per 32-frame chunk with no floor; after 11–90 s of one held note (bass, lead, pad) it sits at a denormal number. One operation per chunk per voice, so the cost is negligible; only the rule of flushing tiny values in loops.
- crates/meloet-sound/src/sampler/player.rs:476-482, 223-227 — a pack region's semitones are not clamped (only SF2 shapes are); `instrument.json` allows `pitchKeytrack` up to ±1200 %, and a key far from `root` then gives a `step` so big that `last + 3` in `mix` overflows (debug panic, or in release a wrong fast path whose slice index panics) on the audio thread. Packs are made by `tools/sounds/build.py`, so not reachable today; a clamp like `MAX_SHAPED_SEMITONES` for all regions would close it.
- crates/meloet-sound/src/sampler/player.rs:35, 709-714 — the comment of `MAX_SHAPED_SEMITONES` says a shaped voice is never further than 120 semitones from its file, but the modulation envelope, vibrato and LFO cents (each clamped to ±12 000) are added per chunk on top, up to ±360 semitones. No crash on 64-bit; the comment or the clamp could be made to agree.
- crates/meloet-sound/src/sf2/zones.rs:261-311 — the work is capped only by the number of zones made: a damaged preset with up to 16 384 local zones whose instruments each have up to 16 384 zones that all fail (sample out of range, no key overlap) loops about 2.7e8 times and builds a 16 384-zone `Vec` per preset zone; the SF2 load (and `keys`, `key_names`) can then take minutes on its thread.
- crates/meloet-sound/src/sf2/mod.rs:160-169 — `Sf2File::keys` takes `key_lo.min(127)`, so a zone whose key range lies wholly above 127 (damaged file) marks key 127; it also counts zones `spec` would drop (ROM sample, bad rate, sample out of range). `key_names` skips `key_lo > 127` but not the unplayable ones.
- docs/architecture.md 25-23 (last line) — names `Gate::gain` and `DeEsser::cut_db` as if they could be read; in `crates/meloet-sound/src/effects/gate.rs` and `de_esser.rs` they are private fields with no accessor.
