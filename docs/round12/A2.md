# A2: whole sections (copy a section, take a section out, put empty bars in)

## Architecture

What goes into chapter 25 of `docs/architecture.md`.

- **One rule for all three** (`crates/meloet-model/src/sections.rs`, pure, no I/O). `Splice { keep, gap, resume }`: the new song is the old one before `keep`, then `gap` empty ticks, then the old one from `resume` on.
  - empty bars at `at`: `keep = resume = at`, `gap` = the bars (`insert_time(doc, at, bars)`)
  - a section `[from, to)` out: `keep = from`, `resume = to` (`remove_time(doc, from, to)`)
  - a copy of `[from, to)` right after it: `keep = to`, `resume = from` (`duplicate_range(doc, from, to)`). What lies between `resume` and `keep` is there twice: once where it was, once moved. So "the copy, then what was after it" is simply "the old song from `from` on, moved by the section's length", and there is only **one seam** (at `to`), not two.
  - Every cut must be the head of a bar (`Bars::of`; else `Unchanged`). `gap` is `bars × ticks_per_bar` of the meter at the cut.
  - The three functions return a `Doc`; `duplicate_section`, `delete_section` and `insert_space` take a marker, work out its section and return an `Edit` with `StepKind::{DuplicateSection, DeleteSection, InsertSpace} { marker, bars }`.
- **The section of a marker** (`section_of`, `section_from`, `song_end`): from the marker to the next marker that is later (markers on the same tick share one section), after the last one to the latest clip end rounded up to a bar. `Section::{Usable { from, to, bars }, NotOnBars, Empty}`. Core's `MarkerView::section` (`play/wave3.rs::sections`) now calls the same function, so the view and the command can not disagree.
- **What moves** (everything that has a place in the song):
  - **Clips**: first every clip over `keep` or `resume` is cut there with the existing `split_clips` rule (the right half keeps the takes with moved offsets; a keys take with a note over the cut gets a new take for the left half; a vocal clip keeps the fade-in on the left half and the fade-out on the right one). Then a clip that starts before `keep` stays, one that starts at or after `resume` appears moved; one that does both (a copy) gets a new `ClipId` and shares the takes (`Arc`). Notes, control points, take offsets, fades, `AudioTake::sync` are counted from the clip or the take and are not touched.
  - **Markers**: the same rule on the tick; a copy has a new `MarkerId` and the same name.
  - **Lanes** (every track's, and `Doc::master_lanes`, the same code: `splice_lanes`): see "seams" below. A lane whose points change gets a new `LaneId` from `next_id` and shares unchanged chunks (`ChunkedVec::sharing`); a lane whose points stay keeps its `Arc` and its id.
  - **Tempo points** and **meter changes**: see below.
  - **The loop**: time put in at `keep` (empty bars or a copy) moves both ends when the loop starts at `keep` or later, and only the end when `keep` is inside it. Taking `[from, to)` out moves an end inside it to `from`; a loop that ends up empty goes (`None`; core then turns the loop off, as after any undo that removes the range). The loop is not copied: a loop over the copied section stays on the original.
  - Mute sets hold track ids and nothing with a place: untouched.
- **Seams of a lane** (`splice_points`). The line on each side of a seam must stay what it was. First every point a seam could need is put in: one a tick before the seam with the old value there (ends the left side), one on the seam with the value the right side starts with, and with a gap one where the gap starts (holds the value). Then the points at those ticks that the line is the same without (`not_needed`, within 1e-6) are taken out again. So a flat line gets no points, a slope cut by empty bars gets two, and where the two sides do not meet there is a jump of two points one tick apart (the rule of 21-5). In a stepped lane the value just holds, so most seams need nothing.
- **Seams of the tempo line** (`splice_tempo`; the head is treated as a point at tick 0). A ramp always ends on the next point's tempo. So:
  - the tempo before the seam does not ramp: a point on the seam only when the right side starts with another tempo or ramps on (or the right side has a real point there, which is kept);
  - a ramp comes to the seam and the right side starts with the tempo it came to: a point on the seam with that tempo;
  - a ramp comes to the seam and the right side starts with another tempo: the ramp is ended **one tick before the seam** by a point that does not ramp (or, when the ramp starts on that very tick, that point stops ramping), then the point on the seam;
  - empty bars: the tempo that came up to the cut is held through the gap, and the ramp goes on from the end of the gap (the spec's rule); at the head, the head keeps its tempo and its ramp moves to the end of the gap.
  - Afterwards (`tidy_tempo_seam`, not for empty bars) the points at the seam that are on the line anyway go again, so that "bars in, the same bars out" and "copy, the copy out" give the points back.
- **A tempo is kept in hundredths.** A point made inside a ramp is rounded, so the line can be off by 0.005 there, and a point made from such a point by 0.01. The tidy step therefore takes "within one hundredth" as on the line. This is also why times before a cut inside a ramp are not bit-identical after the step (the tests allow 0.5 ms in the hand-made case and 2 ms over all the test songs; outside ramps the times are the same).
- **Meter changes** (`splice_meter`, by bar number, head as bar 0). Empty bars: a change on the cut's bar stays (the gap is in that meter) and later ones move by the bars. Else: changes before `keep` stay, changes at or after `resume` move, and a change is put on the seam when the meter there would not be the one at `resume`. Taking bars from the head gives the head the meter that follows.
- **Limits**: every moved position goes through `Splice::moved`, which says `OffTheSong` past `MAX_TICK` (bar numbers past `u32` too). Nothing is changed then; core says `StatusMessage::SongTooLong`.
- **Core** (`crates/meloet-core/src/play/timeline.rs`): `duplicate_section`, `delete_section`, `insert_space` (bars `1..=INSERT_SPACE_BARS_MAX`), all through `section_step`: refused while `busy()`; an old marker id is `NoSuchPoint` and does nothing; a section that is not `Usable` does nothing. The step goes through the normal `apply`, so the engine gets the new song by the existing path (`sync_seats` sends `Sequence` and `Clips` for every track whose clips changed, `Timeline` when the map changed, the loop, the lanes). Then everything selected is let go (clips, notes and control points, the marker or point of the timeline, lane points) and that empty selection is written into the step's "after" (`sel_undo`), so undo brings the old selection back and redo empties it again. The play line is not moved.
- **Audio made for a clip** (stretch): its key holds `tempo_piece` (the tempo under the clip, counted from the clip's start), so a clip that is only moved keeps its made audio; a clip that was cut is made again for each half.
- **Screen**: `MarkerRow.svelte` has the three rows after 「ここから次のマーカーまでをループに」 and a separator. `MenuRow` of the context menu got `hint` (the reason a row is disabled, shown in the row under the pointer; `MenuList` already had it for the menu bar). The box of 「この前に空きを入れる…」 is `timeline/spaceBox.svelte.ts` (which marker) and an input drawn by `MarkerRow` at the marker, in the look of A1's repeat box; `valueClamp.readBars` reads it (M5 can use the same function for its box).
- **Fake** (`ui/src/bridge/fakeSections.ts`): the same splice for clips (cut without new takes), markers, tempo points, meter changes and the loop; it moves no lane points and adds no seam points; no undo. Scene `sections`.
- **Shot tool**: `tools/design/shoot_ui.py` got a `mouse:` step (the real pointer goes to an element, so CSS `:hover` shows), used for the disabled rows.

Traps met:
- `tempo_from` / `tempo_before` must use the **old** list for the right side of a copy: the ramp flag of the last kept point may have been cleared by the seal.
- Meter bars must only be turned into `u32` when a change is really put there; otherwise a far marker makes every step "too long".
- A scene's "おわり" marker can be past the test's 1000 px arrange and is then not drawn.

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md`, "Rules added in round 12":

- A section is from a marker to the next marker that is later, and after the last marker to the song's end (the latest clip end, up to the head of a bar).
- A marker's right-click menu has, after 「ここから次のマーカーまでをループに」: 「この区間をすぐ後ろに複製」「この区間を消して詰める」「この前に空きを入れる…」, then a separator.
- The three rows can be used only when both ends of the section are heads of bars. Else they are disabled; the row under the pointer says 「区間の両端が小節の頭のときに使えます」, or for the last marker with nothing after it 「このマーカーより後ろにクリップがないので、区間がありません」. While counting in or recording they are disabled without a reason.
- 「この前に空きを入れる…」 opens a box of digits at the marker, 4 at first and chosen. Enter puts in that many bars of the meter at the marker (a number outside 1–64 becomes the nearest end and the status line says so; what is not a whole number can not be decided), Esc or a press outside closes it, ↑ ↓ change it by 1 (10 with Shift). While it is open the status line says 「4 小節の空きを入れます」.
- Empty bars: a clip over the place is cut there; what is at the place or later moves (clips, lane points, markers, tempo points, the loop); meter changes after the place's bar move by the bars; the tempo and the lane values that came up to the place stay through the empty bars; the loop grows when the place is inside it.
- Taking a section out: clips are cut at both ends; what is inside goes (the marker too); what follows comes up; the tempo, the meter and the lane values that followed go on from the seam; a loop all inside goes.
- A copy: clips are cut at both ends; the copy has new clips on the same takes, the same lane points, tempo points and meter changes, and a marker of the same name; it starts with the tempo and the meter of the section, and what follows keeps its own.
- Each is one step: 「区間を複製」「区間を消して詰める」「空きを入れる」; the row of the history band says 「A メロ ・ 8 小節」. Nothing stays selected; undo brings the selection back. The play line does not move.
- A song that would get longer than it can be: nothing happens, and the status line says 「曲が長くなりすぎるので、できませんでした。」.

No keys. Step names are already in the list.

## Readings

Decisions where the spec said nothing:

- **All three rows need a usable section**, 「この前に空きを入れる…」 too (the lead's request: "disabled states … per `SectionUse`"). So the last marker at the song's end (`Empty`) can not get empty bars before it, although only its own tick matters for that. Question for the owner: should 「空きを入れる」 only need the marker on a bar head?
- **Markers on one tick** share a section: all of them are copied, and all go with the section.
- **The mock's first row** 「この区間をループに」 is not used: the existing row 「ここから次のマーカーまでをループに」 (spec 3) stays as it is; the text says "add three rows".
- **The reason of a disabled row** is shown in the row under the pointer (the menus' existing way), not as a floating tool tip.
- **Empty bars and a lane**: the bars hold the value at the cut. For a straight lane with a jump on the cut (two points a tick apart), that is the value the jump goes to; for a stepped lane it is the value before the step, and the step comes after the bars.
- **Empty bars and the meter**: a change on the cut's bar stays, so the bars are in the new meter (the spec: 「at の拍子での n 小節」, "changes after at's bar move").
- **Ending a ramp or a slope one tick before the seam** (see Architecture) is how "the points that are needed" was read where the two sides do not meet.
- **Points at a seam that the line is the same without are left out**, also ones that were there before. So that bars in and out again give the song back. What a round trip gives back exactly, and what not:
  - *Empty bars at a place, then the same bars out* (`insert_time`, `remove_time(at, at + gap)`): the `Doc` is the same but for `next_id`, the ids of the lanes whose points changed on the way, and:
    - a clip that was over the place stays cut in two (the left half of a keys clip has a new take when a note was over the cut, and that note ends there);
    - a lane point exactly on the place that was on the line of its neighbours is gone (within 1e-6);
    - a tempo point exactly on the place that ramps on and was on the ramp of its neighbours (within 0.01) is gone;
    - a meter change exactly on the place that said the meter in force before it is gone.
  - *A copy, then the copy out* (`duplicate_range`, `remove_time(to, to + len)`): the same list, with "the place" being `to`, and the clips over `from` and over `to` stay cut.
  - The tests check whole-`Doc` equality (but `next_id` and lane ids) for 6 tempo shapes × 6 sections × {1, n, 7 bars; copy} without clips over the cuts, and the timeline, markers, loop and lane points with clips over them.
- **The loop** is not copied and follows the rule of empty bars for a copy.
- **A section longer than `u32::MAX` bars** counts as not usable.
- **Selection**: clips, notes and control points, the timeline's selection and lane points are all let go. The piano roll keeps showing its clip when it is still there.
- **A typed count outside 1–64** is put to the nearer end and sent; `clampValue` (`field: bars`, range 「1〜64」) tells the status line (「小節は 1〜64 なので、64 にしました。」, the existing sentence with the existing word 「小節」).
