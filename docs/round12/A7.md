# A7: turn a keys track or keys clips into an audio clip (round 12)

Spec: `docs/history/round12-spec.ja.html` A7 (lines 526-544). Protocol 33 shapes were already in; no shape was added.

## Architecture

What goes into chapter 25 of `docs/architecture.md`.

- **One background job, the export's own.** A bounce is a job of the export (`ExportJob`, the second engine, plugins in the export's own plugin processes started one at a time by `Hosts` / `launch::spawn`, live plugin states asked as a save asks them). Nothing in `export/job.rs` changed: the worker (`service/bounce.rs`) starts the job with `export_render` and gives it at once an `ExportTarget` that is a wav in the work place under a name of its own (`.bounce-<pid>-<job>.wav`), at the engine's rate, in the settings' 「録音の形」 (16 / 24 / 32 bits). The job renders, converts and moves the file there (a plain rename in the same folder tree as the export's work folder), and says `ExportNews::Finished`.
  - Job ids start at `BOUNCE_JOBS` (1 << 47), apart from the dialog's (from 1) and below the AI link's (1 << 48), so `Worker::ai_jobs` does not count a bounce. Their news come as `Input::Export`; `Model::export_news` gives those of a bounce id to `state/bounce.rs` first.
  - No work place (no state folder): the worker answers `Failed { CannotWrite }` at once. The AI link's export or measuring runs: the worker answers `Input::BounceBusy` (the rules do not know the AI link's jobs; the core thread does).
- **The file goes into the song as A9's do** (`audiofiles/bounced.rs`, new keeper ask `Adopt`, news `AudioNews::Adopted`): the thread that reads audio files in reads the file once for its peaks (`PeakBuilder`), names it with A9's `put_in_place` (a hard link, so it never takes a name a recording got at the same moment; " 2", " 3" … when taken), keeps the peaks under a fresh uid and tells the store (`file_arrived`). The wanted name is `vocalrec::file_name("（元の名前）（音）", next_id)`. The temporary file is gone afterwards, also when this fails.
- **The phases in the rules** (`state/bounce.rs`): `Rendering` → `Written(temp)` (Finished came) → `Taking` (the keeper was asked) → `Made(audio)` → the step, in `bounce_follow` after every input, once the song may change (not counting in or recording, no AI batch open). The step is `Play::bounce_made` → `meloet_model::bounce_track` (`crates/meloet-model/src/audio/bounce.rs`), one step `StepKind::Bounce { track }`.
- **What is rendered** (`play/bounce.rs` `bounce_doc`): a `Doc` with only that track, not muted, its fader at 0 dB and pan in the middle, its inserts kept, its sends silent, its lanes of the fader, the pan and the sends left out (the other lanes — instrument and insert parameters, mute — move what they move while playing), its arpeggio and sound knobs as they are; the returns without inserts and muted, the master with nothing on it and no master lanes, no markers, loop or mute sets, an empty audio table. The range is the clips' (see Spec rules), the tail `UntilSilent` (−90 dBFS for 0.5 s, 10 s at most), not real time unless a plugin forces it, stopped plugins passed by, the engine's rate.
- **The step** (`bounce_track`): a vocal track right below, 「（元の名前）（音）」 (" 2" … when a track has the name), input 入口 1 ・ モノ, the color of its place, the fader, pan and sends of the track and their lanes (new lane ids), no inserts, not muted; one clip from the range's start, as long as the file sounds at the song's tempo there (as `import_audio`), not following the tempo, no fades, the clip named as the track; the file in the audio table as a recording's (`sha256: None`); the keys track muted, everything else of it unchanged. Undo removes all of it; the file stays in the work place (redo needs it) and moves to `音/` at a save only while a take uses it.
- **Late answers**: the song changed (`Play::serial`) or the track is gone (deleted, an undo past the track's making) → nothing, `BounceGone`, the file is taken out of the work place; 64 tracks by then → `TooManyTracks`, the file taken out. Only the track's id is checked: notes changed during the render are not (the spec only asks whether the track is still there).
- **Cancel** (「やめる」, `CancelBounce`): while rendering, `ExportAsk::Drop` (the job takes its work folder away); a `Finished` of that job that comes anyway, or an `Adopted` that comes after the cancel, has its file taken out of the work place (`Effect::DropVocalFiles`, the core thread, a plain unlink). Nothing of a cancelled bounce stays in the work place.
- **Status**: `AudioWorkView::bounce` is filled by the rules (`Model::bounce_view`); `Play::audio_work_view` leaves it `None`. The percent is the job's `Counted` (frames of the range, not the tail), at most 99; 99 from `Finished` until the step.
- **One job of the second engine at a time**: `OpenExport` while bouncing says `ExportWaitsForBounce` (the dialog does not open); the AI link's export and measuring are refused while bouncing (`ai_render_free` → `AiRefusal::Busy(Exporting)`).
- **Trap (pre-existing, engine)**: an effect that follows the tempo (the delay with 「テンポに合わせる」 on) reads the tempo once for each stretch the engine renders, and the stretches are cut at the next note of **every** track (`engine.rs` `frames_to_next_point`, `EffectContext::tempo_bpm`; `delay.rs` `delay_frames`). Inside a tempo ramp, such an effect sounds a little otherwise when other tracks play than when the track is alone, so a bounced track differs from the track in the mix there (up to 5e-3 in the export fixture's song). Fader and send lanes are read in steps counted from the stretch's start (Fable's reading of `engine.rs`), and they differed by about 1.6e-5. Outside ramps, and for effects that do not follow the tempo, the bounce is the same sound. Found with Fable (asked during the work).
- **Another small one**: a clip at a tick whose time is not a whole frame starts at the rounded frame, as every audio clip does; a bounce from such a tick can be a frame off the keys it was made of.
- Tests: model `audio/bounce/tests.rs` (the step, names, length through tempo changes, undo/redo, ids, errors); core `play/bounce/tests.rs` (the render `Doc`; rendered alone, written in 32-bit floats, taken in and placed: the whole song within 1e-4 of before and the track alone the same to the sample; the tail up to 10 s), `state/bounce/tests.rs` (ranges, refusals, the phases, the step and undo, cancel at every phase, waiting for a recording, late answers, failures), `audiofiles/bounced/tests.rs` (naming, peaks, unreadable file), `service/bounce_tests.rs` (the real threads: the step, the work place holds only the song's file, a cancel leaves nothing; and the Surge XT test below).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md`, "Rules added in round 12":

- 「音のクリップにする」 is at the bottom of a keys clip's right-click menu, after a line. It acts on the selected clips; the keys clips among them must be on one track (else the row is disabled and Rust does nothing). The range is from the earliest start to the latest end of those clips. Audio clips do not have the row.
- 「このトラックを音にする」 is in a keys track head's right-click menu, right after 「録音待機」 and a line, in its own group (where a vocal track has 「音のファイルを入れる…」). The range is from the start of its first clip to the end of its last.
- Both are disabled while counting in or recording.
- The track sounds alone over the range — every clip of the track in it, also clips between the selected ones that were not selected — through its instrument and inserts, its instrument and insert lanes moving them; fader 0 dB, pan in the middle, no sends, no inserts of the master; the tail until it is silent (10 s at most).
- The audio goes on a new vocal track right below, named 「（元の名前）（音）」 (「ピアノ（音） 2」 when the name is taken), with the fader, pan and sends of the track and their lanes, no inserts, the color of its place, not selected, input 入口 1 ・ モノ. Its one clip starts at the range's start, does not follow the song's tempo, and is named as the track. The file is the settings' 「録音の形」 at the engine's rate. The keys track is muted (all of it: clips outside the range of 「音のクリップにする」 are muted too).
- One step 「音のクリップにする」 (already in the list); undo takes the track, the clip and the mute back.
- The status line says 「音にしています… 40 %」 with 「やめる」 while it runs, in place of the usual summary (other messages, and the recording's text, come first).
- Refused: while counting in or recording (the rows are disabled); while another 「音にする」, the export (its dialog or its run) or the AI link's export runs: 「書き出しか「音にする」の途中なので、終わってからもう一度ためしてください。」; at 64 tracks: 「トラックは 64 本までなので、これ以上足せません。」; a track with no clip: 「このトラックにはクリップがありません。」.
- While it runs, 「書き出し…」 does not open the dialog: 「音にしているあいだは書き出せません。終わってからひらいてください。」.
- When it is done while counting in or recording, the step is made when that ends. When the track is gone or another song is open: nothing is placed, 「もとのトラックがなくなったので、できた音はすてました。」.
- No keys (spec 4 unchanged).

## Readings

- Wording from the spec (`user:2026-11-23`, `ui/src/text/ja.ts`): `menu.bounceClips` 「音のクリップにする」, `menu.bounceTrack` 「このトラックを音にする」, `msg.bouncing` 「音にしています… {percent} %」; 「やめる」 is the existing `button.cancel`.
- Wording made up (`builder:2026-11-23`, shown on the status line):
  - `msg.bounced` 「{track} に音のクリップを置きました。もとのトラックはミュートしました。」
  - `msg.bounceCancelled` 「音にするのをやめました。」
  - `msg.bounceFailedPlugin` 「音にできませんでした。{plugin} が止まりました。」, `msg.bounceFailedWrite` 「音にできませんでした。作業の場所に書けません。」
  - `msg.bounceGone` 「もとのトラックがなくなったので、できた音はすてました。」
  - `msg.bounceBusy` 「書き出しか「音にする」の途中なので、終わってからもう一度ためしてください。」
  - `msg.nothingToBounce` 「このトラックにはクリップがありません。」
  - `msg.exportWaitsForBounce` 「音にしているあいだは書き出せません。終わってからひらいてください。」
  - If the lead prefers `user:2026-11-23` for these (spec 1-3 lets helpers make such lines), change the tags.
- 「選んだクリップ…いちばん早い始まりからいちばん遅い終わりまで」 is read as "the track over that range": clips between the selected ones that were not selected are in the audio too (the track is muted afterwards, so leaving them out would silence them).
- 「元のトラックは…ミュートします」 is followed as written for 「音のクリップにする」 too: clips of the track outside the range go silent. **Question for the lead/owner**: is that wanted, or should 「音のクリップにする」 leave the track unmuted (or mute only... nothing smaller than a track can be muted today)?
- 「音量、パン、センドは元のトラックから写します」 is read as the values and their automation lanes (a fader lane drives what the fader is; without it the new track would not sound as the old one).
- Lanes of the instrument's and inserts' parameters, and of the mute, stay in the render (they shape the sound "through the instrument and inserts").
- The new track's color is the one of its place (as 「＋ 歌のトラック」), not the track's (as 「トラックを複製」); the spec says nothing. It is not selected (selecting a vocal track would arm it, 24-C).
- The returns are emptied and muted in the render, not only fed nothing: a plugin there would otherwise be started (and could fail the bounce) for nothing.
- 「書き出しのダイアログがひらいているとき」 refused with `BounceBusy` (the protocol says so); while the dialog is open the status line says that it is open (`ExportDialogOpen`) over any notice, so `BounceBusy` is not seen then (the menus are behind the dialog anyway).
- The AI link's export and measuring are refused while a bounce runs (`AiRefusal::Busy(Exporting)`), the other half of 「裏の仕事は1つずつ」.
- 「なければ捨てて知らせます」: the file is taken out of the work place (not kept until the work place is emptied, as A9's dropped answers are).
- The spec says to widen `clip_doc` of `state/ai.rs`; it is left as it is (the AI tools keep their behaviour) and `play/bounce.rs` `bounce_doc` makes the bounce's `Doc`.
- The status line: the progress is a message with a button (as 「音を作っています」 of stretched clips, the other progress of `audioWork`), not the export's bar at the left; the spec gives only the text and the button. The percent is the range's (the tail is not counted) and shows 99 until the step.
- The fake (`ui/src/bridge/fakeA7.ts`) answers at once with a made-up file as long as the range and a second of tail; the scene `bouncing` shows the progress.
- New pictures: scenes `bouncing` (status line 「音にしています… 40 %」 「やめる」) and `bounced` (the step, 「2 ピアノ（音）」), click shot `menuTrackBounce` (the head menu of 「1 ピアノ」); `menuClip` now ends with 「音のクリップにする」. No mock boards for them. Shot in six themes.

## Left for later

- The tempo-synced effect / lane difference inside tempo ramps (see Architecture): the engine could read the tempo at fixed steps instead of at stretches cut by every track's notes. Not fixed here.
- If meloet ends (or the PC shuts down) in the moment between the job's move into the work place and the step, a `.bounce-*.wav` stays in the work place until it is emptied.
- No message for the case "the work place cannot be written" beyond `BounceFailed { CannotWrite }` (said as 「作業の場所に書けません」).
- A bounce is not cancelled when the song is replaced; it renders on and is thrown away at the end (the spec checks at the end). It could be dropped at once.
- Not checked by hand in the real app (no window opened): the menus, the progress and 「やめる」 were checked in the fake and in six themes of screenshots only.
- A8 (comping) does not touch keys clips; nothing to settle with it.
