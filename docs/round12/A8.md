# A8: comping a vocal clip (「いいとこ取り」)

## Architecture

What goes into chapter 25 of `docs/architecture.md`.

**What a comp is** (`crates/meloet-model/src/audio/comp.rs`). `Clip::comp` is empty (the shown take sounds all along, as before) or a list of pieces that covers the whole clip. A piece is `{ from, take }`: from `from` (ticks from the clip's start) to the next piece's `from` (the clip's end for the last), the take at place `take` of the clip's takes sounds. The rules of a list (`comp_is_valid`, the same in the song file's reader, in the fake and on the screen): the first piece at 0, in the order of `from`, every `from` before the clip's end, every take one the clip has, no two pieces in a row of one take. There is no smallest length above one tick (see "the seams" below for why none is needed). A comp of one piece is allowed when its take is not the shown one (that take sounds whole while another is shown).

- **Every take sounds at its own offset**, exactly where it would be as the shown take (`Clip::zero(take)`), so parts of different laps stay in time. The takes never change; a comp only says which one is heard where. Nothing in a take, in the audio table or in the files is touched by `set_comp` or `clear_comp`, so undo and redo bring back the whole `Doc` (one `Arc` swap of the track, as every step).
- `set_comp(doc, clip, (start, end), pieces)`: one step `StepKind::SetComp`. Nothing (`Unchanged`) when the clip has fewer than two takes, when its edges are not `(start, end)` any more (the screen laid the pieces out for other edges), when the pieces break the rules or are none, and when they say what the clip plays already (its comp, or `[{0, shown take}]` with no comp). `WrongKind` for a keys clip, `NoSuchClip` for an old id. `clear_comp`: one step `StepKind::ClearComp`; nothing for a clip with no comp.
- The protocol names takes by id (`CompPiece::take` = `AudioTakeView::take_id`); `Play::set_comp` (`crates/meloet-core/src/play/comp.rs`) turns ids into places in the clip's takes and does nothing when one id is not a take of the clip. Both intents are refused while `busy()`.
- **The whole list is sent, not a range.** `Command::SetComp` carries the full new list and the clip's edges as the screen drew them. The merge rules (a take chosen for a range, neighbours of one take joined, a covered piece gone) are the screen's (`chooseTake` in `ui/src/components/comp/comp.ts`); Rust only checks the result. So the screen and Rust cannot disagree about a merge, and an intent made for an older picture of the clip does nothing.

**What other edits do to a comp** (all in the model, so every caller gets it):

| Edit | The comp |
|---|---|
| Move, duplicate, repeat (A1), copy and paste, duplicate track (A3) | Unchanged: the pieces are counted from the clip's start. Copies share the takes. |
| Trim an edge, type a length (`with_edges`) | `comp_window(comp, shift, length)`: what sounded at a place of the song still sounds there. A piece that is no longer inside the clip goes (it does not come back when the edge is pulled out again); the first piece reaches back to a start pulled out, the last one forward to an end pulled out. |
| Cut (S, 「ここで切る」, and the sections of A2, which cut with the same `split_clips`) | Each half gets the pieces inside it, the right half counted from its own start. A cut on a seam gives the right half the piece that started there. |
| 「次のテイク／前のテイク」 (T, `switch_take`) | The comp goes in the same step and that take sounds whole. |
| 「テイクを消す」 (`delete_take`) | Refused (`Unchanged`) while the clip has a comp: the pieces name takes by their place. |
| 「曲から外す」 of a missing file (`remove_audio`) | The takes of that file go. A comp that names one of them goes; otherwise its pieces are renumbered (`comp_renumbered`). |
| A recording over the clip (`add_vocal_recordings`: the clips it overlaps become one clip, their takes in turn, then the new takes) | `comp_joined`: when one of those clips has a comp, the merged clip plays what they played (a clip without a comp counts as its shown take), re-based to the merged clip's start. The new takes are there to be chosen; the newest is shown, as always. With no comp among them: none, as before. |
| 「逆にする」「ノーマライズ」 (A9) | Passed by: `played_frames` is `None` for a clip with a comp, at the ask and again at the answer (a comp made while the files were being written). |
| 「音程を寄せる」 (M7) | `tune_usable` stays false (the rows are disabled). A tune set before the comp is kept and bends every take that sounds; while the comp is there it can only be taken off. |
| 「音のクリップにする」 (A7) | Keys clips only; nothing to do. |

**How it sounds: the seams are made by the read-ahead thread, not by the engine.** The spec gave two ways (several rows of the engine's clip table laid over each other, or the idea of the read-ahead thread's cross-fade). The second was taken:

- The engine's `ClipTable` cannot hold regions that overlap (`ClipTable::new` refuses them, and `ClipPlayer::render` reads one ring per track), and it should not learn to: the engine multiplies the ring by the clip's fades and volume, and a clip with a comp is still one clip with one fade and one volume. **The audio thread did not change at all** (no new allocation, lock or command; `crates/meloet-engine` is untouched).
- `pcm::feed_pieces(map, rate, clip)` gives the read-ahead thread one `FeedClip` per piece instead of one per clip. Each plays its take's file from where that take is at the piece's first frame (`feed_take`: `feed_clip` for any take). Two neighbours lie over each other around their seam: `FeedClip::lead` and `tail` are the frames a piece comes in and goes out over.
- `Fed::lay` (`readahead.rs`) writes a clip with no `lead` and `tail` exactly as before (the samples are copied; a clip with no comp is bit for bit what it was). A piece is **added** with `FeedClip::seam_gain(frame)`: a straight line from 0 to 1 over `lead`, from 1 to 0 over `tail`. The incoming and the outgoing piece cover the same frames of a seam and their two gains are `w` and `1 − w` of the same `w`, so they always add up to one: two takes that hold the same audio give that audio (within one float step), and the level never dips. A straight line and not an equal-power curve because laps of one song are much alike (an equal-power curve would give a 3 dB bump on like audio).
- **The seam is 10 ms** (`seam_frames`: rate / 100), centred on the piece's tick: 5 ms of each neighbour. **Short pieces**: the half on each side is at most half the shorter of the two neighbours, so two seams never meet and at most two pieces sound at any frame; a piece that has no frame of its own at this tempo and rate is left out. That is why the model needs no smallest piece.
- The list the thread gets stays in the order of its starts with ends in the same order, which `lay` needs for its search.
- **Export equals play** because both go through `made::seat_audio` → `ReadAhead` (the export's inline one). Tested sample for sample, and the inline thread against the real one to the bit.
- **Stretch, pitch and tune with a comp.** The audio made for a clip (`made::plan`) was for the shown take. `plan_take` makes it for any take, **over the whole clip**, with the same key recipe (a clip with no comp has the keys it had; old cache files stay good). `seat_audio` asks for a plan for every take that sounds, once (two pieces of one take share it), and a piece plays the made file from where the piece starts in the clip (`made_piece`). So the pieces of stretched takes line up exactly as the takes whole would, and moving a seam makes nothing again: only choosing a take that did not sound yet starts a render. Until a render is ready that piece plays its file as it is, as any clip does.
- A change of the comp is other clips for the thread: a new generation, as any clip edit while the song plays (the engine fades the old audio out over 10 ms).
- `Clip::sounds(take)`: whether a take is heard somewhere (the shown one, or one of a piece); `audio_work_view` uses it to drop 「音を作っています」 of takes that no longer sound.

**Screen** (`ui/src/components/comp/`):

- `comp.ts` (pure): `piecesOf`, `chooseTake`, `rowTakes` (the newest `COMP_ROWS_MAX`, newest first), the band's shape (`BandDims`, `takeBars`, `bandRects`, `wasFrames`), the drag (`dragTick`, `draggedPieces`).
- `compState.svelte.ts`: `compBand.clip`, the one clip whose band is open (screen state only). `openCompOf` gives the band only while the clip is still a vocal clip of two takes or more, so a band closes by itself when its clip goes.
- `CompBand.svelte` (time side) and `CompHead.svelte` (head side) sit right under the track's row, above its automation lanes. `arrangeRows` (`automation/autoGeom.ts`) got a fourth argument for the band's height; `Arrange.svelte` and `TrackHeads.svelte` pass the same one, so both sides agree.
- The bars and the waves are one `rects` slot (with `waves`) of the `arrangeLanes` region, as an automation lane: **no new region, no Canvas 2D**. Nothing changes at 60 Hz: the picture is made again only when the pieces, the view or the peaks change, and while a drag moves.
- The clip itself in the arrange now shows what it plays: `waveParts` (`arrange/wave.ts`) gives one wave part per piece, each of its own take (`takeTiming`), drawn over the piece's ticks with the existing `clipWaves(range)`.
- The fake: `ui/src/bridge/fakeComp.ts` (`setComp`, `clearComp`, `stepTake` and `deleteTake` on a clip with listed takes, Rust's `comp_is_valid` and `comp_window` copied); `trimClip` cuts the comp; A9 passes a comp clip by.

**Traps.**

- `SetComp` is easy to misread as "replace the pieces inside `span`". `span` is only the clip's edges as a guard; the list is whole.
- `FeedClip` got two fields; it is `Copy + Eq` and compared whole in `only_audio_differs`, so a change of a seam is a change of the feed.
- The menu's hint text holds the word 「いいとこ取り」 too: a test or a tool that looks for the row by its text must skip disabled rows (`tools/design/shoot_ui.py` does).
- `ReadAhead::inline` needs one `pump()` after `set_wants` before a want is taken (the export does that in `ExportEngine::new`).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md`, "Rules added in round 12" (source `user:2026-11-23`); the old line at "An earlier take is chosen again with the switch tab…" loses its last sentence (「いいとこ取り」 is made now).

- 「いいとこ取り」 is for a vocal clip with two takes or more. Keys clips do not have it.
- The clip's right-click menu has the row 「いいとこ取り」 with the take rows (after 「テイクを消す」), for audio clips only. It opens the band under the clip's track, above the track's automation lanes; pressed again for the same clip it closes the band (the row shows its mark while the band is open). One band at a time. For a clip of one take the row is disabled, and says why on hover.
- The band: a heading row (`size.row`), then a row (`size.lane`) for each take, the newest first, 8 rows at most. Head side: 「▾ いいとこ取り」 (pressing it closes the band) and 「テイク 3」… beside the rows. Time side: 「（クリップの名前） ・ テイク （表示中）」 at the left of the heading and the button 「もとに戻す」 at its right; in each row a bar as wide as the clip (`color.gray.5`) with the take's wave on it; where the take sounds the bar is `color.accent` (the wave on it `color.gray.0`, elsewhere `color.gray.8`).
- Holding a row and moving sideways chooses that take for the range. While dragging only the screen changes, and the parts chosen before keep a `color.gray.7` hairline frame; releasing sends one intent; Esc cancels. The ends go to the grid lines in view (beats when they are drawn, else bars); with Shift they do not. The range stops at the clip's ends. A press without a move, and a range the take already has, do nothing.
- Neighbours of one take become one piece; a piece covered whole goes.
- 「もとに戻す」 takes the comp away: the clip plays its shown take whole. Disabled when there is no comp.
- With a comp the clip in the arrange shows the wave of what it plays, piece by piece.
- The seams are cross-faded over 10 ms (5 ms on each side; less beside a piece shorter than that).
- With a comp: 「次のテイク／前のテイク」 (T, Shift T) take the comp away and show that take whole, in one step; 「テイクを消す」, 「逆にする」 and 「ノーマライズ」 are disabled and say why on hover; 「音程を寄せる」 is disabled (a strength set before stays and is heard). Cutting the clip cuts the pieces; changing its length keeps the pieces inside it; moving, duplicating and repeating keep them; duplicating the track copies them. A recording over the clip keeps what it played, with the new takes there to be chosen.
- While counting in or recording the drag and 「もとに戻す」 do nothing.
- Step names: 「いいとこ取り」 (`SetComp`), 「いいとこ取りを外す」 (`ClearComp`); "what" is the clip's name. Both are already in the list. No new keys (spec 4 unchanged).

## Readings

Decisions where the section said nothing, and the eight the lead asked for.

1. **A9 on a clip with a comp: refused.** 「逆にする」 and 「ノーマライズ」 pass such a clip by in Rust (as they pass a keys clip by), and the two rows are disabled with the reason when the clicked clip has a comp. The protocol comment of `ReverseClips` already said so for reverse; normalize follows it, because "the loudest sample of the shown take" is not what the clip plays.
2. **M7: `tune_usable` stays false with a comp** (the lead's instruction; the section says nothing). A tune set before the comp was made stays in the song and is heard: every take that sounds is rendered with it. It can be taken off (strength 0) but not changed until the comp is gone. Since the audio is now made per take, turning the rows on for comp clips is one line (`Play::tune_usable`), if the owner wants it.
3. **Stretch with a comp: made right, not refused.** See Architecture: one made file per take that sounds, for the whole clip; tested (the same stretched audio over a seam is the stretched take whole within 1e-6, and export equals play sample for sample).
4. **A2: the cut cuts the pieces.** `split_clips` gives each half its pieces; tested through `InsertSpace`, `DeleteSection` and `DuplicateSection`.
5. **A1, duplicate, move, resize, trim.** Pieces are ticks from the clip's start, so moving and copying keep them; trimming keeps what sounds at each place of the song and drops what is outside (rule above).
6. **A3 copies the comp**; the copy's clips share the takes.
7. **Takes going and coming.** Deleting a take is refused while there is a comp (spec). A file taken out of the song takes the comp with it only when a piece names one of its takes. A recording over the clip keeps the comp valid and heard (rule above): after recording into a comped clip the person still hears the comp and chooses the new take in the band. The section does not say this; the other choice (the comp goes and the new take sounds whole) loses work without a word.
8. **A7**: keys only, not touched.

Others:

- **No smallest piece.** The lead asked for a rule for "pieces shorter than the minimum". There is none in the model (one tick is allowed): the seams shrink beside a short piece, so nothing can click or overlap wrongly. The screen cannot make a piece shorter than a grid step unless Shift is held.
- **`SetComp` with what the clip plays already is no step** (also `[{0, shown take}]` on a clip without a comp).
- **The band's place.** Spec 3 says only "its place stays as the band under the track"; it is right under the track's row and above the automation lanes, in the arrange. Rows are `size.lane` high (the mock's bars are thinner; a wave needs the height), the heading `size.row`.
- **The mock's heading is one row across** (「▾ いいとこ取り」「歌 1 ・ テイク 3」 … 「もとに戻す」). The arrange has a head column and a time side, so 「▾ いいとこ取り」 is in the head column and the other two on the time side.
- **「歌 1 ・ テイク 3」** is read as "the clip's name ・ the shown take's number".
- **The row order**: the spec says "新しい方から 8 行まで"; the mock shows take 1 on top. The text wins: the newest take is the top row.
- **The row 「いいとこ取り」 is with the take rows**, not at the bottom of the menu, so the tests that pin 「逆にする」「ノーマライズ」 as the last rows stay as they are.
- **Opening the band scrolls it into view** (the track's row and the band), as selecting a track does.
- **No audition** (the section has none): to listen, play the song.
- **Linear cross-fade** (the section says only "10 ms のクロスフェード"), for the reason in Architecture.
- **The waves in the band are drawn at the clip's volume 1 and without its fades**: the rows show the takes, not the clip's shape.
- Wording from the spec (`user:2026-11-23`): `menu.comp` 「いいとこ取り」, `comp.title` 「いいとこ取り」, `comp.clear` 「もとに戻す」, `comp.clipTake` 「{clip} ・ テイク {take}」 and `comp.take` 「テイク {take}」 (the mock in the spec).
- Wording made here (`builder:2026-11-24`, shown in a disabled menu row on hover): `comp.needsTakes` 「テイクが 2 つ以上あるクリップで使えます」, `comp.notWhileComp` 「いいとこ取りがあるあいだは使えません」.
- New pictures: scene `compTakes`, click shots `compBand` (1280×800 at 100 and 150, 853×448) and `compDrag` in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py`; no mock boards for them.

## Measurements

None (the PC was not quiet: load average about 20 while this was built). From the tests, at 48 kHz:

- Two takes of the same sine across a seam, exported: at most 1e-6 from the take whole (the same for stretched takes).
- A seam between a 220 Hz sine and a 330 Hz cosine at their far-apart point: the largest step from one sample to the next stays under 1.8 times the 220 Hz sine's own largest step (a cut would be about ten times it).

To measure after the merge, on a quiet PC:

- `python3 -I tools/measure/audio_app.py play` and `same` with a song whose vocal clips have comps (16 tracks): the read-ahead thread reads two files for 10 ms at each seam and one otherwise, so the cost should be the same as without comps; `stream_missing` must stay 0.
- `python3 -I tools/measure/audio_app.py arrange` and `python3 -I tools/measure/arrange_parts.py` with the band open (8 rows): frames while scrolling and zooming. The band adds up to 8 bars, the accent parts and one wave part per row and piece to the `arrangeLanes` sheet.
- `python3 -I tools/measure/export_app.py same` with a comped clip, and with a comped clip that follows another tempo.
- `tools/measure/webkit-damage.sh` is not needed (nothing new changes at 60 Hz), but a drag over a row repaints the band's part of the `arrangeLanes` canvas.

## Left for later

- **A take's wave in the band uses the shown take's sample rate** (`AudioTakeView` has no `sample_rate`). When a clip's takes are of files with different rates (a recording made after the device's rate changed), the wave of such a take is drawn at the wrong scale in the band and in the clip. The sound is right. Missing shape: `AudioTakeView::sample_rate`.
- A change of the comp while the song plays is heard after the read-ahead thread starts anew (a short fade, as any clip edit). When the takes are stretched and the new pieces have the same edges as the old ones, the change goes the "other audio for the same clips" way (`only_audio_differs`) and is heard up to two seconds late; the same already holds for T on a stretched clip. Not changed here.
- Choosing a take that did not sound yet on a stretched clip starts a render of the whole clip for that take (「音を作っています」); until it is ready that piece plays unstretched, as any clip does.
- The history rows of the fake for the two steps are the made-up ones (the fake keeps no row names for them).
- `crates/meloet-ai/tests/link.rs` failed twice in five runs here, each time in another test (「another measurement is running」, an export that did not find its file), and passed with `--test-threads 2` and in the full run. It looks like timing under load; none of its songs has a vocal clip. Not looked into.
- Not checked in the real app: the band on the real screen, the sound of a seam between real takes (only sines were tested), a comp made while the song plays.
