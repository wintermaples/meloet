# E2 Arpeggio and note repeat (round 12)

## Architecture

**Where it runs.** On the audio thread, inside the engine (`crates/meloet-engine/src/engine/arp.rs`, a child module of `engine.rs`; rules at the top of the file). Nothing in it allocates or waits: the pattern is fixed arrays (16 keys, 48 notes for 3 octaves), the settings are a `Copy` value on each engine track (`Track::arp`), and one `ArpPlayer` belongs to the play target (only the play target gets keys, so only one arpeggio can run).

**The way of a key.** MIDI keys and typed keys (R2) reach `Engine::key_in` as before. `key_in` first asks `arp_key`:

- it always follows which keys are physically down (`ArpPlayer::down`, a `u128`), arpeggio on or off;
- with the play target's arpeggio on, NoteOn / NoteOff are taken: they change the pattern, they do not sound and they are **not** written down (no `stamp_played`). Controllers, the pedal and pitch bend go on as before (sound, `stamp`, `Recent`). CC 121 / 123 and `AllNotesOff` from a keyboard also let go of the arpeggio (and still go on to the instrument).

**The pattern** (`Pattern`): the keys by note, and `seq`, every note of one way up over the octaves: sorted by pitch, each pitch once, none past 127 (so a wide chord with 2 octaves still goes up by note). 「上へ」 walks `seq`, 「下へ」 walks it backwards, 「上下」 goes up and down without saying the top and the bottom twice, 「ばらばら」 picks by xorshift32 and never the same note twice in a row. One counter of steps runs on while keys come and go (`step % length`); it starts at 0 when the pattern starts from empty and when hold takes a new chord. A 17th key is left out. Velocity is the key's (the fixed velocity of the keyboard band is applied by the MIDI thread before the engine, so the arpeggio sees it).

**The clock** (`Engine::arp_at`, called for every stretch in `render` right after `step_at`; `arp_frames_left` cuts the stretch at the next note start or end, next to `frames_to_next_point`, so the instrument's `event` is called at the exact frame):

- `Clock::Grid(serial)` while the transport moves (count-in, play, recording). Steps are ticks counted from the head of each bar (`bar_of`, `grid_floor`, `grid_next`; the count-in has its own bars as its clicks have), so in 3/4 or 7/8 a step that does not fit is short and the grid starts again at the bar. The frame of a step is `Transport::frame_of(tick)`: the same answer as sequences and clicks get, over tempo slopes too. The first step is the first grid tick whose frame is still to come (`arp_grid_from`, found by frames as `seek_lane` does), so a key waits for the next step.
- `Clock::Free` while it stands still: frames since the engine was made (`block_frame + frame`), kept as f64. The first key sounds at once (frame 0 of the block that reads it, as any key); each step adds `step ticks / (tempo at the transport's place × 32) × sample rate`, read anew at every step.
- A note ends after 80 % of a step (`ARP_GATE_PERCENT`), or at the next step when that comes first (a short step before a bar line; the end is sent before the new start at the same frame).
- **When the clock changes** (`Transport::serial` changed: play starts, stop, count-in ends, the loop goes round, a jump, another tempo map, `SetLoop`; or moving ↔ standing still), the note that sounds ends at that frame and the next step is found anew. On the grid that is the next grid tick from the new place (at the loop's start: at once, when it is on the grid); standing still, it starts at once from that frame. Standing still, a change of `serial` (a jump, another map) is not looked at.
- A change of 「速さ」 or 「三連符」 finds the next grid step anew (`regrid`); the note that sounds keeps its end. Shape and octaves only rebuild `seq`.

**What is written down** (`Engine::arp_send`; the spec's rule: the notes that sounded, at the grid's own ticks, not at the heard position):

- Recording: `Recorded::Event { event, t_us, tick, lap }` with `tick` = the step's tick (a whole number), the end at `tick + 80 %` or where it was cut (`Transport::tick()` then), `lap` = the engine's lap, `t_us` = when that frame reaches the DAC (`dac_us`).
- Count-in: sounds, not written (a note that starts exactly at the start of the recording is the first one written).
- Playing: `Recorded::Recent { at: Some(RecentAt { tick, lap: run_lap }) }`; standing still: `Recent { at: None }` with the DAC time, which R1 turns into ticks by the tempo (0.1 tick or less from the steps). Both keep R1's ring headroom rule.
- The keys held are in no item. So a take played back goes straight to the instrument and is not arpeggiated twice.

**Because the engine says these notes before they are heard** (by the output's delay), three things were made to agree:

1. `meloet_model::record::Recorder::drop_after(tick, lap)` (new): notes that start after the place the stop was heard at, and passes after that pass, are dropped; a note that reaches past it ends there. The core calls it in `RecordReport::Stopped` before `finish` (`play.rs`). Keys a person played are never after the stop, so nothing changes for them (tested). Not called for `stream_gone` (the last heard place is old there).
2. `Recorder::control` puts a controller move into the pass it names while that pass is open (`open_from`, set by `close_laps`), also when notes of the next pass came first, and then corrects the heads of the later passes (`set_head`). Without it a pedal let go just before the loop's end would be written into the next take and that take would start with the pedal down for ever.
3. `Play::played` (R1, `play/recent.rs`): while the keys target's arpeggio is on, the keys the MIDI thread says are held (`HeldNow::keys`) are not taken over: what is said as played is the arpeggio's notes, each with its end. Without it "nothing held for two bars" would never be true after a recording.

R1's kept phrase keeps a note said just past the place its run ended (the phrase's end moves to its last event, as before).

**Hold** (「指を離しても続ける」): releases do not take keys out. A key pressed while no key is physically down replaces the chord (the step counter starts at 0, the clock goes on). Let go of by: arpeggio off, hold off (only keys still down stay; with none, silent at once), another play target, `ClearTrack`, `EngineCommand::AllNotesOff` (keyboard pulled out, MIDI stopped), CC 121 / 123, a new stream (`prepare`). Stop and play do **not** end it (the spec's list does not name them): the chord goes on, on the other clock.

**No stuck notes, path by path** (each is a test in `crates/meloet-engine/tests/arp.rs`): off, another target, no target, `AllNotesOff`, CC 123, CC 121, `prepare`; `ClearTrack`; an instrument swapped under a running chord (the old one fades out in 10 ms as before, the chord goes on, on the new one); turning the arpeggio **on** while keys sound by themselves (they get NoteOff at once and that is written down as a release: their real releases would be taken by the arpeggio); `settle` (no stream) resets the player, so nothing is said into the ring with a time of 0.

**Core.** `Command::SetArp` → `Play::set_arp` (`play/arp.rs`): refused while `busy()`, octaves brought into 1..=3, `edit::set_arp` (model: `NotKeys` for a vocal track, `Unchanged` for the same settings) → one step `StepKind::SetArp`. The engine is told with the mixer: `EngineOp::Arp { seat, arp }`, sent from `sync_mix` when it differs from what the seat was told (`MixTold::arps`; a cleared seat is "off" again), so undo, redo, opening a song, a copy of a track (A3) and an engine made anew all tell it. It is a seat op (`service.rs` `Audio::op`: reaches an engine without a stream through `settle`), is kept in the `MixBook` for an engine that was lost, and `ExportEngine::tell` ignores it.

**Export.** An export has no keyboards, so it is the same with the arpeggio on or off (`export/job/tests.rs` `an_arpeggio_on_the_tracks_changes_nothing_of_the_export`: the same samples).

**Screen.** `InputMeter.svelte` (button 「アルペジオ」 + ▾ for the keys target, in room that is left: `meterFit`'s `arp`), `ArpWindow.svelte` (the small window, the frame and rows of `MetronomeWindow.svelte`; every press sends one `setArp` with the whole `Arp`), `mixer/Strip.svelte` (「アルペジオ」 at the right end of the strip's heading while on; a press turns it off), fake `reduceArp` in `bridge/fakeWave3.ts`, scenes `arpOn`, `arpSettings`, and the clicked `arpWindow` (`tools/design/shoot_ui.py`). The key lights (input meter, piano-roll key column) show the keys **held** (they come from the MIDI thread's `play_key`), not the notes made.

**Traps.**

- A grid tick can lie a part of a frame before the position of the frame it sounds on: "is it still to come" must be asked by frames, not by ticks (`arp_grid_from`), or a key read at a block that starts on a step misses that step.
- `Transport::frame_of` gives 0 for every tick before the anchor: at `frames == 0` compare ticks.
- Notes come into the core's recorder ahead of controllers of the same moment (see the three points above).
- `LatencyRecord` is still written for every MIDI NoteOn in `read_midi`; with the arpeggio on while playing, the note sounds later than that record says (it waits for the grid). The settings' measured latency is only right with the arpeggio off.
- A track whose plugins delay the sound: sequences are read ahead to make up for it, the arpeggio is not (as live keys are not), so live it sounds later than the take will by that delay.

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md` ("Rules added in round 12"):

- 「アルペジオ」 is a setting of each keys track (not of vocal tracks), part of the song. Every change is one step 「アルペジオを変える」; refused while counting in or recording.
- Input meter: the button 「アルペジオ」 turns it on / off for the keys target track (accent while on), 「▾」 opens the small window. Disabled with no keys target; while counting in or recording the button cannot be pressed (the window still opens). Both take only room that is left in the meter, before 「キーで弾く」.
- The window, in this order: 「アルペジオ」 オン／オフ; 「形」 上へ／下へ／上下／ばらばら; 「速さ」 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 and 「三連符」; 「オクターブ」 1, 2, 3; 「指を離しても続ける」 オン／オフ; the line 「和音を押さえるだけで、テンポに合ったフレーズになります」. Closes with Esc (in the order of the menus) and a click outside.
- Mixer strip: while on, 「アルペジオ」 shows at the right end of the strip's heading (above 「音源」); a press turns it off.
- The keys held (up to 16; MIDI keyboards and 「キーで弾く」) play one at a time in the order of the shape, each with its own velocity (after the fixed velocity), each 80 % of a step long. Octaves go up by note; a note is said once; none above the top of the keyboard. One key is note repeat.
- While the song moves (count-in too) the steps are on the grid counted from the head of each bar, from the next step on. While it stands still, the first key sounds at once and the steps go on at the tempo of the play line.
- Only the keyboards' notes go through it: not the notes of the song, auditions, the chord sheet, the shelf's trial. Controllers, pedal and pitch bend pass and are recorded as they are.
- A recording and 「さっきのを残す」 hold the notes that sounded, at the grid's ticks, not the keys. What was not yet heard when the person stopped is not in the take.
- Hold ends by: arpeggio off, hold off, another keys target, CC 123 / 121, the keyboard going away, or a new key after every key was let go (the new chord). Stop and play do not end it.
- Turning it on while keys are down lets go of them; press again to start. The key lights show the keys held.
- Not in an export.

`docs/spec/4-keys.md`: no key. Step name `setArp` 「アルペジオを変える」 was already in all 6 places.

## Readings

- **Stop does not end a held chord.** The spec's list of what ends 「続ける」 does not name stop, so the chord goes on (standing still, from the stop on, at the tempo). **Question for the owner: should stop also end it?** (One line in `Engine::stop` would do it.)
- **Count-in**: the arpeggio sounds through the count-in on the grid that leads to the start; nothing of it is recorded before the start.
- **Punch-in while playing** (`RecordHere`): the recording starts at the heard place, a little behind the engine; the note that sounds then is not in the take (its start was said before the recording).
- **Order**: notes sorted by pitch over all octaves, each pitch once (the spec says only "the order of the shape").
- **「上下」** does not say the top and bottom twice. **「ばらばら」** never says one note twice in a row; its seed is fixed when the engine is made and runs on (the spec: it is recorded as notes, so 22-25 does not apply).
- **Keys that join or leave** keep the count of steps; the change is heard from the next step.
- **A short step before a bar line** (triplets in 3/4, quarters in 7/8): the note ends at the bar line.
- **Rate changed while running**: taken at the next step of the new grid.
- **A 17th key** is left out, also after one of the 16 is let go.
- **Hold with a new chord**: a key pressed after every key was let go replaces the chord at the next step; keys pressed while one is still down join.
- **Octaves out of range** in an intent are brought into 1..=3 (not refused).
- **Screen, decided by the screen helper (please confirm):**
  - **The mixer strip has no free seat above 「音源」** (spec 3 line 41 reserves the place, nothing is laid out for it). 「アルペジオ」 is at the right end of the heading row, only while on; nothing below moves (checked by pixels), but the track's name is cut to about one character on a 108 px strip. The other way: an empty row on every strip, which moves everything below down by about 32 px. **Question for the lead / owner: which?**
  - **On the strip it can only be turned off** (nothing shows while off; the spec says 入切).
  - **In the input meter the arpeggio takes left-over room before 「キーで弾く」** (it has no key and no menu row; 「キーで弾く」 has Mod K and the menu). So at 1280 × 800 at 100 % in the slim form 「アルペジオ ▾」 shows where 「キーで弾く」 showed after R2. At 1280 × 800 at 150 % neither shows: **the arpeggio cannot be turned on there** (the spec gives no menu row and no key). **Question for the owner.** At 1920 × 1080 at 100 % everything shows.
  - While counting in or recording the button is shown disabled (as the track heads do), not sent and refused.
- **Wording** (`ui/src/text/ja.ts`): from the spec, `user:2026-11-23`: `input.arp` 「アルペジオ」, `arp.on` / `arp.off` 「オン」「オフ」, `arp.shape` 「形」, `arp.up` 「上へ」, `arp.down` 「下へ」, `arp.upDown` 「上下」, `arp.random` 「ばらばら」, `arp.rate` 「速さ」, `arp.quarter` … `arp.thirtySecond` 「1/4」…「1/32」, `arp.triplet` 「三連符」, `arp.octaves` 「オクターブ」, `arp.hold` 「指を離しても続ける」, `arp.hint` 「和音を押さえるだけで、テンポに合ったフレーズになります」 (from the spec's picture). ## Left for later

- Not checked in the real app by hand or by ear: the button, the window, the sound, recording with the arpeggio, typed keys through it. `scripts/check-all.sh` was not run.
- `stream_gone` (the output is lost while recording) does not call `drop_after`: a note or two said ahead can make that take a little longer.
- Under ring headroom (`RECORDED_HEADROOM`) a `Recent` of the arpeggio can be dropped like any other (R1's note).

- No look-ahead for tracks whose plugins delay the sound (see Traps).
- Flaky under load, not touched: `sounds::fetch_tests::a_stopped_fetch_keeps_its_part_and_goes_on_later` and `readahead::tests::other_audio_for_the_same_clips_comes_in_over_ten_ms_where_the_ring_ends` (meloet-core) each failed once in a full run and passed alone.
