# E3 The knobs of a built-in sound: 「明るさ」「立ち上がり」「のび」「ゆれ」

## Architecture

- **Where each part lives.**
  - Model (`meloet-model/src/knobs.rs`): `knob_usable(instrument, knob)` (only a sound that comes with meloet; of a drum kit only 「明るさ」 and 「のび」), and `set_sound_knob(doc, track, knob, value)`, the step 「音のつまみを変える」 (`StepKind::SetSoundKnob`). `Track::has_target` takes `AutoTarget::Instrument { param }` of a built-in sound for a knob it uses (`param` = the knob's place in `SoundKnob::ALL`), so `check_lanes` and the song file take knob lanes. `with_instrument` (replacing the sound, also 「外す」 of a stopped plugin) puts the knobs back to where they start; it already drops the lanes of the old instrument.
  - Engine (`meloet-engine/src/knobs.rs`, `instrument.rs`, `engine.rs`): `Instrument::set_knob(knob, place)` (default: nothing, so plugins and SF2 pass it over). `VoiceKnobs` is what an instrument keeps of 「立ち上がり」「のび」「ゆれ」, `Glide` a straight 20 ms glide that lands on its target exactly, `vibrato_ratio` the vibrato of a voice from its age. They are in the engine crate because the sine (`PlaceholderSynth`) is there.
  - Sound (`meloet-sound/src/knobs.rs`): `Knobbed<I>` goes around every sound that comes with meloet (made in `meloet-core/src/instruments.rs`; not around SF2 presets, plugins or the silent stand-in while loading). It holds 「明るさ」, a filter on the instrument's output with the track's own state (the samples are shared by tracks in an `Arc`, so nothing is kept with them), and hands the other three to the instrument's `set_knob`. Lanes come in as `set_param_at(frame, knob, place)`: 「明るさ」 at the 32-frame piece that holds the frame, the others at once.
  - Instruments: `Sampler` (`sampler/player.rs`), `PolySynth` (`synth/poly.rs`), `ElectroKit` (`synth/kit.rs`, only 「のび」) and `PlaceholderSynth` (the sine). Each voice keeps its own attack step and release coefficient, set when it starts and when it lets go, and its age in frames.
  - Core: `Preview::Knob` and `Play::set_sound_knob` (`play/mixer.rs`) go through the same `mix()` as faders and effect parameters (preview while dragging, one step on release, rest or write of a lane, `CancelMixPreview`). `MixTold` keeps the knobs told for each seat (`play/mixsync.rs`); a change goes out as `EngineOp::Knobs { seat, knobs }`. The audio side keeps it in `MixBook` for an engine made anew; the export takes it with the other mixer ops (`export/render.rs`).
- **The hand and the lanes, in the engine.** `EngineCommand::SetKnobs { track, knobs }` sets the hand's places for the seat; the engine keeps them with the seat (like `Strip::hand` for the fader), whatever instrument comes. The seat's own instrument (the one set aside while another is tried out) takes each knob that no un-held lane plays. A knob whose lane stops playing (`SetAutomation` without it) or is held (`HoldLane`) gets the hand's value again. Whenever another instrument comes on the seat (swap, move, end of a trial) it takes the hand's values, and the instrument lanes send their values again (`AutoSet::resend_instrument`, only the instrument's lanes: `forget()` would also restart the fader's glide). `ClearTrack` puts the knobs back. While an instrument is tried out on the seat, the seat's instrument lanes are not sent to it (they are the own instrument's).
- **At the values they start at nothing changes.** Every place a knob could change a number has a branch that takes the old expression at the default (attack and release at 50 %, vibrato resting at 0, 「明るさ」 resting at 50 % with no lane points: the filter is not run at all). Checked once against the code before E3: a fixed scenario (notes, chords, pedal, bend, wheel, song notes, voice stealing, odd block lengths) through all 31 built-in sounds (5 computed, 26 of the dev sound pack) gave the same hash of every output bit before and after, with the knobs set to their defaults by hand. Kept as tests: `at_the_values_they_start_at_the_knobs_change_nothing` (the wrapper and default knobs against the bare instrument, bit for bit) and the existing sound tests unchanged.
- **The same wherever play starts.** The vibrato's phase is the voice's age (5.5 Hz from 0 when the voice starts); attack and release are read at the note's start and let-go from the target (not from a glide). With knobs set by hand, playing from the head twice and from bar 9 give the same samples (`turned_knobs_sound_the_same_from_the_head_twice_and_from_the_middle`), and so do playing and the export (`turned_knobs_are_exported_as_they_play`, with a lane on 「明るさ」). A value set before the first block after `prepare` or `reset` is taken at once instead of gliding, so an instrument made anew (a sound that finished loading, an engine made anew) sounds at its knobs from its first frame.
- **「明るさ」** below 50 %: a 12 dB/oct state variable low-pass (Q 0.707) whose cutoff falls exponentially from 20 kHz (at most 0.45 × the rate) to 400 Hz at 0 %; between 45 % and 50 % it is mixed in with the dry sound (`x + w·(lp − x)`, `w = 10 × distance`), so the sound comes to the dry sound exactly. Above 50 %: `x + (A − 1)·hp(x)` with a 12 dB/oct high-pass at 4 kHz, `A` up to +9 dB (about +4 dB at 4 kHz, the full +9 dB an octave above). The coefficients are worked out every 32 frames from the glided value, the weights move per frame. Filter states are zeroed when not used, flushed below 1e-18 every block, and cleared if not finite.
- **Traps met.**
  - A plugin that is not on this PC sounds as the sine, which now has knobs: its instrument lanes would have moved the sine's knobs. `send_automation` leaves the instrument lanes of such a track out (`play/autosync.rs`).
  - A test that plays twice failed in the last bits when the knobs made the song louder: the master limiter's gain never comes back to exactly 1 after it has held the sound down (see Left for later). The export test leaves the limiter out.
  - Lanes moving a knob glide 20 ms from where the knob was when play starts somewhere else, as built-in effects do. In use the play line is set while stopped, the lane sends the line's value then, and the knob is there before play starts; the export waits 250 ms before playing (`REST_S`).

## Spec rules

- 「明るさ」「立ち上がり」「のび」「ゆれ」 (round 12 E3; user:2026-11-23): pressing the sound of a keys track whose sound comes with meloet shows, in the params panel, four rows of the form of a built-in effect's parameter (spec 2 "Parameter row"), each 0–100 %, starting at 50, 50, 50, 0, under the name and 「最初から入っている」; 「音源を入れかえる…」 stays under them. The status line no longer says 「（音）には、動かせるパラメータがありません」 for these sounds; an SF2 preset still shows the 19-A panel.
- Drum kits (studio, electro, brush): 「明るさ」 and 「のび」 only; 「立ち上がり」 and 「ゆれ」 are shown disabled (gray, cannot be chosen, moved or given a lane).
- Moving: as the mixer's knobs. A drag changes only the sound (once a frame at most), releasing is one step 「音のつまみを変える」 (history row 「1 ピアノ ・ 明るさ 50 → 25 %」), Esc goes back (`CancelMixPreview`). A double click on the number opens the typing box; Enter sets the value as one step, put into 0–100. ← → move the chosen row's value, ↑ ↓ choose among the usable rows, Enter opens the typing box (spec 4, as for effect parameters). Works while counting in and recording, as the mixer does.
- The mark on the right adds the knob's lane (`AutoTarget::Instrument { param }`, the band heading is the knob's name). Hand and lane follow the table of architecture 21-11.
- Choosing a row: the status line says 「1 ピアノ ・ 「明るさ」をえらんでいます」 (with 「ループ 5–9 ・ 」 before it while the loop is on).
- Replacing the sound puts the four knobs back to where they start, in the same step 「音源を入れかえる」, and takes the knobs' lanes away with it.
- Duplicating a track copies its knobs (the copy sounds the same).

## Readings

- **「のび」 of a drum kit.** The spec says 「のび」 is used by drum kits but defines it as "the time after the key is let go", and drum hits are one-shots (nothing happens when the key goes up). Read as "how long a hit rings":
  - Electro kit: the loudness decay times of each hit are multiplied by `4^(2v−1)` at the hit (1/4 to 4 times; the pitch falls are not changed).
  - Sampled kits (studio, brush; any one-shot region): below 50 % the hit falls faster from its start by an added decay of 60 dB over `length × f / (1 − f)` (`f = 4^(2v−1)`, `length` its sample's length as played), which is no change at 50 %. **At 50 % and above nothing changes**: a recording cannot ring longer than it was recorded (stretching its decay would raise the noise at its end). Fable suggested this over cutting the hit at a point. **Question for the owner**: is "only shorter" right for the sampled kits?
- 「立ち上がり」 above 50 % adds time in a straight line (+400 ms at 100 %); at 0 % the attack is one frame. A sound whose own attack is 0 (most sampled sounds) has no change below 50 %, as the spec's formula says.
- 「のび」 changes the release of voices that are let go (key up, pedal up, all notes off). The piano's release sounds and pedal sounds are not changed.
- 「ゆれ」: a sine that starts at the note's pitch and goes up first; depth `v × 50` cents, one depth for the whole instrument gliding over 20 ms. A sample region follows it as it follows the bend (a region with no pitch key tracking does not waver).
- A typed knob value is read by the screen ("35", "35 %", "35%", "−5"); a text with no number sends nothing and the status line says nothing (there is no `TypeParam` for knobs, and no shape for a status line saying it was not read).
- 「音源を入れかえる…」 is kept under the four rows (the spec puts the rows where the two texts were and says nothing of the button).
- Wording made here, in the style of the neighbours (source `builder:2026-11-24`): `status.soundKnobSelected` 「{track} ・ 「{knob}」をえらんでいます」 (the form the protocol comment gives), `history.soundKnob` 「{track} ・ {knob} {from} → {to} %」 (as `history.send`).
- The fake keeps the knobs of the selected sound only (as it keeps the values of the selected insert only); a sound chosen again starts at the defaults in the fake.
- The scene `paramsSound` (19-A, mock F7-Answers92) is left as it was: it shows a grand piano with no knobs, which Rust no longer sends; it still pictures the panel an SF2 preset gets. New scenes: `paramsSoundKnobs`, `paramsSoundDrums`.

## Left for later

- **The master limiter's gain does not come back to exactly 1** after it has held the sound down (`gain += step × (1 − gain)` stops one step under 1 in f32; `effects/limiter.rs`). Two plays of a song that drove the limiter differ in the last bits, so does an export made after such a play. Not changed here.
- `meloet-ai --test link` `a_note_is_rendered_into_the_private_folder` failed once while all test binaries ran at once, and passed three times alone (a wait under load).
- Knob lanes glide 20 ms at a jump of the play line while playing (as built-in effect lanes do). A note starting exactly where a lane's 「立ち上がり」 or 「のび」 changes takes the value the lane had at the end of the piece before.
- The flat line of a resting knob lane (`handPosition` in `ui/src/components/automation/laneValue.ts`) is not known to the screen, as for plugins.
- The sine's vibrato moves in a straight line across each render call, as its bend does, so it depends on how the engine cuts a block (same for playing and export).
- Pre-existing, made right because E3 needed it: while an instrument is tried out on a seat, the seat's instrument lanes (plugins' too) are no longer sent to the instrument being tried.
