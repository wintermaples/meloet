# E7 音に合わせて動く絵 (round 12)

## Architecture

- **The bands** (`crates/meloet-core/src/analysis/spectrum.rs`, `Spectrum`; on the analysis thread `meloet-analysis` that M2 made, never on the audio thread):
  - The last 2048 frames are kept in a ring; every 1024 frames (half overlapped) they go through a periodic Hann window into one 2048-point FFT. The FFT is the one meloet already had (`crates/meloet-core/src/export/analysis/fft.rs`, radix-2, f64, in place; only `Fft::new` allocates), now `pub(crate)`. No new crate.
  - One FFT for both sides: left in the real part, right in the imaginary part. For bin k, |X_L|² + |X_R|² = (|Z_k|² + |Z_{N−k}|²) / 2. A band's power is the mean of the two sides, scaled by 32 / (3N²), so a full-scale sine on both sides whose main lobe is in one band reads 0 dB (on one side only: −3 dB).
  - 24 bands from 40 Hz to 16 kHz on a log scale (band i starts at 40 · 400^(i/24) Hz). A band with two bins or more adds up their power. A band narrower than two bins (the lowest 5 bands at 48 kHz, the lowest 7 at 96 kHz) is read by a DFT at its geometric middle (Goertzel, on the windowed frames before the FFT overwrites them), so a sine still lands in its own band. The plan of the bands is made again only when the rate changes.
  - −72..0 dB as 0..255. A bar rises at once (never over 0 dB: a louder sound would otherwise hold it at 255 until it had fallen that far) and falls 30 dB a second, counted in frames that came (sound time).
  - A window with no sample over 1e-4 (−80 dBFS) is not worked: no band could read above 0 (a band holds at most 4·x² of it, −74 dB).
  - Everything is allocated in `Spectrum::new`; `push`, `restart`, `fall_for` and `bars` allocate nothing (`crates/meloet-core/tests/spectrum_alloc.rs` counts with the global allocator).
- **The thread** (`crates/meloet-core/src/analysis.rs`, `Side`): `spectrum_on` (off at the start), set by `Analysis::set_spectrum` (`Msg::Spectrum`). Only while it is on are the frames pushed into the bands; turning it off writes 0 into `Readings` at once. `afresh()` (new reader, new rate, frames the ring dropped) restarts the bands from silence. When `pump` sees that the ring dropped frames or the rate changed, what the ring still holds came before the gap (or at the rate before): it is read and let go of first, then `afresh()`, then what comes next is worked (pushing it first would join the two sides of the gap). Each wake while on writes the 24 bars into `Readings::spectrum`, which the telemetry thread already sends (byte 100..123 of telemetry 5, in `same_readings`).
  - **A stream that stopped**: after 20 wakes in a row with no frames (200 ms; longer than one block of the largest quantum the PC may force), the bars fall by the time (10 ms a wake) until they are 0, so the top band does not keep the last picture when the output goes away. The window is emptied then too, so a stream that comes back does not show the frames from before the stop again.
- **The setting** (`crates/meloet-core/src/state.rs`): `Command::SetSpectrum { on }` sets `Model::spectrum` and saves the settings (`Effect::SaveSettings`); the same value again does nothing. Not a step, not refused while busy. The service (`crates/meloet-core/src/service.rs`) tells the analysis thread the setting at the start (from the settings it read) and at every `Effect::SaveSettings` (as it already takes `wish_frames` from there).
- **The screen**:
  - A new slot kind `{ kind: "spectrum" }` (`ui/src/gl/slot.ts`), painted by `paintSpectrum` (`ui/src/gl/paint.ts`): the ground `color.meter.ground`, 24 standing bars sharing the box's width, the last device pixel of each band's share left as ground; colors from `SPECTRUM` in `ui/src/gl/dims.ts` (`color.track.1` to `.5`, 5 bars each, the last 4 of track.5). The engine (`ui/src/gl/engine.ts`) keeps the last 24 bands of the telemetry and marks the slots dirty only when a band changed.
  - **No new region**: the box `.spectrum` is inside `.master` of `Transport.svelte`, which is already a `cover` slot of the `masterMeter` region. The canvas covers the same rectangle as before (from the position numbers to the master's right edge, the bars take room from the spacer between them), so a frame with moving bars is the same one dirty rectangle as a frame with a moving master meter. The box has a fixed size (`calc(var(--part-meter-horizontal) * var(--bands))`, 96 / 120 / 144 px) and the height of the master's box; no text, no style and no size changes per frame.
  - **Fitting** (`transportWithSpectrum` in `ui/src/components/fit.ts`): everything else is decided as without the bars (`transportWithKeep`), and the bars come only when the room left is at least their width plus space.1. `measure()` in `Transport.svelte` takes their width out of the master's box while they are shown.
  - The menu row is in `ui/src/components/menu/menus.ts`; the fake answers `setSpectrum` (`reduceSpectrum` in `ui/src/bridge/fakeWave3.ts`).
- **Damage, reasoned from chapter 7** (not measured, see Measurements): rule (a) holds (the box is under the `masterMeter` canvas, inside a `cover` box); rule (c) holds (no new rectangle; the canvas is not larger); rule (d) holds (no text under it changes); rule (e) holds (nothing moves or changes text per frame). While playing, the master meter already redraws that canvas every frame. After the sound ends, the bars fall for at most 2.4 s, during which the master meter is still falling (20 dB in 1.7 s), so no frame draws the canvas that did not draw it before; but the telemetry keeps coming at 60 Hz for those seconds (before, it stopped when the peaks were 0). So the default stays on.
- **Cost**: per 1024 frames (21 ms at 48 kHz), one 2048-point complex FFT in f64 and, for the 5 to 7 narrow bands, a Goertzel over 2048 frames each. Nothing while the setting is off, while silent, or with no stream. About 100 KB of memory for the thread's buffers.
- **Traps**:
  - A sound that starts after silence (and the start after `afresh`) is cut by the window and spreads into every band for one window (a click looks like a click). The tests that check bands use sines that start from silence and still pass; the far bands read lower by 30 steps or more.
  - At 96 kHz a bin is 47 Hz: the lowest bands are narrower than the FFT can tell apart. A sine at a band's middle still reads highest in its own band, but by only 1 or 2 steps of 255.
  - At 1280 × 800, 100 %, the room left in the top band is 103 px (the count-in keeps room for its counting form, 6 px wider). With space.2 between the bars and the master's column the bars needed 104 and did not show; with space.1 they need 100.
- Fable was asked once (a read-only review of the bands and the thread): it found that the frames before a gap were joined to those after it, that a stream coming back after a stop showed its old window again, and that a bar had no cap at 0 dB. All three are fixed and tested.
- **What M3 will find** (the seams `// round12 stub: M3` in `Side::afresh` and in `Side::pump`'s closure, next to the bands):
  - The bands are worked only while `spectrum_on`. M3's mixer box shows the same 24 values (in `color.meter.ok`), so M3 has to turn them on while the mixer is open too, even with 「音の動きを見せる」 off: for example the model decides "bands wanted" (the setting, or the mixer open) and the service sends that to `Analysis::set_spectrum` instead of `settings.spectrum`.
  - The slot `{ kind: "spectrum" }` paints the track colors; M3 can add a color to it (for example `{ kind: "spectrum", color?: ColorName }`) and put it in the `mixerMeters` region.
  - The frames M3 needs come through the same closure (`frames: &[[f32; 2]]`, at most two slices per wake); the rate is `Side::sample_rate`. M3 still needs "the play started" (see `docs/round12/M2.md`).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md` ("Rules added in round 12", E7):

- Left of 「マスター」 in the top band (inside the master's box, space.1 before 「マスター」), 24 bars of the master's sound from low to high: 40 Hz to 16 kHz on a log scale, −72 dB at the bottom and 0 dB at the top, after the master's inserts (not the test tone, the latency click or the metronome). From the left 5 bars each of `color.track.1` to `color.track.4` and the last 4 of `color.track.5`, on `color.meter.ground`, one device pixel of ground between bars. 24 × `part.meter.horizontal` wide (96 at 100 %), as tall as the master's box (its name and its meter).
- They move while sound comes out (playing, and keys while stopped): a bar rises at once and falls 30 dB a second. When the output stops, they fall to nothing.
- They are shown only in room the top band has left when everything else is as it would be without them: they never hide, fold or shrink anything else. At 1280 × 800 they show at 100 % and not at 150 %.
- 「表示 ▸ 音の動きを見せる」: the last row of 「表示」, a check, on at first; a meloet setting (not in the song), not a step, usable while recording. Off: no bars, and the sound is not analysed for them.
- Keys (`4-keys.md`): none. Step names: none.

## Readings

- **The FFT**: the request named `realfft`, but the spec says to use the FFT meloet already has (`export/analysis/fft.rs`) when it allocates only when made, which it does. So no new crate (no `node tools/licenses/inventory.mjs write` needed; `realfft` 3.5.1 is in the lock file only through `rubato`). Switching to `realfft::RealFftPlanner` with `process_with_scratch` would be a small change inside `Spectrum` if the lead wants it.
- **"When nothing reads, do no FFT work"** read as: no reader (no stream, or the export's engine, which writes no ring), the setting off, or a silent window. When the screen hides the bars for lack of room, the analysis still runs: core does not know the screen's width, and telling it would need a new shape. Question for the lead.
- **A band's level** is the power in the band (the mean of the two sides), so a full-scale sine reads 0 dB in its band and pink noise reads about level across the bands that hold two bins or more. The spec says only "−72..0 dB". Bands narrower than two bins are read at their middle (Goertzel), not by the nearest bin, so that a sine lands in its own band at 96 kHz too.
- **A sine in a narrow band** (read at the band's middle) reads −1.8 dB (249 of 255) at the middle and about −4 dB at the band's edge, against 0 dB in a wider band. Scaling those bands up to 0 dB would make noise read 1.8 dB higher there, where it already reads a little high (the analysis is wider than the band). Left as it is.
- **The fall in sound time**: the bars fall by the frames that came (so the fall is the same at every buffer size); with no frames for 200 ms they fall by the clock instead.
- **Frames the ring dropped** restart the bands from silence (the stub of M2 said so), and the frames the ring still holds then (from before the gap) are let go of; the bars blink to 0. It happens only when the analysis thread stalls for 341 ms.
- **The gap** between the bars and 「マスター」 is space.1 (the spec gives none; the mock's row gap is 8 = space.2). With space.2 the bars did not fit at 1280 × 800, 100 %, which the spec requires.
- **The gap between bars**: one device pixel of ground at the right of each band's 4 px share (the spec gives "24 × 4 px = 96 px"; the mock has 5 px bars 2 px apart, which would not make 96).
- **The height**: "マスターのメーターの箱" read as the master's whole box (its name, its dB and its two bars), not the meter bars alone (10 px).
- **The fitting rule**: the bars may show while 「さっきのを残す」 or the song name is hidden for lack of room, when the room left is enough for them (their width is about that of 「さっきのを残す」, so this is rare). Nothing else changes with them on or off.
- **The menu row** is the last of 「表示」, after 「テーマ ▸」, with no separator (the spec gives no place). E1 adds 「鳴っている音を光らせる」 to the same menu; the two rows will meet there in the merge.
- **The fake**: a scene whose master sounds sends a fixed spectrum shaped like a song under the master's peak (`fakeSpectrum` in `ui/src/bridge/fakeWave3.ts`); it does not move, so shots are the same every run. New scenes `spectrum` (the mock: playing at 9. 1. 000, master −6.2 dB) and `spectrumOff`, in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py` with no board.
- **Tests changed**: `ui/tests/theme.test.ts` said 「テーマ ▸」 is the last row of 「表示」; now it checks that it follows 「拡大率 ▸」. The fake and core tests that said `setSpectrum` changes nothing no longer list it.
- **Wording**: 「音の動きを見せる」 (`menu.spectrum`, `user:2026-11-23`) from the spec. Nothing made up.

## Measurements

None (other helpers were building; the PC was not quiet). Checked only by looking at screen shots of the fake scenes (`shoot_ui.py spectrum spectrumOff`, 6 themes): the bars show at 1280 × 800 at 100 % with 3 px to spare and are hidden at 150 % without breaking the band; they show at 1920 × 1080 at 100 % and 150 %. Measured in headless Chrome: the band's spacer is 109 px at 1280 × 800, 100 % without the bars and 9 px with them.

For the lead after the merge, on a quiet PC, with an 8-track song that plays:

- `tools/measure/webkit-damage.sh --song <song> --commands '{"afterMs":2600,"command":{"type":"playStop"}}'`, and again with `{"afterMs":1300,"command":{"type":"setSpectrum","on":false}}` first: the top band should be the same one rectangle in both, of the same size, and never "one rectangle around all".
- `tools/measure/webkit-layout.sh` with the same song and commands: no layout per frame from the top band.
- `tools/measure/latency.sh --state playing` (and `--state stopped`): key → screen and key → sound next to the numbers before.
- `tools/measure/app-watch.sh --song <song> --commands '{"afterMs":2600,"command":{"type":"playStop"}}' --seconds 30`: busyMs, and the CPU of the thread `meloet-analysis` (`ps -L -o tid,comm,pcpu -p <pid>`; expected well under 1 % in release, near 0 when stopped and silent).
- The bars fall to nothing after the output is pulled out (look at the top band).

## Left for later

- The room at 1280 × 800, 100 % is only 3 px: anything added to the top band (or a longer count-in text) hides the bars at that size.
- The analysis runs while the bars are hidden for lack of room (see Readings).
- At 96 kHz the lowest bands are barely told apart (a sine wins its band by 1 or 2 steps).
- At 96 kHz the middle of the lowest band is less than a bin above 0 Hz, so a DC offset in the master shows there (about 6 dB under the offset's level). Taking out the window's mean would also change how a sine that low reads; not done.
- Not checked in the real app (no measurement, no real sound): what the bars look like with real songs, and whether 30 dB a second feels right.
