# M2 「音が大きすぎます」 (round 12)

## Architecture

- **Effects say how far they hold the sound down** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/effect.rs`): `Effect::reduction_db()` (dB, 0 or more, at the end of the last `process`; default 0) and `Effect::is_limiter()` (default false). The limiter returns `−20·log10(gain)` of its `gain` at the end of the block; the compressor returns its `reduction_db`. Plugins return nothing (a limiter plugin is no "limiter" here).
  - **Why `is_limiter`**: the spec wants only the master's limiters that are not passed by, and the engine does not know effect kinds. A message from core that names the limiter slots would need to be kept right through `MoveEffect`, replace and `EngineHandle::rebuild`; the effect that answers for itself moves with its slot. Fable agreed (the least-bad way; `Option<f32>` in one method would also do, but the spec gives `f32`).
- **The engine** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/mixer.rs` `Strip::limiting_db`, `engine.rs` `render`): right after the master's inserts, the most of `reduction_db()` over the slots that hold a limiter and are not passed by (`!slot.bypassed`: a slot on its way to another place still counts; only `slot.effect`, not the one fading out). `EngineMeters` gets `master_reduction` (f32 bits with `fetch_max`, "the most since the last read", as the peaks; `take_master_reduction`), `master_limited` (an unbypassed limiter is there; `master_limited`) and `aside` (the test tone or the latency click sounded since the last read; `take_aside`). Atomics only: no allocation, no lock (watched by `crates/meloet-engine/tests/mixer.rs`, `crates/meloet-sound/tests/effects_rt.rs`, `mixer_rt.rs`).
- **Telemetry 5**: `master_reduction` = the dB × 10, rounded, at most 255. The other new fields come from the analysis thread's `Readings` (none and 0 until E7 and M3).
- **Finding it** (`crates/meloet-core/src/tooloud.rs`, `Watch`): fed by the telemetry thread (`service.rs` `telemetry_loop`, 60 Hz) with one `Reading` a tick: time, playing (the heard state is playing or recording; not stopped, not counting in), fresh (`EngineMeters::callbacks` moved since the tick before), aside, limiter, reduction, master peak.
  - A tick with no new block says nothing and is skipped; the next one stands for the whole time since the last one that counted (capped at 100 ms). Fable pointed out that otherwise a 1024 buffer (21 ms) would read 0 in about every fifth tick.
  - A tick with the test tone or the latency click, and the tick after it, are left out (`aside` stays up until read, so the tick after a tone that ended is caught too).
  - A: the time of the readings with 3 dB or more in the last 4 s is 2 s or more; the amount is the mean over that time. B: 3 readings or more over 0 dBFS (peak > 1.0) in the last 4 s; the amount is the largest overshoot.
  - Once a play: after a finding the watch is quiet until the transport stops (or counts in) and plays again. The finding goes to the core thread as `Input::TooLoud { by_db, limiter }`.
- **Core state** (`crates/meloet-core/src/state.rs`): `too_loud: Option<(song serial, StatusMessage)>`, `too_loud_found`, `was_moving`. `Input::TooLoud` makes the line once a play (`tooloud::notice`: the amount to a tenth, the button's amount rounded up from it into 1..=12). A play that starts (moving after an input where it was not) puts the line away and finds anew. The line is a **lasting line**, not a notice: in `status()` it comes after the notices, the audition and the sample lines, before 「新しい版」. Count-in and recording lines come before notices, so a line found while recording shows when the recording is over, with nothing more to build. It shows only for the song it was found in (`Play::serial`).
- **Lowering** (`meloet_model::mixer::lower_all_tracks`, `Gain::lowered`, `Gain::lowered_place`, `meloet_model::automation::lower_volume_points`): one `Edit` over one `Doc` (as `ai_set_effect_params` does), step `StepKind::LowerAll { count }` (`count`: tracks whose fader or lane changed). A track whose volume lane moves (`Lane::moves`: not resting, has points) gets every point lowered, with a new lane id (`with_points`), and keeps its fader; the others get the fader lowered. −∞ stays, nothing goes below −60 dB. The returns and the master stay. `Play::lower_all_tracks` (`play/mixer.rs`); `Command::LowerAllTracks` is refused while `busy()`; when taken it puts the line away (also when nothing changed). `DismissTooLoud` puts it away.
- **The analysis path** (the foundation for E7 and M3):
  - Engine (`crates/meloet-engine/src/analysis.rs`): an rtrb ring of `[f32; 2]` frames, `ANALYSIS_RING_FRAMES` = 16384 (341 ms at 48 kHz, 171 ms at 96 kHz), made with every engine in `Engine::assemble`. `Engine::render` writes the master **after its inserts** (its fader and pan come before them, 19-11) and **before the test tone, the latency click and the metronome**, every piece, also while stopped. It writes only while the reader is out of the handle and not dropped (one atomic load), so an export engine copies nothing. A piece that does not fit is dropped whole and counted (`AnalysisReader::dropped`); a frame that is not a number goes in as silence. The reader also has the stream's rate (`AnalysisReader::sample_rate`, set by `Engine::prepare`).
  - `EngineHandle::take_analysis()` gives the reader once, and once more after `rebuild()` (as `take_stream_wants`).
  - Core (`crates/meloet-core/src/analysis.rs`): `Analysis` owns the thread `meloet-analysis` (usual priority). `Audio::new` (`service.rs`) spawns it with the handle's reader; `Audio::open` hands it the new reader after a rebuild (next to `take_stream_wants`); dropping `Audio` (the core thread's end) stops and joins it. It wakes every `ANALYSIS_TICK` = 10 ms (`recv_timeout` on its channel) and takes what is in the ring (about 480 frames at 48 kHz). It writes into `Readings` (atomics: `spectrum`, `lufs_short`, `lufs_integrated`, `true_peak`, and `frames` taken so far), which the telemetry loop reads every tick (`Core::start` hands the `Arc` to the telemetry thread).
- **Seam for E7 and M3** (marked `// round12 stub: E7` and `M3` in `crates/meloet-core/src/analysis.rs`):
  - Work goes in `Side::pump`'s closure (frames in at most two slices per wake) and the analysis state lives in `Side`. `Side::afresh()` is called for a new reader (a rebuilt engine), a new rate and frames the ring dropped: start the FFT window and the loudness meters anew there.
  - Write results with `store(…, Relaxed)` into the `Readings` fields (in telemetry units: `spectrum` −72..0 dB as 0..255; LUFS and dBTP × 100 as i16, `TELEMETRY_NO_VALUE` for none). The telemetry thread sends only when a reading changes (`same_readings`), so a value that moves every tick sends at 60 Hz.
  - Allocate in `Side::new` / `afresh` only, not per wake (`export/analysis/fft.rs` `Fft::new` allocates, `forward` works in place).
  - **M3 needs "the play started"** for `ebur128::reset()`, and the thread does not know it yet. Not built (the section did not ask M2 for it). Ways: give `Side` the engine's `TransportClock` and tempo map as the telemetry thread reads them (`shared.clock`, `shared.tempo_map`), or a play counter in `EngineMeters` that the engine raises when the transport starts moving (exact to the block; the ring is about 10 ms behind it).
  - **M3's mono listen** is "after the master inserts, before the meter": decide whether the analysis hears the mono sound (put it before `self.analysis.write`) or the song as it is (after).
  - Cost now: on the audio thread one atomic load and a copy of 2 × frames floats per piece while the reader is taken; on the analysis thread nothing but emptying the ring every 10 ms. Memory: 128 KB per engine for the ring.
- **Traps**: rtrb 0.4.1 has no `push_entire_slice` for two separate channels; `write_chunk_uninit(n)` + `fill_from_iter` interleaves without allocation. `--allowedTools` of `claude -p` takes every word after it: put the question on stdin.
- Fable was asked once (the engine design); see Readings and the report.

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md` ("Rules added in round 12", M2):

- While the song plays (not while stopped or counting in, nor while the test tone or the latency click sounds), meloet looks at the last 4 seconds:
  - with a limiter on the master that is not passed by: when it held the sound down by 3 dB or more for 2 seconds or more of them, the status line says 「音が大きすぎます。リミッターがずっと 5.6 dB おさえています」 (the mean of what it held down in that time, to a tenth of a dB);
  - without one (or passed by): when the master went over 0 dBFS 3 times or more (a time is one reading, about 60 a second), 「音が大きすぎます。マスターが 2.1 dB 越えています」 (the largest overshoot).
  - Buttons 「全部のトラックを 6 dB 下げる」 (the amount rounded up, 1 to 12) and 「閉じる」.
- Once a play at most. It stays until 「閉じる」, 「下げる」, the next play, or another song; a notice comes over it and it comes back after. Found while recording, it shows when the recording is over.
- 「全部のトラックを N dB 下げる」: every track's fader N dB lower, the returns and the master not. A fader at −∞ stays; none goes below −60 dB. A track whose volume a lane moves (not resting) gets every point of the lane N dB lower instead (the lane keeps moving; a point stops at −60 dB, a point at −∞ stays). One step 「全部のトラックを下げる」. Not while counting in or recording.
- Keys (`4-keys.md`): none. Step names: none new (「全部のトラックを下げる」 `lowerAll` was already in all 6 places).

## Readings

- **The number in the sentence and on the button** (the spec's sentence and its example disagree): the sentence has the measured amount to a tenth (`by_db`, 「5.6 dB」), the button the whole dB it lowers by (`lower_db`, 「6 dB」), as the lead said. `lower_db` is rounded up from the amount as shown (5.04 shows 「5.0」 and lowers by 5).
- **A's amount**: the mean over the time the limiter held 3 dB or more, not over the whole 4 s. Reason: the sentence says it holds the sound down 「ずっと」 by that much, and the trigger needs 3 dB; a mean over the whole window could say 「1.5 dB」 for a song that was just found too loud, and lowering by it would leave the limiter at work. **This differs from the comment on `StatusMessage::TooLoud` in the protocol** ("the mean reduction … over the last 4 s"); to follow the comment instead, divide by the window in `Watch::held` (`crates/meloet-core/src/tooloud.rs`). Question for the lead.
- **B's 「3 回」**: three readings of the telemetry thread (about 60 a second) whose master peak is over 1.0. A long over counts many times (it is too loud then too); a single stray peak does not.
- **Half of the 4 s**: the time the readings stand for, not their count (readings with no new block in between are joined into the next one).
- **A lasting line, not a notice**: notices go at the next thing the person does, and Space (stop) is one; the buttons must stay pressable after the stop. So the line stays as 「新しい版」 does, until 「閉じる」, 「下げる」, the next play or another song (a stale line would lower another song's tracks). Not said in the spec.
- **「下げる」 when nothing can be lowered** (every fader at −∞): no step, the line goes all the same.
- **`LowerAllTracks` with 0, a negative number or not a number**: nothing. There is no upper bound (everything stops at −60 dB).
- **Lane points** (the spec says to use the value side of `move_points`): `move_points` moves places on the fader's scale by one amount and stops the whole group at 0. That is not N dB (the scale is not even in dB) and would make the lowest point silent. Each point is lowered by N dB on the scale instead. −60 dB is the place 0 of a lane, which is −∞; a point that reaches −60 dB stops at `LOWEST_HEARD_PLACE` (1e-5), which reads −60 dB.
- **`count`** of `StepKind::LowerAll`: the tracks whose fader or lane changed.
- **The limiter's reading** is its gain at the end of each block (the spec's `gain`), the most over the blocks since the last tick.
- **The master's reduction is not shown** on the screen (the spec asks for no display; the telemetry carries it).
- **Wording**: all from the spec, `user:2026-11-23`: `msg.tooLoudLimiter` 「音が大きすぎます。リミッターがずっと {db} dB おさえています」, `msg.tooLoudOver` 「音が大きすぎます。マスターが {db} dB 越えています」, `button.lowerAllTracks` 「全部のトラックを {db} dB 下げる」; 「閉じる」 is `button.close` (spec 6). Nothing made up.
- **Fake** (`ui/src/bridge/fake.ts`): `lowerAllTracks` lowers the faders (refused while counting in or recording; a track whose volume lane moves keeps its fader, its points are not held by the fake); `dismissTooLoud` and `lowerAllTracks` put the line away. Scenes `tooLoudLimiter` (the mock) and `tooLoudOver` (the master's limiter passed by), in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py` with no board.
