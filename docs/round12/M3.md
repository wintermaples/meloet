# M3 「仕上げのメーター」 (round 12)

Spec: `docs/history/round12-spec.ja.html` M3 (lines 630-647). Protocol 33 and telemetry 5 shapes were already in; no shape was added.

## Architecture

- **The loudness** (`crates/meloet-core/src/analysis/loudness.rs`, `Loudness`; on the analysis thread `meloet-analysis` of M2, never on the audio thread). `ebur128` 0.1.11 (already a dependency since F1), two meters, both with `Mode::HISTOGRAM`:
  - `now` (`Mode::S`): fed every frame heard, also keys played while stopped. Gives 「いまの大きさ」 (the last 3 s).
  - `play` (`Mode::I | Mode::TRUE_PEAK`): fed only while the song is heard playing (playing or recording, not counting in), `reset()` when it starts to play. Gives 「曲ぜんたい」 (gated, BS.1770-4) and 「トゥルーピーク」 (the louder side, 4× oversampled below 96 kHz). They hold their values after the stop.
  - **Why two meters, not the one of the spec** (`Mode::I | S | TRUE_PEAK | HISTOGRAM`): one meter fed always would put keys played while stopped (and the tail after the stop) into 「曲ぜんたい」 and the true peak; one meter fed only while playing would freeze 「いまの大きさ」 after the stop instead of going to 「−∞」. With `HISTOGRAM` neither grows: the gating blocks are 1000 counters (`crates/meloet-core/tests/loudness_alloc.rs`: 30 simulated minutes of play, fed in 10 ms pieces and read every second, make 0 allocations).
  - Everything is made in `Loudness::new` (and again only for another rate). `feed`, the readings, `start_play`, `stop_hearing` and `restart` allocate nothing.
  - **Flush-to-zero**: as in F1, ebur128 sets FTZ in MXCSR inside its own call and restores it. `Loudness::feed` is `#[inline(never)]`; a test checks that denormals are kept after a feed. It is the analysis thread, not the audio thread.
- **When the song plays** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/analysis.rs`): `AnalysisTap::write(left, right, playing)` takes the transport's state at the end of each piece (`Playing | Recording`), and counts the starts in two shared atomics; `AnalysisReader::playing()` gives `(playing, plays)`. `Side::pump` reads them before it reads the ring; a new count resets `play`, and `playing` decides whether `play` is fed. This is the way M2's note proposed (a counter raised by the engine), inside the analysis ring rather than `EngineMeters`, because the reader is all the analysis thread has. Placing is exact to the piece and about one wake (10 ms) late: up to ~10 ms before a play can go into it, the last ~10 ms of a play may be left out (Fable, see Readings).
- **The analysis thread** (`crates/meloet-core/src/analysis.rs`):
  - `Wants { spectrum, loudness }` replaces E7's `set_spectrum`: only what the screen shows is worked. `Model::analysis_wants()` (`state.rs`): the bands while 「音の動きを見せる」 is on **or the mixer is open** (its box shows them, E7's note), the loudness while the mixer is open. The service tells the thread at the start and at the end of every input (`handle_now`, where `wants_sound` and the others are told); `Analysis::want` sends only a change. The call at `Effect::SaveSettings` is gone.
  - Loudness off: no meters exist, nothing is fed, the readings say none at once. On: new meters (so a mixer opened in the middle of a play measures from that moment).
  - The readings (`Readings::lufs_short`, `lufs_integrated`, `true_peak`, in the telemetry's hundredths) are written ten times a second (every `rate / 10` frames fed; the short-term reading sums 3 s of frames), and at once when the meters reset or turn off. A short-term reading under −70 LUFS (BS.1770's absolute gate) and a true peak of 0 are none. The telemetry thread sends them when they change (they were already in `same_readings`).
  - `afresh()` (a new reader, a new rate, frames the ring dropped) restarts both meters. A stopped stream (`STALLED_WAKES`, as E7) forgets only `now` (「いまの大きさ」 goes to 「−∞」; the play's numbers stay).
- **「モノで聞く」** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/engine.rs`, `Mono`, `EngineCommand::SetMonoListen {on}`):
  - In `Engine::render` after the master's inserts (and the limiter's reading of M2) and **before** the analysis ring and the master's peak meter; the test tone, the latency click and the metronome join after it, so they are not mixed. `amount` moves linearly 0 → 1 over `RAMP_S` (20 ms), per frame; each side becomes `x + amount/2 · (other − x)`, so at 1 both are `(L + R) / 2`. No allocation, no state but two floats (no denormal tail: `amount` stops exactly on its target). A non-number on one side makes both non-numbers, which the ring and the output turn into silence as before.
  - Core: `Model::mono_listen` (not in the song, not in the settings, off at start). `Command::SetMonoListen` → `Effect::MonoListen(on)`, which the service sends to the playing engine only (`Audio::command`, only while a stream runs). `Input::AudioOpened` says it again (a rebuilt engine starts off). It is not an `EngineOp`, so the export (which is made from the song and `EngineOp`s) never hears it.
  - The status line: a notice when it is turned on, and 「モノで聞いています」 in place of the summary while it stays on (`Model::status`).
- **The screen** (`ui/src/components/mixer/FinishMeters.svelte`, `finish.ts`), after the master strip in `MixerStrips.svelte`, `2 × size.strip` wide:
  - Head row as a strip's (gray.10 top line, like the master): 「仕上げのメーター」 and the button 「モノで聞く」 (`part.toggle.size` high; accent border and text while on, as 「ループ」).
  - **Level with the master's meter**: the box measures the master strip's meter (`[data-strip="master"] [data-gl-slot="meter"]`) with a `ResizeObserver` and puts its meter area out of the flow at the same top and height. In it: 「いまの大きさ」 and its number, the short-term bar (a new slot kind `{ kind: "loudness" }`, `part.meter.horizontal` high), 「曲ぜんたい」, 「トゥルーピーク」, and the 24 bars (`{ kind: "spectrum", color: "color.meter.ok" }`, E7's slot with a color) filling the rest. Under it 「低い音 ← → 高い音」, at the bottom (where the strips have M / S / ●) 「公開の目安は −14 LUFS、−1 dBTP」 (from `EXPORT_MATCH_LUFS` and `EXPORT_MATCH_TRUE_PEAK_DB`).
  - The two bars are slots of the `mixerMeters` region (no new region, no new context; `ui/tests/gl.surface.test.ts` unchanged). The GL engine keeps the telemetry's last `lufsShort` and marks the `loudness` slots dirty only when it changes; `paintLoudness` draws on the meters' scale (`dbToLength`), meter.ok and meter.hot past `meter.hot-from`, on meter.ground.
  - The numbers: one decimal with 「−」, 「+」 above 0, 「−∞ LUFS」 / 「−∞ dBTP」 for none. `calmly` (finish.ts) runs the update at once and then at most every 250 ms, however many telemetry messages come; text is replaced only when it changed. The number boxes are `.live > .in` (text out of the flow, as the strips' fader values), inside the meter area, which is itself out of the flow.
- **Damage, reasoned from chapter 7** (not measured; see Measurements): (a)/(b) the two bars are under the same canvas as the strips' meters, and level with the master's meter, so the right tile of `mixerMeters` does not grow taller, only as wide as the box when the row is narrower than the window (with a wide song the tiles are as wide as the window either way). (c) no new rectangle in a frame that shows the right tile: the numbers and labels are inside it. In a frame without that tile (silence), a number change (≤ 4 a second) is one small rectangle per number. (d) the box's labels and numbers are text under the canvas, as `cover` boxes are: they are repainted with the right tile (15 times a second while meters move). (e) nothing runs layout per frame; numbers change ≤ 4 times a second in out-of-flow boxes; the `ResizeObserver` measures only when a size changes.
- **Cost**: the 30-minute test feeds 86.4 M frames through both meters and reads them 1800 times in 2.7 s (debug test binary, ebur128 at opt-level 3; another helper building at the same time), so about 0.15 % of a core while playing, plus a short-term reading ten times a second (a sum over 3 s of frames, about 0.3 M additions). Nothing while the mixer is closed. Memory, only while the mixer is open (the meters are dropped when it closes): ebur128 keeps the filtered frames of its window as f64, 3 s for `now` and 0.4 s for `play`, so about 2.6 MB at 48 kHz (5.3 MB at 96 kHz), plus two histograms of 8 KB each.
- Fable was asked once (a read-only review of the analysis thread, the play signal and `Mono`): no bug found; it listed the behaviours under Readings (one-wake boundary, a ring overflow resets the play's numbers, a mixer opened mid-play measures from then).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md` ("Rules added in round 12", M3):

- In the mixer band, right of the master strip, the box 「仕上げのメーター」, two strips wide, while the mixer is open: its name and 「モノで聞く」 at the top; level with the master's meter 「いまの大きさ」 (the last 3 s, LUFS, with a bar on the meters' scale), 「曲ぜんたい」 (LUFS since the song last started to play, gated), 「トゥルーピーク」 (dBTP, the most since then), and 24 bars of the master's sound from low to high (the same values as the top band's, all `color.meter.ok`); under them 「低い音 ← → 高い音」; at the bottom 「公開の目安は −14 LUFS、−1 dBTP」.
- 「いまの大きさ」 follows whatever is heard, keys played while stopped too, and is 「−∞」 3 s after the sound stops (under −70 LUFS) or when the output stops. 「曲ぜんたい」 and 「トゥルーピーク」 count only while the song plays or records (not the count-in), start anew each time it starts to play, and keep their values after it stops; before the first play they are 「−∞」.
- Numbers have one decimal, change at most 4 times a second, and are 「−∞」 with no sound.
- The loudness is measured only while the mixer is open: opened in the middle of a play, 「曲ぜんたい」 counts from that moment.
- 「モノで聞く」: while on, the master after its inserts is left and right mixed into one, the same on both sides (each side half of each), over 20 ms; the master's meters, the bars and the loudness hear it; the test tone and the metronome are not mixed. Lit while on; the status line says 「モノで聞いています」 when turned on and in place of the summary while on. Not a step, usable while counting in and recording, not in the song or the settings, never in an export, off when meloet starts.
- Keys (`4-keys.md`): none. Step names: none.

## Readings

- **Two meters** in place of the spec's one (see Architecture): the spec says nothing about what the numbers do while stopped; this way 「曲ぜんたい」 stays the last play's and 「いまの大きさ」 shows what is heard now. Question for the lead if one meter is wanted after all.
- **What the meters hear with 「モノで聞く」 on**: the spec puts it "after the master inserts, before the meter", so every meter of the master (the strip's meter, the top band's number, the bars, the loudness, M2's watch) shows the mono sound, as heard. 「曲ぜんたい」 of a play with mono switched on part of the time is then partly mono (the export is never mono). To have the loudness hear the song as it is instead, move `self.mono.mix(…)` after `self.analysis.write(…)` in `Engine::render` (one line); the master's peak meter would still show mono.
- **The level of mono** (the spec gives none): `(L + R) / 2` on both sides: a centred sound is as loud as before (as the pan law, 19-11), nothing gets louder than the louder side, so no new clipping; a sound on one side only is 6 dB lower on each.
- **The bar of 「いまの大きさ」** is on the meters' scale (spec 2 "Scale marks of meters and faders": −14 LUFS at 37 % of the bar), so it reads as the master's meter beside it. The spec's picture has −13.2 LUFS at 70 % (another scale, not given anywhere); no new numbers were made for one.
- **The bar is on its own line** under 「いまの大きさ … −13.2 LUFS」, the whole width of the box; the picture has it between the label and the number. Between them its length would change with the width of the number (「−∞」 is shorter), and a fixed width for the number would need a size the tokens do not have.
- **The rows are level with the master's meter** (measured on the screen) and the 24 bars fill what is left of that height, so they grow with the band; the picture's 56 px is about what they get at the smallest mixer at 100 %. 「低い音 ← → 高い音」 is under the bars (the picture), 「公開の目安は …」 at the bottom (not in the picture).
- **The button** is in the head row as in the picture (the text lists it last, with no place).
- **Colors**: labels, 「低い音 ← → 高い音」 and 「公開の目安は …」 gray.8 (`m-lbl`; 「うすい字」); the number of 「曲ぜんたい」 gray.10 (the picture's "w"), the other numbers gray.9; the box's background gray.3 as a track strip, the head's top line gray.10 as the master's.
- **No sound**: 「−∞ LUFS」 / 「−∞ dBTP」 (the number part is `common.dbSilentBare`). 「いまの大きさ」 under −70 LUFS is shown as 「−∞」.
- **The play**: playing or recording, not counting in; a recording after a count-in starts anew when the recording starts. Moving the play line or a loop going round is not a new play.
- **Not worked while nothing shows it**: core knows only whether the mixer band is open; while the box is scrolled out of view the loudness is still measured (as E7's bars while hidden for lack of room).
- **The fake**: `reduceMonoListen` (`ui/src/bridge/fakeWave3.ts`) switches `workspace.monoListen` and says `monoListening` while on (back to the summary when turned off). Scenes `finishMeters` (the picture's numbers, playing), `finishMetersMono` (mono on, the status line), `finishMetersSilent` (stopped, 「−∞」), in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py` with no board (1280×800 at 100 and 150 %, 1920×1080 at 100 %; shot in all 6 themes).
- **Tests changed**: the "changes nothing yet" lists of protocol 33 (`crates/meloet-core/src/state/wave3_tests.rs`, `ui/tests/bridge.protocol33.test.ts`) no longer have `SetMonoListen`; `opened()` of `state/tests.rs` lets `Effect::MonoListen` through, and `HP_NODE` there is `pub(super)`.
- **Wording**: all from the spec (`user:2026-11-23`): 「仕上げのメーター」「いまの大きさ」「曲ぜんたい」「トゥルーピーク」「公開の目安は {lufs} LUFS、{peak} dBTP」「モノで聞く」「モノで聞いています」, and from its picture 「{lufs} LUFS」「{peak} dBTP」「低い音 ← → 高い音」 (`ui/src/text/ja.ts`, `mixer.finish*`, `mixer.monoListen`, `msg.monoListening`). Nothing made up.

## Measurements

None (other helpers were building; the PC was not quiet). Only the cost estimate above from a test's time. For the lead after the merge, on a quiet PC:

- `tools/measure/webkit-damage.sh --song <8-track song> --commands '{"afterMs":1300,"command":{"type":"toggleBand","band":"mixer"}},{"afterMs":2600,"command":{"type":"playStop"}}'` (the mixer open, playing): the right half of the mixer should be one rectangle that now reaches over the box, not taller than before, and no frame "one rectangle around all"; also with 「モノで聞く」 on (`{"type":"setMonoListen","on":true}`).
- `tools/measure/webkit-layout.sh` with the same: no layout per frame from the box (numbers ≤ 4 a second).
- `tools/measure/latency.sh --mixer --song <song> --state playing` (show the virtual keyboard first), next to the numbers before M3.
- `tools/measure/app-watch.sh --song <64-track song> --commands <open the mixer, play> --seconds 30`: busyMs of the mixer with 64 tracks, before and after; and the CPU of the thread `meloet-analysis` with the mixer open while playing and with it closed (`ps -L -o tid,comm,pcpu -p <pid>`; expected well under 1 % in release, and as E7's alone when closed).
- `python3 -I tools/measure/export_app.py same` with 「モノで聞く」 on: the export must be the song as it is.
- By ear: 「モノで聞く」 on and off while playing (no click), a hard-panned part becoming centred and quieter.

## Left for later

- The fake's scene `mixer` (and others with the mixer open) now shows the box with bars but 「−∞」 numbers: `MIXER_METERS` has no loudness (the scenes were not changed).
- 「曲ぜんたい」 and 「トゥルーピーク」 start anew when the analysis ring overflows (the thread stalled for 341 ms), even after a stop when they should hold; rare.
- A loud key held in the 10 ms before 「再生」 can reach the play's true peak (the one-wake boundary).
- ebur128's short-term window keeps 3 s of filtered frames (2.6 MB at 48 kHz) while the mixer is open; a short-term meter of 100 ms sums (as `export/analysis.rs` does) would need a few hundred bytes, if the memory matters.
- Not checked in the real app (no screen run with real sound): how the bar and the numbers move with a real song, whether 4 updates a second feels calm, the mono switch by ear.
