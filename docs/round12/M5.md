# M5: a lane for the master's volume, and "add a fade-out"

## Architecture

What goes into chapter 25 of `docs/architecture.md` (and changes the line "No lanes for returns A/B or the master" of 21-5: the master has one lane, of its volume; the returns, the master's pan and the master's inserts still have none).

- **Where a lane lives** (`crates/meloet-model/src/automation.rs`): `LaneHome { Track(TrackId), Master }` (`From<TrackId>`). Every function of the model that named a lane by its track now takes `impl Into<LaneHome>` (`add_lane`, `delete_lane`, `write`, `write_press`, `put_points`, `set_resting`, `rest_lane`, `restore_lane`), so the callers that pass a `TrackId` did not change. The functions that name a lane by its `LaneId` (`add_point`, `move_points`, `delete_points`) find the home themselves. `Doc::lane(id)` returns `(LaneHome, &Arc<Lane>)` (it returned the track); new `Doc::lanes(home)`, `Doc::lane_of(home, target)`, `Doc::has_lane_target(home, target)` (the master: `AutoTarget::Volume` only; `add_lane` on the master with another target is `NoSuchInsert`).
- **Steps**: the eight lane steps of `StepKind` (`AddLane`, `DeleteLane`, `AddAutoPoint`, `MoveAutoPoints`, `DeleteAutoPoints`, `WriteAutomation`, `RestLane`, `RestoreLane`) carry `home: LaneHome` in place of `track: TrackId`. No new step kind and no new step name: the history row of a master step has the same name and `StepWhat::Lane { target: Volume }` as a track's, and `StepPlace::Master` in place of the track (`play/history.rs` `on_home`). A hand on the master's fader that rests the lane is the old `SetGain { strip: Master }` step with the rest inside it, as for a track.
- **`Doc::check_lanes`** goes over the tracks' lanes and the master's in one loop: the target is one the home has, one lane for a target, ids below `next_id` and once in the whole song (a track's lane and the master's can not share one), points in order and in 0..=1.
- **`add_fade_out(doc, bars)`** (model, pure): see "Spec rules". One `Edit` with `StepKind::AddFadeOut { range }`. Inside it: the lane is made when there is none (`add_lane`), a resting lane is woken (`set_resting`), the points are put with `with_points` (new `LaneId`, chunks shared). `NoTakes` for a song with no clip (core turns it into `StatusMessage::FadeOutNoClips`), `Unchanged` when the lane already is exactly that fade and does not rest, `OffTheSong` past `MAX_TICK`.
- **Engine** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/engine.rs`): `MasterAuto` holds the master's `AutoSet` (the same type as a track's, with the one lane `Volume`), whether a hand holds it, whether the lane had the fader in the last piece, and a list of steps. New commands `EngineCommand::SetMasterAutomation { set }` and `HoldMasterLane { on }`, `EngineHandle::set_master_automation(lanes)`; the old set comes back on the same retired ring as the tracks' (`take_retired_automation`).
  - **The trap**: the master's strip is not cut into stretches (it runs once over the whole piece, after the returns), but the song's position moves stretch by stretch (tempo points, the loop's end, mute steps cut the piece). So the ticks can not be worked out when the strip runs. `MasterAuto::mark(clock, from, to)` is called for every stretch right after `Engine::automate`, before the transport moves, and writes for every step of `MIX_STEP` = 32 frames "the frame after its last, the tick at its last frame" into a list made once (`max_frames` entries: a stretch is a frame at least). `MasterAuto::level` then walks the list: `AutoSet::level` (the same function as a track: the line's value → `Gain::from_position` → linear, the place moving at most the whole way in `RAMP_S` = 20 ms), `Strip::steer_level`, `Strip::level` on that step's frames. So the steps, the line, the slope and the speed limit are a track's, frame by frame (`tests/automation.rs` runs the track's frame-by-frame test on the master too).
  - **No lane**: `MasterAuto::level` calls `strip.level(left, right)` once over the piece, exactly the call that was there before. A master without a lane, with a lane that rests, or whose lane was taken away long ago gives the same samples as before M5 (tests below).
  - When the lane stops playing (deleted, at rest, held), the fader goes back to the hand's value over 20 ms (`steered`, as `Track::steered`). A hold is kept whatever sets come. `Engine::prepare` (a new stream) forgets where the place had got to.
  - Nothing is allocated and no lock is taken: the list is a boxed slice made with the engine; `tests/automation.rs::automation_allocates_nothing_on_the_audio_thread` now also swaps the master's set and holds it.
- **Core** (`crates/meloet-core/src/play/automation.rs`, `autosync.rs`): `LaneKey` is `(LaneHome, AutoTarget)` (it was `(TrackId, AutoTarget)`), so the hands (`Grip`), 「● 書く」 (`armed`), the writing, the rest-with-a-value step, the holds and the selection of points are one code for tracks and the master. `gain_lane(StripId::Master, …)` now names the master's lane, which is all it takes for the mixer's drag table of 21-11 to hold for the master's fader. The five lane commands take `LaneHome` as it comes (`model_home`). `hand_place` of the master is its fader; `settle` (a deleted lane leaves the value of the line at the play line) writes the master's gain.
  - **What the engine is told** (`autosync.rs`): `EngineOp::MasterAutomation { lanes }` (the lane when it moves: not resting, with points; else none) whenever the lane's id differs from the one last sent, and `EngineOp::HoldMasterLane { on }`. `Audio::op` (`service.rs`) and `ExportEngine::tell` (`export/render.rs`) pass them on; the export ignores the hold, as it ignores a track's.
  - **A new stream is always told the master's lane, also "none".** The engine outlives a stream, and `Audio::op` drops automation while no stream runs; so a lane deleted while no stream ran would stay in the engine. (`play/tests.rs::the_stream_opening_gets_everything` now counts 10 ops.) The tracks' lanes have this hole, see "Left for later".
  - **Folded**: `Session::master_lane_folded` (the tracks' list `folded_lanes` keeps its type, which `duplicate_track` reads). In the song file it is one more entry of the session's `foldedLanes`: `{"master": true, "target": {"kind": "volume"}}`; an entry that names a lane the song does not have is skipped, as before.
  - **`AddFadeOut`** (`Play::add_fade_out`): refused while `busy()`; bars outside `1..=FADE_OUT_BARS_MAX` do nothing (as A2's `insert_space`; the screen puts the number into the range and tells); `FadeOutNoClips` with no clip; else one `apply`.
- **Export**: no code of its own. The job's `Play` opens the song, `send_automation(true)` sends `MasterAutomation`, `ExportEngine::tell` hands it to the same engine code. `service/export_tests.rs::a_fade_out_is_exported_as_it_plays_and_ends_in_silence`: the exported file and what the playing side gives are the same sample for sample; before the fade's start the file is the song's without a fade (within 1e-6); half way the level is the line's; the last frames are below 1e-3; with 「余韻」 the plain song rings on for more than 0.5 s and the faded one for less than 0.25 s; a song whose master lane rests exports bit for bit as the song without a lane.
- **A2** already moved `Doc::master_lanes`; core test `empty_bars_put_into_the_song_move_the_fade_with_its_end`.
- **M2** (`lower_all_tracks`) does not touch the master's lane or fader (it lowers tracks; its doc said so already). Test `lowering_all_tracks_leaves_the_masters_lane`.
- **A3** (duplicate track) is not touched: the copy's lanes are the track's.
- **Song file**: nothing new but the fold entry. `mixer.master.automation` was already read and written by the shapes pass, and `check_lanes` now also guards it in every `Doc`.

- **Screen**:
  - `automation/laneValue.ts`: `masterHome`, `isOnMaster`, `LaneOwner` / `masterOwner`; where the screen looks a lane's owner up by a track id the master is `MASTER_HOME` = −1 (only inside the screen; intents carry `{kind: "master"}`). `trackHome` and `isOnTrack` take it. `StripAuto.mark` is the master's automation mark.
  - `automation/autoGeom.ts` `arrangeRows` has the master's row after the last track (`master`, `tracksHeight`; track reordering stops at `tracksHeight`). `TrackHeads.svelte` draws its head (`LaneHead` with 「マスター 音量」), `Arrange.svelte` its lane (`AutoLane`, `WriteLine`), inside the regions the tracks' lanes use (`arrangeLanes`, `recordingClip`): no new WebGL region.
  - `bridge/lane.ts` `songLaneIds` also returns the master's lane id (without it `keepOnly` throws its points away).
  - `autoState.svelte.ts` works out the master's line value at the play line; `mixer/MixerStrips.svelte` and `Strip.svelte` make the master's fader follow it (the same slot under the `mixerMeters` canvas as a track's fader), show 「オートメーション」 / 「休み」 and 「もどす」, and put an `AutoMark` at the right end of the master's value row (there was no disabled mark before; it is new).
  - `menu/menus.ts`: the row at the end of 「編集」 after a separator. `arrange/fadeBox.svelte.ts` (open or not, and the screen's own "song end") and `arrange/FadeBox.svelte` (the box, in the bar ruler with its right edge at the song's end; `valueClamp.readBars`).
  - `StatusLine.svelte`: the three master sentences reuse the tracks' sentences with 「マスター」 and 「音量」; `automationMoving` names the master after the tracks.
  - Fake: `bridge/fakeFade.ts` (`fakeFadeOut`, the core's rule), the five lane intents and `selectAutoPoints` for the master in `fake.ts`. The fake adds no history row for it, still does nothing for adding, moving or deleting points, and does not rest a lane when a fader is moved (tracks neither).
  - Scenes (`tools/design/shoot_ui.py`, six themes, no mock boards): `masterFade` (100 and 150 %), `masterFadeFolded`, `masterFadeWriting`, `fadeOutNoClips`, `mixerMasterAuto` (100 and 150 %), `mixerMasterRest`, `menuEditFade`, `fadeOutBox` (100 and 150 %), `fadeOutAdded`, `fadeOutLonger`. Tests: `ui/tests/round12.m5.test.ts` (28).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md`, "Rules added in round 12" (and the line of `7-readings.md` that said the master has no automation):

- The master can have one automation lane, of its volume. The returns, the master's pan and the parameters of the master's inserts have none.
- While the master has the lane, the arrange has a row 「マスター」 at the foot of the tracks that shows it. Points, 「● 書く」, 「休み」 and 「もどす」, folding and 「帯を消す」 are those of a track's volume lane. A hand on the master's fader rests the lane (or writes, with 「● 書く」 on while the song plays), as the table of the mixer drags says for a track.
- The lane is made from the automation mark of the master's volume in the mixer, or by the fade-out.
- 「編集 ▸ 曲の終わりにフェードアウトをつける…」 opens a box for the length in bars (4 at first, 1 to 16). Then:
  - the end of the fade is the song's end: the end of the last clip, up to the head of a bar;
  - its start is that many bars before, counted in the bars of the meters they are in; the song's head when the song is shorter;
  - a point at the start with the master's volume there as it sounds now (the lane's value when the lane moves the fader, else the fader), a point at the end at the bottom (−∞), a straight line between (straight in the place of the fader, as every lane);
  - points of the lane from the start on go; the lane is made when there is none; a lane that rests moves again;
  - it is one step, 「フェードアウトをつける」; the history band shows its bars and the place 「マスター」;
  - a song with no clip: nothing happens and the status line says so; while counting in or recording: nothing.
- The export has the fade, as it plays.

No keys. No new step name (「フェードアウトをつける」 is in the list already).

## Readings

Decisions where the spec said nothing:

- **Only straight segments exist.** A lane's line is straight in the place of the fader between two points, so the fade is straight on the fader's scale (not in dB, not in level): half way it is at half the fader's way. No curve shapes were added.
- **A shorter fade over a longer one changes nothing that is heard.** The start of the new fade takes the lane's value there, which lies on the old fade; so the result is the old line with one more point on it (it is a step). To make a fade shorter: undo, or drag the points. A longer one replaces the old one. **Question for the owner**: should a new fade always start from the fader's value (or from the value before the old fade) so that "again with fewer bars" shortens it?
- **A lane at rest**: the fade starts from the fader (what sounds now), and the lane moves again, so points before the fade's start play again too. (The protocol's text says so; the spec only says "the master's volume now".)
- **A lane with no points** is as no lane: the fade starts from the fader.
- **A point exactly on the start** is replaced by the fade's point.
- **Bars outside 1 to 16** reaching core do nothing (the screen never sends them), as A2's empty bars.
- **The export's 「曲ぜんぶ」 ends at the end of the last clip, not at the head of the next bar.** When the last clip ends inside a bar, the file ends before the fade's end, at the level the line has there, and 「余韻」 then rings on at that level until it is quiet. When the last clip ends on a bar line (the usual case, and the tested one) the file ends in silence. **Question for the owner**: should the fade end at the end of the last clip instead (what the export sees), or the export's range end at the bar?
- **M2 「全部のトラックを下げる」 leaves the master's lane and fader alone** (it lowers tracks).
- **The status line of the mixer's opening** (`automationMoving`) is also said when only the master's fader is moved by a lane.
- **Folding the master's lane** is saved with the song, as a track's.

Screen decisions:

- **The row** is one row as in the mock: where a track's lane head says 「オートメーション ・ 音量」 it says 「マスター 音量」.
- **Folded**, the row stays as a low row 「▸ マスター 音量」 (a track's folded lane hides behind 「＋ 帯を足す」; the master has no such row, and the only other way back would be the mixer's mark). Question for the owner.
- **The mixer's mark** sits at the right end of the master's value row (after 「dB」); the spec speaks of a mark "disabled today", but there was none. Question for the owner.
- **The box** opens in the bar ruler with its right edge at the song's end (the left edge in a song with no clip; Enter then gives the status line). ↑ ↓ change it by 1, 10 with Shift; what is not a whole number can not be decided. The row is disabled while counting in or recording and in the arrange's whole-song view.
- No key for the menu row.

## Left for later

- **A track's lanes deleted while no stream runs stay in the engine** (seen, not fixed; it was there before): `send_automation(true)` on a new stream skips seats that have no lanes now, `Audio::op` drops `EngineOp::Automation` while no stream runs, and the engine is the same one from stream to stream. So: no output open → delete a track's lane (or put it to rest) → open an output: the engine still plays the old lane until the next change of that track's lanes. For the master this round always sends the lane, also "none". The same fix for tracks: on `all`, send `Automation { lanes: [] }` to every seat that has none (or have `Audio` keep the lanes as it keeps the mixer).
- **The pan under a volume lane**: while a lane has the fader, a pan set by hand gets to its place within one step of 32 frames, not over 20 ms (`steer_level` overrides the ramp of `set_level`). The same for tracks since round 6; the master's pan under its lane now too.
