# M7: pulling the pitch of a vocal clip toward the scale (「音程を寄せる」)

## Architecture

**Where it lives.** Everything that listens and bends is in `crates/meloet-stretch`, in safe Rust; no new crate, no change to `vendor/`, no new C function (the C door already had `set_transpose_semitones`; it is now called between `process` calls).

- `src/pitch.rs`: the pitch detector. YIN (de Cheveigné and Kawahara 2002, steps 1 to 5: difference function, cumulative mean normalised difference, absolute threshold 0.15, parabola through the dip of the raw difference). The channels are mixed to one, low-passed (a sinc under a Hann window) and brought down by a whole factor to about 24 kHz (48 kHz ÷ 2, 96 kHz ÷ 4, 44.1 kHz ÷ 2). Range 70 to 1000 Hz, integration window 25 ms, one point every 10 ms (`track_pitch` → `PitchTrack`: MIDI notes with their fraction, NaN where there is no voice). A point has no pitch when it is quieter than −60 dB, when its best dip is above 0.25, when the dip is not a dip of its own (the true period is outside the range), or when fewer than 3 points in a row have a pitch.
- `src/tune.rs`: the curve (`tune_curve` → `TuneCurve`), numbers only. It is laid on the clip's own time (one point per 10 ms of what is heard), so the smoothing is the same however the clip is stretched. Per point: the file frame `p(j)` → the nearest point of the track → note + the clip's transpose → distance to the nearest note of the scale (the lower one on a tie) × strength. Points without a voice want 0; those within 60 ms of a voiced point take that point's move (the spectral blocks that make the start and the end of a note reach that far). Then the whole line is smoothed forward and backward with a one-pole of 25 ms (together `exp(−|t|/25 ms)`: a sudden change of the wanted move is spread over about 50 ms, no lag). Moves under half a cent become exactly 0. A safety limit of ±2 semitones (no scale needs more than 1.5).
- `src/render.rs`: `render_bent` is the old `render` with a call before every step (256 frames) that sets the transpose for the blocks made in that step; `render` calls it with a closure that does nothing, so it does exactly what it did. `render_tuned` = `track_pitch` over the file frames the clip plays, `tune_curve`, then `render_bent` with `transpose + curve.at(frame)`. The frame given to the curve is the middle of the step plus the stretcher's output latency: a block made while output frame J is read is heard around J + output latency. Progress: listening is the first 20 %.
- `Preset::for_tuning(rate)`: block 0.12 s, interval 0.02 s. A block takes the transpose of the moment it is made, so the pitch moves in steps of one interval. With `presetCheaper` (interval 0.04 s) a pure tone asked to move 15 cents moved 7; with six blocks over every moment small moves come out within 1 to 2 cents for a voice-like tone. Only tuned clips use it.

**Order of what is applied.** One run of the stretcher does all three: (1) the stretch (where the file is read, `p(j)`), (2) the clip's semitones and cents, (3) the tuning, computed on the pitch as it sounds after (2). The clip's length and place do not change (the same `Follower` arithmetic as before).

**Core.**

- `made::plan`: a clip with `AudioShape::tune` is made even when it is neither stretched nor pitched. `MadePlan::tune` (`TunePlan { scale: [bool; 12], strength }`), `MadePlan::tuning()` gives `meloet_stretch::Tune`. 「声の感じを保つ」 now also counts for a tuned clip (the formant step is on when the clip is pitched or tuned).
- The key: only when tuned, these are appended: `"tune"`, the preset name `tune-120-20`, `PITCH_VERSION`, `TUNE_VERSION`, the 12 notes as bits, the strength. A clip that is not tuned has the key it had before (old cache files stay good). The notes are in the key, not the scale's name: C major and A minor are the same audio and are not made twice. Changing the strength or the notes makes the audio again; volume, fades, name, the song's scale, edits elsewhere do not.
- `maker::render_plan`: `render_tuned` for a tuned plan. While the pitch is looked for nothing is written, so the check for new asks (cancel) is also made from the progress call (a `RefCell` around `wrote`). Export uses the same function (`make_now`), so an export has the same audio.
- Model: `set_clip_tune_strength(doc, clip, percent, root, mode)` and `set_clip_tune_scale(doc, clip, root, mode)` in `audio/stretch.rs`, both `StepKind::SetClipTune`, through `with_shape` (the take is not touched).
- Intent: `Play::set_clip_stretch` handles `TuneStrength` and `TuneScale`; the scale is `session.roll.scale` of that moment. `Play::tune_usable(clip)`; `SelectedClip::tune_usable` is false for a clip whose file is missing and for a clip with a comp.

**Traps.**

- After a tuned stretch of sound, the samples of what follows are not bit-equal to an untuned render even where the curve is 0: the phase vocoder carries its output phases on. Same sound (levels per band within 0.5 %), other samples. Before the first move, the output is bit-equal.
- Where the curve is exactly 0 and the clip is not pitched, the library takes its "no pitch change" path for that block; elsewhere the mapped path. Switching between them block by block showed no clicks in the listening files (largest sample-to-sample jump not above the dry file's).
- YIN's dip of the normalised difference can sit one lag beside the dip of the raw difference (a period of exactly N + 0.5 samples); the parabola is taken at the raw dip.
- `44100 / 100 / 2` is not whole: at 44.1 kHz the points are 442 frames apart (10.02 ms).

## Spec rules

For `docs/spec/3-bands.md`, "Rules added in round 12" (source `user:2026-11-23`):

- The panel 「選んだクリップ」, group 「のび縮み ・ 音の高さ」 (now 5 rows in its count), after 「声の感じを保つ」: the row 「音程を寄せる」 with the strength 0 to 100 % (0 = off, the start value), and under it a row with the scale it is brought toward (「A マイナー」) and the button 「いまのスケールにする」.
- The strength is dragged up and down (2 px a percent; the screen alone moves, one intent on release, Esc stops) or typed after pressing twice (「60」 or 「60 %」; out of range goes to the ends).
- Leaving 0 the clip takes the song's scale (the piano roll's, 9 kinds) and keeps it; changing the song's scale later does not change the clip. 「いまのスケールにする」 takes it again (disabled at strength 0 and when the clip has that scale already). At 0 the clip keeps no scale, and the row shows the song's.
- Where a voice sounds, its pitch is moved toward the nearest note of the scale by "distance × strength", smoothed over 50 ms. Where no voice sounds (breath, consonants, silence) nothing is moved.
- It works together with semitones, cents, 「声の感じを保つ」 and 「曲のテンポについていく」; the tuning is applied to the pitch after semitones and cents.
- The audio is made in the background like any stretch (300 ms after the change, 10 ms cross-fade, the progress bar).
- Tooltip on the label: 「ひとりの声や、音が1つずつの楽器に使えます」.
- Both rows are shown disabled when the clip cannot be tuned (file missing: the notice is shown instead; a clip with 「いいとこ取り」), and while recording or counting in.
- Step name 「音程を寄せる」 (`SetClipTune`), "what" is the clip name. No new keys.

For `docs/spec/6-messages.md`: status line while dragging the strength: 「音程を寄せる 60 %（ドラッグ中）」; the reading in place is 「60 %」.

## Readings

- The spec says "YIN or McLeod": YIN, as in the paper, with the paper's 25 ms window and threshold 0.15; "not sure" above 0.25 (my number).
- "50 ms でなめらかに": read as a zero-lag smoothing whose step response goes from 18 % to 82 % in 50 ms (two passes of a 25 ms one-pole). A 5.5 Hz vibrato keeps about half its depth at strength 100.
- The spec gives no limit of the move. The natural limit is half the widest step of the scale (150 cents in the pentatonic and blues scales); a safety limit of ±200 cents is in the code.
- "声でない所は変えません": points within 60 ms of a voice move with it (else the first 50 ms of each note would be half tuned, because a spectral block is 120 ms long); further away the move is exactly 0.
- The spec says the stretcher of tuned clips should get its transpose every 256 to 512 frames: done (256), but the library uses it once per block, so the real step is the interval. For that reason tuned clips use a denser preset (block 0.12 s, interval 0.02 s) than other made clips (`presetCheaper`). A clip that is stretched or pitched therefore sounds slightly different once it is also tuned.
- 「声の感じを保つ」 on a tuned clip keeps the formants in place for the small moves too.
- The strength row is a dragged number like the pitch row beside it (the spec does not say bar or number; the protocol comment says "bar"). Step: `line.mark` (2 px) per percent. `StretchNumber` got an optional `step` property for it.
- The scale row has no label of its own: the scale's name is in the label's place, the button on the right. No wording was needed for it.
- The group's count went from 4 to 5 (the scale row belongs to 「音程を寄せる」).
- The strength is shown with the existing `param.value.percent` (「60 %」), the scale with `scale.name`.
- Made-up wording: `msg.tuneDragging` 「音程を寄せる {percent} %（ドラッグ中）」 (source `builder:2026-11-23`), in the form of 「音の高さ … （ドラッグ中）」.
- Wording from the spec (`user:2026-11-23`): `clipParams.tune` 「音程を寄せる」, `clipParams.tuneToScale` 「いまのスケールにする」, `clipParams.tuneTip` 「ひとりの声や、音が1つずつの楽器に使えます」.
- `tune_usable`: the spec has no rule. True for a vocal clip that plays one take and whose file is found. There is no "kind of audio" in the model, so drums or chords cannot be told apart; the tooltip is the only warning. Taking the tuning off (strength 0) is allowed even when not usable.
- Existing fake scenes with a selected vocal clip now say `tuneUsable: true` (as core does); the missing-file scene stays false.
- **For A8 (comp) to decide**: `made::plan` makes audio from the shown take only (`clip.active_audio()`). A clip with a comp plays pieces of several takes, so a comp clip is `tune_usable: false` for now and the two tune intents do nothing on it (a tune it already has stays in the song and is still applied to the shown take's render). A8 must decide whether a comp clip is rendered piece by piece (then tuning can be on: pitch per take, one curve over the clip) or tuning stays off for comps, and what happens to a tune set before the comp was made.
- If the owner's ear says no: one line turns the rows to disabled, `tune_usable` in `crates/meloet-core/src/state/audio.rs` (set it to `false`); songs that already have a tune keep playing it.

## Measurements

No speed was measured (the PC was not quiet). Accuracy from the tests (48 kHz, printed with `--nocapture`):

- A voice-like tone 30 cents flat (notes 45, 57, 64, 72): median distance from the note 0.4 to 1.7 cents at 100 %, 13.7 to 14.3 at 50 %. A pure sine: 0.1 to 5.3 at 100 %, 12.3 to 14.8 at 50 %.
- A slide of two semitones in one second at 100 %: largest move in 10 ms 28 cents (a step would be 200); 20 % to 80 % of the change in 50 ms.
- Vibrato ±40 cents at 5.5 Hz, 20 cents flat: mean on the note (64.01), depth 38 → 17 cents.
- Stretched ×1.25, +2 semitones and tuned in one run: 1.3 cents from the note.
- A click before a note stays on its frame; the note's start within 1 ms, its end 81 frames late (the block's tail).
- Through the maker (core test): the first harmonic lands +5.5 cents (100 %) and +1.6 cents (50 %) from where it should; single harmonics are less exact than the period.

To measure after the merge, on a quiet PC: `tools/measure/stretch_app.py kept` (no remaking), `tools/measure/stretch_app.py prepare` (speed, next to the old numbers; a tuned clip does about twice the stretcher work of `presetCheaper` plus the listening pass), `tools/measure/export_app.py same` with a tuned clip, and `cargo test -p meloet-stretch --release --test stretch -- --ignored --nocapture speed_of_sixty_seconds_of_stereo` for the untuned base.

## Left for later

- Octave mistakes of the detector are not repaired. They rarely matter (a scale repeats every octave, so the move is the same), but a mistake by a fifth can pick another note.
- Above 1000 Hz (whistling, a very high soprano) a sub-period inside the range can be taken; the move is then toward a wrong note.
- Breathy or rough voices fall under "not sure" and are left alone in patches; the tuning then comes and goes inside one note.
- Chords and backing tracks: the result cannot be trusted (the tooltip says so); nothing detects them.
- On the real voices of the listening set the 50 ms level differs from the dry file by up to a few dB at some note edges (9.4 dB at one place in `3-singing-male` at 100 %); not looked into.
- The pitch is listened for again on every render (each change of strength). A cache of the track per audio and range would make changes of the strength quicker.
- Every tuned clip goes through the phase vocoder as a whole, also where its curve is 0, so consonants are smeared a little, as in any stretched clip.
- `tune_usable` with a comp could not be tested through commands (no intent makes a comp before A8).
- Not checked in the real app: the rows on the real screen, the cross-fade when a tuned render is swapped in, `webkit-damage.sh` (nothing new changes at 60 Hz).
