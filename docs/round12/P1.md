# P1: scale highlight and 9 kinds of scale

## Architecture

- The shapes came with protocol 31 (`ScaleMode` with 9 kinds, `MusicalScale.highlight`, `SetMusicalScale.highlight`, song file `ScaleFile.highlight` with `#[serde(default = "yes")]`, so a version-6 song opens with it on). P1 adds no shape.
- The steps of each kind live in Rust (`ScaleMode::steps` in `crates/meloet-model/src/roll.rs`). The screen keeps a copy, `SCALE_STEPS` in `ui/src/components/pianoroll/geom.ts`. A Rust test (`write_the_scale_steps_for_the_screen` in `crates/meloet-core/src/play/roll_tests.rs`) writes `ui/src/components/pianoroll/scaleSteps.table.json` (protocol names → steps, in the order of the list), the same way the tempo map writes `tempoMap.table.json`: a table that is not the committed one shows up in `git status`. `ui/tests/pianoroll.scale.test.ts` holds `SCALE_STEPS` equal to that table. The test lives in meloet-core because only core knows both the model's `ScaleMode` and the protocol's names.
- Row colors (`rowColor` in `geom.ts`): a row outside the scale is `color.row.out` when the lock **or** 「色をつける」 is on; otherwise white and black rows as before. The hatch in `paint.ts` still comes only with the lock (unchanged code), so "highlight only" is `row.out` with no hatch, and notes can be placed there (`writable` still looks only at the lock). No new color, no new WebGL region, nothing new at 60 Hz (the same number of row rectangles as before).
- Drum tracks: `paintDrumRoll` never calls `rowColor`, and the lock rule in core already skips drums, so neither highlight nor lock works there (as before).
- The scale stays session data: `SetMusicalScale` is no step and does not change `song.file` (checked for every kind in `the_locked_scale_keeps_the_write_tool_on_its_keys_for_every_kind`).
- Chords of the key (`keyChords` in `chords.ts`): 7-note scales use their own notes. A 5- or 6-note scale uses its parent: major pentatonic → major; minor pentatonic and blues → minor (`PARENT_MODE`). The chord row still appears only while the lock is on (unchanged).
- Names of the kinds: `scaleModeName` in `ui/src/components/format.ts` (one table, used by `scaleName`, `ScaleMenu.svelte` and the menu bar). The kind lists take their order from the generated `SCALE_MODES`.

## Spec rules

- 「スケール」 has 「色をつける」 (`highlight`), on at first (a new song, and a song saved before round 12). On with the lock off: rows outside the scale are `color.row.out`, no hatch, and notes can be written there. Lock on: as before (`color.row.out` and the hatch, cannot be written). Both off: white and black rows only.
- 9 kinds, in this order: メジャー `0 2 4 5 7 9 11`, マイナー `0 2 3 5 7 8 10`, ハーモニックマイナー `0 2 3 5 7 8 11`, メロディックマイナー `0 2 3 5 7 9 11`, メジャーペンタトニック `0 2 4 7 9`, マイナーペンタトニック `0 3 5 7 10`, ブルース `0 3 5 6 7 10`, ドリアン `0 2 3 5 7 9 10`, ミクソリディアン `0 2 4 5 7 9 10`. The scale ▾ list, the list of the right panel and 「ノート」 → 「スケール固定 ▸」 of the menu bar all list the 9.
- Choosing a kind or root from the toolbar ▾ or from the menu bar turns the lock on (as before) and keeps 「色をつける」. Choosing from the right panel keeps both.
- Toolbar: right of the scale ▾, a toggle 「色」 (on color while on). Right panel 「スケール」: a row 「色をつける　オン／オフ」 under 「固定」.
- Chords of the key: 5- and 6-note scales list the chords of their parent (major pentatonic → major; minor pentatonic and blues → minor).
- Drum tracks: 「色」 is 「使えない」 (hover shows 「ドラムには音階がないので、スケール固定は使えません。」), and the right panel's row reads 「使えない」.
- No new key, no new step name.

## Readings

- Toolbar label: the spec's text says 「色」 (the mock shows 「色をつける」; the text wins). Its `aria-label` is 「色をつける」 (`inspector.highlight`). It sits right after the scale ▾, so the order is 「スケール固定 C メジャー」「▾」「色」. The spec says "beside 「固定」"; the toolbar has no separate 「固定」 button (the lock is the 「スケール固定 …」 button), so it goes after the ▾ that belongs to it.
- New wording (all `user:2026-11-23`, `ui/src/text/ja.ts`): `scale.harmonicMinor` 「ハーモニックマイナー」, `scale.melodicMinor` 「メロディックマイナー」, `scale.majorPentatonic` 「メジャーペンタトニック」, `scale.minorPentatonic` 「マイナーペンタトニック」, `scale.blues` 「ブルース」, `scale.dorian` 「ドリアン」, `scale.mixolydian` 「ミクソリディアン」, `inspector.highlight` 「色をつける」, `pr.scaleHighlight` 「色」.
- Drum tracks: no new wording. The toolbar's 「色」 reuses the tip `msg.drumsNoScaleLock` (its first half gives the reason; the second half names スケール固定). The right panel reuses 「使えない」 (`inspector.lockUnusable`). If the owner wants a tip of its own for 「色」, it needs new wording.
- The menu bar's 「スケール固定 ▸」 gets the 9 kinds but no 「色をつける」 row (the spec names only the toolbar and the right panel).
- The default is on, so every fake scene and the real app now show rows outside C major in `row.out` even with the lock off (for C major these are the black-key rows). Scenes compared with mocks drawn before round 12 (A-PianoRoll with lock on is unchanged; F-PRPlay, F-PRRec, F-PREmpty and others with the lock off) now differ from their mocks in those rows. This follows the spec ("はじめはオン").
- Fake scenes: `scaleHighlight` (A-PianoRoll in D blues, highlight on, lock off) and `scaleHighlightLocked` (the same, locked). D blues was chosen so white-key rows outside the scale (E, B…) and a black-key row inside it (G#) are both seen. Added to `BOARDS` (no mock board) and sizes in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py`.

## Measurements

None. Nothing new moves at 60 Hz and the note area draws the same number of rectangles as before (the row color only changes), so no damage or frame check should be needed; a quick look with `tools/measure/webkit-damage.sh` is not required.
