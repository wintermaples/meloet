# P2: show the notes of other tracks faintly in the piano roll

## Architecture

- Screen only. No new shape, no protocol change, nothing in Rust. The takes come from the take cache with `takeNotes(id)` / `peekTakeNotes(id)` (`ui/src/bridge`); every clip's shown take is kept by `keepOnly(songTakeIds(song))`, so a fetched take of another track stays while a clip shows it.
- State: `ghostView` in `ui/src/components/pianoroll/view.svelte.ts` (`on`, `hidden`, `serial`). It lives in the module, so it is kept when the band folds and until the app closes; it is not saved. `hidden` holds the tracks taken off in the list (so every track the list offers is shown at first, and a track added later is shown too). Track ids are per song, so `hidden` counts only while `song.serial` is the `serial` it was made in (`ghostHidden`).
- Logic: `ui/src/components/pianoroll/ghosts.ts` (pure, no DOM):
  - `ghostCandidates`: key tracks (`kind.kind === "keys"`) that are not drums (`trackDrums`), other than the track the piano roll shows (`shownTrackId`: the shown clip's track, or `keysTarget` when there is no clip).
  - `ghostWindowIndex` / `ghostWindow`: a window three visible widths wide (one to the left, two to the right), as `lanePicture` in `Arrange.svelte`. The index changes only when the left edge moves by a whole width, so scrolling inside one width does not rebuild the list.
  - `ghostClips`: clips of notes (not audio) overlapping the window. `ghostShown`: a `Shown` (geom.ts) with base `clip.start + clip.takeOffset` and range `[clip.start, clip.end)`, so only notes that start inside the clip are drawn; the same object comes back while take, base and range are the same.
  - `paintGhosts`: one `color.gray.6` rectangle per note in view (rows and ticks), at the place of a note's face and edge (1px right of the start line to 1px left of the end line, height `part.note.height`), no edge, no border. At most `GHOST_MAX` = 20 000; past it, the notes farthest from the play line (in ticks; 0 when the line is inside the note) are left out: a first pass counts, and only when over 20 000 the distances are sorted to find the limit (ties: the first met are drawn).
- `Roll.svelte`: `ghosts` ($derived) asks for the takes of the clips in the window (fetch once per id, `ghostVersion` goes up when one arrives) and returns the same array while nothing in it changed, so the note area is not drawn again for unrelated state changes. It is null when off, on drum rows, or before the area has a width. The `rollPicture` effect puts it in `RollPicture.ghosts` with the play line position read at that moment (`shownTick`, untracked). Redrawn only when it changed: a take arrived, scroll, zoom, the choice of tracks, on/off. Never per frame.
- **Draw order in the `pianoRoll` region** (`paintRoll` in `paint.ts`): background → rows → hatch (scale lock) → grid lines → **notes of other tracks (ghosts)** → the shown clip's notes → borders of the position before quantizing → borders of the drag's starting place → notes being recorded. The velocity lane has no ghosts. Drum rows (`paintDrumRoll`) never draw them. The `pianoRollLive` region (a canvas over the note area, `z-index: 1`) is drawn above all of it; E1 (sounding notes light up) can draw there or after the notes in `paintRoll` and stays above the ghosts either way. The ghost code is one call line in `paintRoll` (`if (p.ghosts) paintGhosts(...)`) plus `ghosts.ts`.
- They cannot be pressed: hit tests, box selection, the drag, the note list and audition read only the shown take (`n`); ghosts never enter it.
- Tool row (`Toolbar.svelte`): the toggle 「ほかのトラックも見る」 and its ▾ (a button of its own, the same form as the scale ▾). The ▾ opens a `ChoiceMenu` of the offered tracks (`track.label`, 「2 ベース」), each a check row; picking a row takes the track off or puts it back and the list stays open. On drum rows both are 「使えない」 (the toggle with a tip). With no other track to offer, the ▾ is 「使えない」.
- **Width**: the label is about 155 px at 100 %. `toolbarWithGhost` in `ui/src/components/fit.ts`: the label hides **first**, only when the row cannot hold everything; then the tool keys and the clip text hide as before (`toolbarParts`, unchanged). When the label is hidden, only the ▾ stays and the on/off switch becomes the first row of its list (「ほかのトラックも見る」, checked while on). The label's width is measured with a hidden copy (`.ruler`).
  - Result: the label shows at 1920×1080 100 %; at 1280×800 (100 % and 150 %) and at 1920×1080 150 % only the ▾ is there. The piano roll scenes keep what they showed before P2: compared pixel by pixel with shots taken in this worktree at the base commit (5460482) before any change, `pianoRoll` 1280×800 100 % differs only in a 20×24 px box (the new ▾, between the clip text and 「選ぶ」), `pianoRoll150` 1280×800 150 % only in a 30×36 px box (the ▾), `pianoRollWide` 1920×1080 100 % only in a 157×24 px box (label and ▾); the clip text and the key text are where they were. The ▾ sits in the room the spacer had, so nothing else moves.
- Development: the UI script (`ui/src/dev/script.ts`, `MELOET_UI_SCRIPT`) has a part `pr-ghosts` that turns it on for the parts after it, so `tools/measure/ui-script.sh` can measure scrolling and zooming with it on.

## Spec rules

- Piano roll tool row: 「ほかのトラックも見る」 with its own ▾, right of the clip text (after the spacer, before the tools). Off at first. Pressing turns it on and off. The ▾ lists the key tracks that are not drums (built-in kits and SF2 drum kits) other than the track shown, as 「2 ベース」; vocal tracks are not listed. All are on at first; a row takes a track off or puts it back (the list stays open). Screen-only state, kept until the app closes (not saved, not in undo); what was taken off counts only for that song.
- On drum rows both are 「使えない」 (hover on the toggle: 「ドラムのトラックでは、ほかのトラックは見られません。」). With no track to offer, the ▾ is 「使えない」.
- Drawn: the shown take of each clip of the chosen tracks that overlaps the view, at song positions (take 0 at clip start + take offset; only notes that start inside the clip), across the whole note area (also outside the clip being edited). `color.gray.6` fill only, after the grid lines and before the shown clip's notes. Cannot be selected, hit, moved or heard. Not in the velocity lane. At most 20 000 rectangles; past that, those farthest from the play line are left out.
- Narrow rows: the label 「ほかのトラックも見る」 hides first, then the tool keys, then the clip text (as before round 12); with the label hidden, the ▾ stays and its list starts with the row 「ほかのトラックも見る」 (the switch).
- No new key, no new step name, no menu bar entry.

## Readings

- The spec writes 「ほかのトラックも見る ▾」 as one thing but says pressing turns it on and the ▾ list chooses tracks. Read as the existing toggle + ▾ form (scale lock + scale ▾, draw tool + chord ▾): the label (`pr.ghost` 「ほかのトラックも見る」, `user:2026-11-23`, the spec's text without the ▾) is the toggle, and the ▾ is a separate button.
- Place: right after the spacer, before the tools, as in the spec's picture (the button right of the track text).
- The spec's picture also shows the chosen track names (「1 ピアノ」) after the button. The text does not say so, so they are not shown (the row is already short of room).
- Fold order (not in the spec): **the label hides first**, then the keys and the clip text as before. The lead decided this on 2026-11-24: the spec and the mocks are the source of truth for the existing look, and the new control must not push existing text out, so every piano roll scene at 1280×800 100 % (A-PianoRoll, F-PR720, F-PRPlay …) still shows the clip text and the key text (only the ▾ is new). The first build had the other order (label last, the same form as 「重ねる」 in the transport), which hid the clip text at 1280×800 100 %.
- **Question for the owner** (for `docs/spec/open-questions.md`): at 1280×800 the label does not fit next to the clip text, so there the switch is only in the ▾'s list (first row). Is that all right, or should the row show the label and give up the clip text (or the key text) at that size?
- The folded form (only the ▾; the switch as the first row of the list) is made up: the spec has no smaller form of 「ほかのトラックも見る ▾」.
- The list is a check list: rows stay open after a pick (other `ChoiceMenu`s close). Its heading is made up.
- Default `hidden` (not a list of chosen tracks): "all at first" is kept as tracks are added; the shown track is never offered, so switching to another track's clip offers the one before.
- When the song changes (another song opened, `song.serial`), what was taken off is forgotten; on/off is kept.
- A disabled ▾ when there is nothing to offer (spec: parts with no content are shown 「使えない」). The toggle stays usable (it is kept on for later tracks).
- The play line for the 20 000 limit is the position when the picture is made (the picture is not drawn every frame).
- Clips of audio on a key track (none today) are skipped.
- Wording made up (`builder:2026-11-23`, `ui/src/text/ja.ts`): `pr.ghostTracks` 「見せるトラック」 (the ▾'s name and the list heading), `pr.ghostDrums` 「ドラムのトラックでは、ほかのトラックは見られません。」 (tip of the 「使えない」 toggle on drum rows; in the words of `msg.drumsNoScaleLock`).
- Fake scenes: `pianoRollGhost` (F-WidePR with 「3 ドラム」 made a kit and 「5 ボーカル」 a vocal track, plus a bass clip 「ベース イントロ」 at bars 2–4 with its first bar trimmed, take offset −1 bar). Shots (`tools/design/shoot_ui.py`, no mock board): `pianoRollGhost` (clicks the label; 1920×1080 100 %, where it shows), `pianoRollGhostMenu` (the list open, 1920×1080 100 %), `pianoRollGhostFolded` (1280×800 100 % and 150 %: the ▾ alone, its list open with the switch row picked). Shot in all 6 themes.
