# P4 Drum patterns on the shelf

## Architecture

- Data: `tools/patterns/drums.json` (format `meloet-drum-patterns`, version 1), written by us, no outside material. Each pattern: `id`, `group` (`basic` / `fill`), `bars` (1), `grid` (480 = a sixteenth, 640 = an eighth-note triplet for the shuffle) and `notes` (`piece` = a `DrumPiece` id, `at` and `length` in ticks from the pattern's head, `velocity`). Built in with `include_str!` (as `tools/sounds/catalog.json`) and read once (`LazyLock`) in `crates/meloet-core/src/patterns.rs` (`all`, `find`, `Pattern::notes_at`, `Pattern::view`). A note's key comes from `DrumPiece::note()`, so it sits on the same key in the three kits of meloet and in an SF2 kit (General MIDI).
- Core: `crates/meloet-core/src/play/patterns.rs`.
  - `ShelfView::patterns` lists them only in the shelf's `NewTrack` mode (also while the shelf is closed, whose mode is `NewTrack`). `Shelf::pattern` is the chosen row (`ShelfSelectPattern`; an unknown id or another mode gives none). It is forgotten when the shelf closes and when the shelf opens in another mode.
  - `PlacePattern`: refused while `busy()`, outside `NewTrack` mode, or with the shelf closed. The place is the head of the bar of the play line (`bar_head(play_line())`); every bar of the pattern must be 4/4 there, else `StatusMessage::PatternNeedsFourFour`. The target is the selected track when its instrument's group is `Drums` (a kit of meloet or an SF2 kit; a plugin's group is always `Synth`, so a plugin drum track is never one). Else a new studio kit track right below the selected one (`edit::add_track`; past 64 → `TooManyTracks`). Then `notes::create_clip_to(start, start + length)` (`Overlap` → `StatusMessage::NotesNotWritten`) and `notes::write_notes(WriteAt::Clip)` on that `Doc`; the three `Doc` changes are put together into one `Edit` with `StepKind::PlacePattern { track, clip }` (one undo step that also takes the new track away). The new clip is selected and shown (`ClipPick::one`, as pasting); a new track becomes the selected track; the play line goes to the clip's end (`locate`), so the next Enter places the next one right after it on the same track.
  - `PlayPattern` (▶): goes through the sample ▶'s path (`play/sounds.rs`). `Sample` and `SampleWaiting` got a `pattern` field; `sample_when_read` waits for the kit's samples as a sound's sample does. The kit: the selected track's when it is a drum track of meloet (installed) or of an SF2 file, else the studio kit; nothing when the studio kit is not installed either, or without an output. The notes are turned into seconds with the tempo map from the bar head where it would be placed (the song's tempo there), and the length in µs (`PatternSample::length_us`) replaces the fixed 4 s of a sound's sample for the end (`sync_sample`: length + the 2 s tail). `StopSample`, closing the shelf, a sound's ▶ and the output going away stop it, as before. `ShelfView::sound_sample` leaves out a pattern; `pattern_sample` shows it (`playing: None` while the kit is read). The status line says nothing of a pattern (`sample_status` gives `None` for it).
  - Engine: new core-only op `EngineOp::PlayPattern { instrument, hits }` (`hits` = `meloet_sound::phrases::Hits`, (start s, length s, key, velocity)). `service.rs` makes a `PhrasePlayer::hits` with it and sends it to the sample seat like `PlaySample`. `ExportEngine::tell` ignores it (never exported); the sample seat feeds only the master sum (never recorded, no meters, no keys).
  - `meloet-sound`: `PhrasePlayer` keeps a velocity per note now (`Hits`); `PhrasePlayer::new` gives every note `VELOCITY` as before.
- Screen: `ui/src/components/shelfPatterns.ts` (`patternModel`: headings 「基本」「フィルイン」 that fold with `shelfUi.open["pt:<group>"]`, open at first; `placeText`), `Shelf.svelte` (the third switch 「パターン」 only in `newTrack` mode; the list; the bottom line with 「ここに置く Enter」), `tracks.svelte.ts` (`shelfUi.view` can be `"patterns"`; ↑ ↓ → `shelfSelectPattern`, Enter → `placePattern`, Space → `playPattern` / `stopSample`, F does nothing; no shelf status line in that view), `StatusLine.svelte` (`patternNeedsFourFour`).
- Fake: `ui/src/bridge/fakeP4.ts` (`FAKE_PATTERNS`, a copy of the json's rows which a test compares; `reduceP4` for the three intents and `stopSample` while a pattern plays; the notes it writes are a plain 8 beat for every pattern). `fake.ts`: `closedShelf`/`settleShelf` give the rows in `newTrack` mode, `newKitTrack`, the chain in `send`, `patternNeedsFourFour` in `NOTICES`. Scenes `shelfPatterns`, `shelfPatternsNewTrack` (shot through `CLICKS` in `tools/design/shoot_ui.py`, since the switch is screen-only state).
- Trap: the existing tests that pin the shelf's heading text (「▾ 棚 音源 31 エフェクト 9 P」) now have 「パターン」 in it; and the round-12 wave-2 tests (`state/tests.rs`, `bridge.protocol32.test.ts`) expected no patterns.

## Spec rules

- The shelf's switch has 「パターン」 after 「音源」「エフェクト」, only when the shelf is open as 「新しいトラック」. Its list: 「▾ 基本」 (8 ビート, 8 ビート（ハイハット開き）, 16 ビート, 4 つ打ち, シャッフル, ハーフタイム, ライドで 8 ビート, バラード) and 「▾ フィルイン」 (スネアの連打, タムまわし, クラッシュで止める, 小さなフィル); each row ▶, the name, 「1 小節」. The headings fold. At the bottom 「（トラック） ・ （小節） 小節めに置きます」 and 「ここに置く Enter」.
- ↑ ↓ or a click choose a row; ▶ (or Space for the chosen row) plays it once at the song's tempo with the selected drum track's kit, else the studio kit; ■ or Space stops it. It ends by itself. It is not recorded and not exported.
- 「ここに置く」 (Enter): at the head of the bar of the play line, a clip one pattern long with its notes, on the selected track when it is a drum track (a kit of meloet or an SF2 kit), else on a new studio kit track right below the selected one, which becomes the selected track (one step with the clip). The clip is selected, the play line goes to its end, the shelf stays open. Refused: over another clip (「ほかのクリップと重なるので、書けませんでした。」), where the meter is not 4/4 (「4/4 のところにだけ置けます」), a new track past 64 (the usual notice), while counting in or recording.
- Step name 「パターンを置く」 (already in the list). No new keys (↑ ↓ Enter Space of the shelf).

## Readings

- Drum track = the instrument's group is ドラム (the three kits, an SF2 drum kit). A plugin drum track (avldrums and so on) is not one: 「ここに置く」 makes a studio kit track, and ▶ plays the studio kit. Reason: meloet does not know a plugin's key map, and the sample of a plugin needs its own loaded copy (`PlugSample`), which takes no notes of ours. On an SF2 kit the GM keys are the same as ours, so the pattern sounds right.
- Placing over a clip uses the existing `NotesNotWritten` 「ほかのクリップと重なるので、書けませんでした。」 (the spec says 「今ある『重なる』の知らせ」; of the five, this one says "could not write", which fits placing notes best).
- The new clip is selected and shown in the piano roll, as pasted clips are (the spec says nothing).
- The tempo of ▶ is the tempo map at the bar where it would be placed (「いまの曲のテンポ」 when the song has tempo changes).
- While a pattern plays, the status line has no "sample playing" line (`msg.samplePlaying` says 「行をえらぶと、鍵盤でためし弾きできます」, which is about sounds); the row's ■ shows it. No 「0:01 / 0:02」 time on a pattern row (the mock shows none).
- In 「パターン」 the mode line, the search box and 「すべて ／ お気に入り」 are not shown (the mock shows only the headings, the rows and the bottom line; they are about sounds). No star on a pattern row.
- The headings start open (the mock shows ▾).
- The bottom line's bar is the play line's start position (`TransportView::position`), as P6.
- ▶ of a pattern row does not choose the row (as a sound's ▶).
- Wording `user:2026-11-23` (from the spec): 「パターン」, 「基本」, 「フィルイン」, the twelve names, 「{bars} 小節」, 「{track} ・ {bar} 小節めに置きます」, 「ここに置く」, 「4/4 のところにだけ置けます」 (without 「。」, as written in the spec).
- Wording made up (`builder:2026-11-23`): `shelf.placeOnNewTrack` 「トラック {number}（{instrument}）を作って、{bar} 小節めに置きます」 (the bottom line when a studio kit track will be made; the number is the place right below the selected track), `shelf.playPattern` 「パターンを聞く」 (the ▶ button's name for screen readers, as 「サンプルを聞く」).
- The patterns themselves (which drum where, velocities): my own, from generic rhythm knowledge. Rules the data test checks: only `DrumPiece` sounds, inside one 4/4 bar, starts on the 16th grid (the shuffle on the eighth-note triplet grid), a sound at most once at a time, at most 2 hands and 2 feet at once (feet = kick and pedal hi-hat), velocities with at least 10 between the weakest and strongest, the bar's head at 96 or more (「クラッシュで止める」 is one blow: crash, snare and kick together, so it has no weak hits).
