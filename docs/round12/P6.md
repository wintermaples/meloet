# P6 Chord progressions

## Architecture

- Core: `crates/meloet-core/src/play/progression.rs`, `Play::add_notes_at` for `Command::AddNotesAt`. It uses the same way as the chord stamp (`write_at`, then `Play::write_notes` → `notes::write_notes`): the clip grows by bars to hold the notes (back and on, B-82, the same `Reach` growth as `PasteNotes`), existing notes stay, all in one step `WriteNote`, the notes written are selected. Refused while `busy()`; an old take id does nothing; `take: None` while no clip is shown writes on the play-target track as `AddNotes` does.
- "Not past the next clip": `notes::write_notes` refuses the whole write with `EditError::Overlap` when the grown clip would overlap another. `add_notes_at` tries again without the notes of the latest start, until it fits (at most 8 tries for the longest progression). So the chords from the first one that would hit the next clip on are left out, and nothing is said.
- Notes given twice (same start and key) are written once; keys past 127 are left out; length at least 1, velocity put into 1..=127.
- Screen: `chords.ts` holds the table of the six progressions in degrees of the major scale, `parentScale` (P1's rule; the P1 helper may add the same function: keep one), `progressionTonic`, `progressionChords`, `progressionText`, `placedKeys` (root C3..B3, root position). `progression.ts` holds `firstBar`, `progressionNotes` (bar heads with `barAt` of `ui/src/time/bars.ts`, which uses `TempoMap.tickOfBar`, so meter changes are followed bar by bar), `chordMs`, and `ChordSequence` (▶: `Audition` sent from a screen timer, one chord per two quarter notes at the song's tempo, then an empty `Audition`). `ProgressionMenu.svelte` is the list; `ChordRow.svelte` has the button and opens the list in `document.body` (as the lists of the 「選んだノート」 panel), closes it on Esc (through `menuOpened`), on a press outside, after placing, and when the lock goes off; closing stops ▶.
- Fake: `fakeRound10.ts` `addNotesAt` (only into the shown take; grows the clip to the right by bars, leaves out from the chord that would reach the next clip; one history row).

## Spec rules

- With the scale fixed, the chord row has 「進行から並べる ▾」 at its right end. It opens a list: each row ▶, the name, the chords in the current scale (「F – G – Em – Am」). Rows in this order: 王道 IV V iii vi, カノン I V vi iii IV I IV V, 小室 vi IV V I, 循環 I vi IV V, ポップ I V vi IV, 切ない vi V IV V. The bottom line 「▶ で聞く ・ Enter で 1 小節に 1 つずつ並べる」 and 「並べる」.
- ↑ ↓ or a press choose a row (「選んだ行」 look); Enter or 「並べる」 puts the chosen one and closes the list; Esc or a press outside closes it. ▶ plays the chords one after another, two quarter notes each at the song's tempo (■ while playing; pressing it again stops). Closing the list stops it.
- Placing: from the bar of the play line when the play line is inside the shown clip, else from the clip's first bar; one chord a bar, root position, the root from C3 to B3, a bar long, velocity the fixed one when it is on, else 100. One step (step name 「ノートを書く」 as the chord stamp). Notes there stay. The clip grows as pasting does; chords from the first one that would reach the next clip are left out.
- Minor-like scales (マイナー, ハーモニックマイナー, メロディックマイナー, ドリアン; and マイナーペンタトニック, ブルース through their parent) are read in their relative major (root + 3 semitones). The others (メジャー, ミクソリディアン, メジャーペンタトニック) in the major of their root.
- No new keys.

## Readings

- Which scales are "マイナー系": the ones whose third is minor (minor, harmonic minor, melodic minor, dorian, and minor pentatonic and blues through their parent scale). Mixolydian is read as the major of its root. This is the spec's rule taken word for word; for dorian and mixolydian it gives chords outside the scale (D dorian → read in F major: B♭ C Am Dm; G mixolydian → D major chord). Reading them in the major of the same notes (D dorian → C major, G mixolydian → C major) would sound better. Question for the owner.
- 「クリップの最初の小節」: when the clip does not start on a bar line, the first bar whose head is inside the clip (no growth to the front). The same when the play line is in that first part bar.
- With no clip shown (the chord row is still there with the scale fixed), the chords go from the bar of the play line with `take: null`: Rust writes them on the play-target track (in the clip there, or a new clip of 4 bars or up to the next clip, as the write tool; notes past that clip are left out).
- The play line is `TransportView::position` (while playing it is the position of the last state patch, not the moving line).
- Chords left out at the next clip are not said on the status line (the spec says nothing; `NotesNotWritten` would say nothing was written, which is wrong when some were).
- The chord names use the same names as the chord row (「Bdim」 style, sharps as `pitchClassName`).
- ▶ plays at C4..B4 (as the chord row's buttons), placing is at C3..B3 (as the spec says).
- The chosen row starts at 王道 each time the list opens.
- Wording (`user:2026-11-23`, `ui/src/text/ja.ts`): 「進行から並べる ▾」, 「並べる」, the six names, the separator 「 – 」 and the bottom line 「▶ で聞く ・ Enter で 1 小節に 1 つずつ並べる」 (from the spec's picture), 「聞く」 (the name of the ▶ button for screen readers; from the bottom line).
