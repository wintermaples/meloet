# R3 Metronome settings and tap tempo

## Architecture

- **Engine** (`crates/meloet-engine/src/transport.rs`, `engine.rs`):
  - The engine has its own `ClickSound` (`Beep`, `Tick`, `Cowbell`), because the engine does not know the protocol. `crates/meloet-core/src/service.rs` (`click_command`) maps the protocol's `ClickSound` to it and turns the dB into a linear gain (`10^(dB/20)`).
  - `EngineCommand::SetClick { sound, gain, recording_only }`. The sound and the gain are kept in `Engine` (`click_sound`, `click_gain`); `recording_only` is kept in `Transport` (`click_recording_only`). They are read only in `Click::start`, so a change takes effect from the next click and never changes a click that sounds. `prepare` does not reset them (they belong to the PC; core sends them again at every stream open anyway).
  - `click_audible`: count-in always clicks; playing clicks when the metronome is on and `recording_only` is off; recording clicks when the metronome is on. So 「録音のときだけ」 also works for `RecordHere` (recording that starts while playing): the clicks start at the frame the state turns to recording.
  - `Click` is now a sum of up to 6 cosines (a fixed array, no allocation). Every cosine starts at phase 0 and its level only goes down, so `|s(t)| ≤ Σ level_k(t) ≤ Σ level_k(0) = s(0)`: the first sample is the loudest and is positive, for every sound. One voice: `start` replaces the click before. 35 ms with the 48-frame fade at the end, as before. The gain is put into 0..2 (+6 dB); a value that is not a number counts as 1.
  - The three sounds (all made by calculation, no samples):
    - 「ピッ」 `Beep`: exactly the old click (1760 Hz on the bar head, 1046.5 Hz on other beats, 8 ms decay).
    - 「カチ」 `Tick`: three cosines at 1, 1.59 and 2.31 times 2500 Hz (bar head 3150 Hz), shares 0.55, 0.3, 0.15, decays 4, 2.5 and 1.5 ms.
    - 「カウベル」 `Cowbell`: 540 Hz and 800 Hz with their 3rd and 5th harmonics (the drum machines' cowbell idea, without the band-pass filter, which would break "the first sample is the loudest"), decays 10, 6 and 4 ms; the bar head is a fourth up (720 Hz).
  - The bar head is **higher and louder** with every sound: the first sample is 0.17 on the bar head and 0.13 on other beats at 0 dB, for every sound. `tools/measure/click-check.py` tells the bar head by this level ratio (`BAR_LEVEL`, `BEAT_LEVEL`), so it keeps working with all three sounds and any volume (it scales by the loudest click).
- **Core**:
  - `EngineOp::Click { settings: ClickSettings }`. `Play::set_click` (called from `Command::SetClick` in `state.rs`): the values given change, the others stay. The volume is put into −24..+6; a value that is not a number is not taken. `commit: false` changes only what the engine plays (`Play::click_heard`); `commit: true` keeps the values (then `Effect::SaveSettings`) and drops `click_heard`. An engine op is sent only when what the engine plays changes.
  - Not a step, not part of the song, and taken while counting in and recording (it is a PC setting, like `ToggleMetronome`).
  - `Play::stream_opened` sends `EngineOp::Click` right after `Metronome` (with the heard values, so a stream that opens during a drag plays what is heard).
  - `ExportEngine::tell` ignores `EngineOp::Click`; the export's own `Play` has the metronome off, so no click reaches an export (as before).
- **Screen**:
  - `Transport.svelte`: the metronome button and its ▾ are in one `.metro` box, so the ▾ (always there now) counts as part of the fixed width; the count-in no longer leaves a stub when it hides (`transportWithOverdub` gets no `countIn` stub).
  - `MetronomeWindow.svelte`: the small window, opened by the ▾ into the same popup place as the lists (in `body`, closed by Esc, by a press outside, or by another menu). The volume bar is built like `params/ParamRow.svelte` (jump to the press, `commit: false` at most once a frame by `requestAnimationFrame`, one `commit: true` on release, a gray.7 frame at the setting while moving, Esc via `onDragCancel` sends `commit: false` with the setting to put the sound back). ← → use `KeySteps` from `mixer/mixDrag.ts` (`commit: false` per press, `commit: true` a second after the key is let go or when focus leaves).
  - `metronome.ts`: `TapTempo` (pure) and the volume mapping and text.
  - **Trap**: the window lives in `body`, outside the mount target `#app`. Svelte then handles its events with its listener on `document`, which is the same node as the document key handler (`keys/attach.ts`). `stopPropagation` does not stop another listener on the same node, so Space on 「タップ」 would also have played the song. The window uses `stopImmediatePropagation` (Svelte's document listener is added at `mount`, before `App`'s `onMount` adds the key handler, so it runs first).

## Spec rules

- spec 2 "Metronome ▾" (replaces the row): A button 20 wide, always right of the metronome button with no gap. When pressed, a small window (not a list) with rows: 「メトロノーム」 and 「オン」/「オフ」, 「音量」 (a bar from −24 to +6 dB and the number), 「音」 (「ピッ」「カチ」「カウベル」), 「鳴らすとき」 (「いつも」「録音のときだけ」), 「テンポを手拍子で」 (「タップ」, the tempo found, 「この速さにする」). While the count-in button is hidden, the window also has the row 「カウントイン」 (「なし」「1 小節」「2 小節」). States: same as a button; when the metronome is on, the ▾ also has the on color; while the window is open, the ▾ has the open look.
- spec 3, "Rules added in round 12" (R3, `user:2026-11-23`):
  - The metronome's volume, sound and when it clicks are meloet settings of the PC, not of the song. Changing them is not a step and works while counting in and recording. They are never in an export.
  - 「音量」: −24 to +6 dB in whole dB, 0 dB at first (the click as it always was). While dragging only the sound changes; the release keeps it. Esc during the drag puts the sound back. A double click on the number types it. A change is heard from the next click; a click that sounds is not changed.
  - 「音」: the head of a bar is higher and louder than the other beats with every sound.
  - 「鳴らすとき」: 「録音のときだけ」 clicks while recording (also a recording started while playing), not while playing. The count-in clicks either way, also when the metronome is off (as before).
  - 「タップ」: two taps or more give a tempo from the mean gap of the last 8 taps; a gap of 2 seconds or more starts again. Whole BPM, put into 30 to 300. Nothing is sent per tap. 「この速さにする」 sends one `SetTempo` (one step, the same as typing the tempo in the transport), then the taps start again. Both cannot be used while counting in or recording; the tempo found is dropped when the count-in starts.
- spec 4 keys (in the window only; no new key for the whole app): ← → on the volume bar move it by 1 dB. Space and Enter on 「タップ」 tap (and do not play or stop the song). Esc during the volume drag goes back; Esc otherwise closes the window.
- Step names: none added.

## Readings

- The look of the window (the mock gives the rows, not the sizes): rows `size.toolbar` high with `space.3` on the left and right and a `line.row` line below, the label on the left (gray.8; the first row 「メトロノーム」 in the name look) and the buttons on the right. The window is `part.list.width` wide at least and as wide as its rows; background gray.3, border gray.8 (as the lists). A chosen choice is the on look of the transport buttons (accent border and text, `aria-pressed`).
- The volume bar is the bar of a parameter row (spec 2: line gray.7, fill gray.9, a 6×12 gray.10 handle; `part.param.control` at least, the number `part.param.value` wide), not the meter-like bar of the mock, because it is a value the person moves.
- The volume is in whole dB (the mock shows 「−12 dB」). Typed values are rounded to whole dB and put into the range. A positive volume has no plus sign (「3 dB」), as the other dB numbers of the app.
- The first row's button says 「オン」 when on (the mock) and 「オフ」 when off (the spec's 「オン／オフ」).
- Before two taps, the tempo shows 「—」 (as other numbers that are not there).
- 「この速さにする」 is on the tap row, right of the tempo found (the mock has no place for it). After it is pressed the taps start again and the tempo found goes back to 「—」.
- "直近8回まで" is read as the last 8 taps (7 gaps). "2秒あいたら" is read as a gap of 2000 ms or more (a tap earlier than the one before also starts again).
- While the count-in button is hidden, the window has the count-in row: spec 3 says the count-in becomes the metronome ▾ and its contents are the same as in a wide window. The ▾'s name for screen readers is 「メトロノーム」, or 「メトロノームとカウントイン」 while the count-in button is hidden (both already in `ja.ts`).
- Pressing a sound does not play it at once; it is heard on the next click.
- The sounds 「カチ」 and 「カウベル」 were designed by me (numbers above); the owner should listen.
- New wording in `ui/src/text/ja.ts` (all `user:2026-11-23`, from the spec's 「」 or its mock): `metronome.on` 「オン」, `metronome.off` 「オフ」, `metronome.volume` 「音量」, `metronome.sound` 「音」, `metronome.beep` 「ピッ」, `metronome.tick` 「カチ」, `metronome.cowbell` 「カウベル」, `metronome.when` 「鳴らすとき」, `metronome.always` 「いつも」, `metronome.recordingOnly` 「録音のときだけ」, `metronome.tapRow` 「テンポを手拍子で」, `metronome.tap` 「タップ」, `metronome.tapNone` 「—」 (made up, as `inspector.scaleNone`), `metronome.useTempo` 「この速さにする」.

## Measurements

None (the PC was not quiet). The lead should measure after the merge:

- `tools/measure/latency.sh --state stopped|playing|recording` and `tools/measure/record-timing.sh private`: the audio thread's click path changed (a sum of up to 6 cosines for 35 ms per beat; expected to cost nothing measurable).
- `tools/measure/click-check.py OUT --song <song with --notes 0>` with each of the three sounds and at −24 dB: every click on its frame, the bar head told apart. (The sound must be set by hand or through the settings file before the run: `click_sound`, `click_volume_db` in the settings.)
- `tools/measure/export_app.py same` with the metronome on: no click in the export.
- The private PipeWire tests that record the metronome (`crates/meloet-core/tests/vocal_private.rs`, `vocal_timing_private.rs`, `vocal_play_private.rs`, `stretch_play_private.rs`) use the default 「ピッ」 at 0 dB, which is unchanged; not run here.
