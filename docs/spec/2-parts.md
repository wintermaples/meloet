## Scale marks of meters and faders

Meters and faders use the same scale marks. For the length, the very bottom (the left end for a horizontal meter) is 0 % and the very top is 100 %, and the points below are joined with straight lines. Sounds quieter than −60 dB are 0 % (−∞). Meters rise at once, and fall 20 dB in 1.7 seconds. Above 0 dB, a meter.clip 2px at the top end for 2 seconds.

| dB | +6 | 0 | −3 | −6 | −12 | −24 | −60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 100 % | 80 % | 70 % | 60 % | 40 % | 20 % | 0 % |
