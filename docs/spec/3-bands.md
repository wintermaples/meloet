# 3 Rules of the bands

From the top, the screen has the transport, the input meter, the arrange, the open lower bands, the band tab row, and the status line. The shelf is on the right rail. Heights and widths are px at 100 %.

| Band | What it shows when closed (when slim) | Smallest | When open | What it pushes smaller |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transport | — (cannot fold) | 48 | 48 | Nothing pushes it |
| Input meter | When slim: the link mark, the keyboard name, a keyboard 12 high, the held notes, velocity, sustain, pitch bend, modulation | 24 | 52 | 24 if even one lower band (piano roll, mixer, history) is open; 52 if none is open. It does not depend on the window height or on recording. Starting a recording or a count-in does not change its size |
| Arrange | Band tab: the number of tracks. While the height is under 136, the overview (the lines of the clips, the border of the visible range, the play position). From 40 to 135, the overview is drawn stretched up and down | 40 | All the rest. From 136 (the 3 ruler rows, 72, + one track, 64) it becomes track rows | Pushes nothing. The lower bands take their smallest heights and the part they grow by, and the arrange gets what is left |
| Piano roll | Band tab: the number of notes | 268 (toolbar 32 + ruler 20 + 10 note rows 120 + velocity 72 + CC tab 24) | Gets the rest, up to the largest, 736 (rows for 49 keys, 588) | Pushes the arrange down to 40. If it still does not fit, folds the older band |
| Mixer | Band tab: the value of the master fader (not the loudness. A new song is 0.0 dB) | 516 (toolbar 32 + strips 484; keeps a fader length of 120) | Gets the rest, up to the largest, 660 (fader length 264) | Pushes the arrange down to 40. If it still does not fit, folds the older band |
| Undo history | Band tab: always 「取り消せる手 / 全部の手」 (「14 / 14」「12 / 14」「0 / 0」) | 248 (heading 32 + 8 rows + 1 row) | 248 | Pushes the arrange smaller |
| Tempo and time signature changes | The number of changes (the tempo and time signature at the start of the song are not counted; a new song has 0), the current tempo and time signature | 24 | 72 | Inside the arrange. Pushes the tracks below it down |
| Automation | 「▸ パン」「▸ プラグインのパラメータ」 and the number of points | 24 | 48 for each lane | Inside the arrange. Just under the track that owns it |
| Velocity | — (「▸ ベロシティ」 at the left end of the CC tab) | 24 | 72 | Inside the piano roll. Pushes the note area smaller |
| Sustain, pitch bend, modulation | Side by side in the CC tab row. The number of pedal presses, the number of points | Tab row 24 (one row for all) | 48 each | Pushes the note area smaller. If the notes would go under 10 rows, folds the older band |
| Note list, selected notes | A vertical heading 24 wide. Name and number | Width 24 | Width 216 and 240 | Pushes the note area smaller sideways. In a window where the note area is under 4 bars (640), they start folded |
| Shelf | A vertical heading 24 wide. 「棚」, the number of instruments and effects, P | Width 24 | Width 320 | Pushes the arrange or the mixer smaller sideways. In the piano roll it uses the same place as 「選んだノート」, and the one opened later comes to the front |
| Params panel | A vertical heading on the right rail (under the shelf heading). 「パラメータ」, the name and number of the chosen item, U | Width 24 | Width 320. Inside, from the top: heading 24, name, format and place 48, (the window hint), search box 32, list | Pushes the arrange or the mixer smaller sideways. Takes no height. Uses the same place as the shelf and 「選んだノート」, and the one opened later comes to the front |
| Status line | — (cannot fold) | 24 | 24 | Nothing pushes it |

- A folded band does not go away. A lower band always stays in the band tab row, an inner band as one heading line, and a left or right panel as a vertical heading 24 wide, always in the same place.
- The band tab row is just above the status line. The order is arrange, piano roll, mixer, undo history. Bands added later go to the right of these. When the width is not enough, the counts and current values of the bands on the right are removed first. The names and keys are never removed.
- The vertical split is decided in this order. Window height breakpoints (960 and so on) are not used. (1) Take away the menu bar 24, the transport 48, the input meter, the band tabs 24, and the status line 24. (2) Give 40 to the arrange. (3) Give each open lower band its smallest height. (4) Give the rest to the most recently opened band, up to that band's largest height. (5) If some is still left, give it to the older band, up to its largest height. (6) If some is still left, give it to the arrange. In this order, making the window taller never makes anything smaller or makes anything go away.
- Besides the arrange, up to 2 lower bands can be open at the same time. When they do not fit in (3), the band opened earliest is folded automatically (its band tab stays). A band folded automatically is not opened again automatically, even when the window gets taller. Press its band tab to open it again. A band is folded automatically only in these 4 cases: the person makes the window lower, the person opens a band that does not fit, the person opens a third band (the rule of up to 2), and the person raises the scale. In each case, the status line says that the band was folded and why. So making the window taller never changes the set of bands. Each time a band opens, it counts as 「いちばん新しい帯」 (the newest band) (the same for a band opened again).
- Esc means 「ひとつ戻る」 (go back one). It closes things in this order: the box where text is being typed → a term tip or a menu → a dialog → the shelf → the most recently opened lower band. If nothing is open, it does nothing. Pressing it a few times always brings you back to the screen with only the arrange.
- A always goes back to the arrange in one press (it folds all the lower bands; the shelf stays as it is).
- Folding a band keeps the state of what is inside (the selected notes, the scroll position, the CC bands that were open).
- Call them 「パラメータ」 (the values a plugin has that can be moved). Only things whose shape really is a knob or handle, like the pan and the faders of the mixer, are called 「つまみ」.
- Pressing an insert slot or an instrument name makes that item 「えらんだ」 (chosen), and the params panel on the right shows its contents. An item with a window opens its window at the same time; if the window is already open, it comes to the front. For an item without a window, and for an item that cannot open its window yet, the params panel on the right opens automatically.
- The params list shows the parameters in the order and groups that the plugin gives. For an item with up to 40, all are shown open. For an item with more than 40, the groups start folded. An item that gives no groups is shown as one list, and the search box is relied on. At the very top, 「オートメーションに書いている」 is shown as one group.
- Typing in the search box shows only the parameters whose names match, group by group. Up to 6 are shown in each group, and the rest open with 「… ほか 6」. Emptying the box goes back to the group list.
- Pressing the mark at the right of a parameter row makes, under the track, an automation band that writes the movement of that parameter. Pressing 「▸ プラグインのパラメータ」 in the automation also opens the same list on the right.
- An item whose plugin window is open gets a window mark. meloet cannot know whether a window has gone behind other windows, so the mark only means 「ひらいている」. To bring the window to the front, press that slot or name again, or choose it from 「窓」 on the status line.
- When the same plugin is installed in 2 formats, the shelf shows it as one row. The format used is chosen in this order: the format the song already uses, a format that can open a window, CLAP, VST3, LV2. In the shelf counts it counts as 1.
- Outputs and inputs are shown with the real names that the PC gives (「アナログヘッドフォン」「ライン出力」「HDMI / DisplayPort 2」「リアマイクロフォン」). Only when the type is known, easy words are added next to the name. In the order, outputs with something plugged in come first, and among them headphones come first. The top one starts chosen, so that one Enter decides.
- Keyboard names are also shown as the real names. When the input meter is full and the name is over 8 characters, the name field widens from 108 to 148. When slim, up to 176 (up to 128 when scaled up and the width is not enough). A name that does not fit is cut at the end with 「…」, and hovering shows all of it. Before the name is cut, the readings are hidden, modulation first, then pitch bend. The link mark is never made smaller.
- 「バッファ」 on the status line shows the size that is really running, not the size asked for. When they differ, both are shown, as in 「バッファ 1024 ・ 頼んだ数 256」, and the running size is gray.10. No color is added.
- Instruments on the shelf are listed by instrument group (「ピアノ ・ 鍵盤」, 「ベース」, 「ドラム」, 「パッド ・ 弦」, 「シンセ」). Groups fold by their headings, and when folded, the heading and the count stay. Switching to 「お気に入り」 shows only the marked instruments, still in their groups.
- Choosing an instrument row on the shelf starts 「ためし弾き」 (audition). Meanwhile the keyboard plays with the chosen instrument, and 「弾くと鳴る先」 on the input meter changes to 「ためし弾き ・ （名前）」 in accent. Recording cannot be done. Enter puts it in; Esc stops and goes back to the sound of the original track. While you are in the shelf, Space is used for the sample, not for play.
- Places for features added later: groups and buses = left of the return strips of the mixer. Sidechain = the input chooser of an insert slot. Picking the best parts of takes = a band under the track (the same level as automation). MIDI effects = the slot above 「音源」 in a strip. MIDI mapping of knobs = the right end of the input meter. Nothing is drawn for these now.
- Even when parts are hidden, the way to their list stays as a ▾. The song name becomes a ▾ 28 wide, and the count-in becomes the ▾ of the metronome. The contents of the lists are the same as in a wide window.
- Pressing the song name gives the list 「新しい曲」「ひらく…」「保存」 (the keys are Mod N, Mod O, Mod S).
- Recovery file: just before the PC shuts down, the unsaved changes are written quickly. At the next start, the song opens restored without asking anything, and a hint is shown above the arrange (the same form as the hint of flow 3). The same goes for a new song that was never saved.
- Pressing 「戻さない」 makes the song the last saved form (for a new song that was never saved, an empty new song). This goes into the undo history as one step, so Mod Z can bring it back. The recovery file is not deleted until the next save.
- When the recovery file cannot be read, the last saved song opens (a new song if there is none), and a hint is shown in the same place. The file is kept, not deleted.
- The hint stays until the song is saved, or until 「戻さない」 or 「閉じる」 is pressed. Playing or recording does not remove it.
- The band tab of the undo history always shows 「取り消せる手 / 全部の手」 (「14 / 14」「12 / 14」「0 / 0」).
- Keys and sounding notes light up within 2 frames (33 ms at 60 Hz) of the event. It is fine for the second and later notes of a chord to be 1 frame late.
- During the count-in, the button shows 「カウントイン」 and 8×8 squares (space 4), as many as the beats in one bar. Beats already played are filled with accent; beats still to come are gray.7 lines. The text 「3 / 4」 is not shown. It is about 6px wider than the usual 「カウントイン 1 小節」 (an estimate). When it starts, things around it must not hide or move.
- Pressing the number of the tempo or the time signature turns it into an 「入力の枠」 (input box). Enter decides, Esc stops, ↑ ↓ change it by 1 (by 10 with Shift). A value out of range is set to the nearest end and decided (the rule of 「数字の欄」 below). The ranges are: tempo 30.00–300.00, time signature top 1–12, bottom 2, 4, 8, 16.
- Dragging on the ruler sets the loop range in whole bars and turns the loop on. Both ends can be moved by dragging. Turning the loop on when there is no range gives 4 bars from the bar of the current position. Turning the loop off keeps the range in memory, but it is not shown on the ruler or in the heading. Turning it on with L brings back the earlier range.
- The play position line is always shown, even in an empty song (a new song is at 1. 1. 000), so that you can see where recording will start.
- While there is no output yet, ▶ and ● are 「使えない」. Space and R also do nothing.
- Horizontal scrolling of the arrange: when the play line reaches the right end, the view moves on by one screen. After the view is moved sideways by hand, it does not follow the play line until play starts the next time. The piano roll is the same.
- The dialogs about saving (not saved, cannot save, the file is broken) are at the middle of the window from top to bottom, like the sound settings. 「やめる」 is on the left, and the bright button (「保存する」, 「場所をえらぶ」, 「1 つ前を開く」) is at the right end. Enter is the button at the right end, Esc is 「やめる」.
- **Rules for round 3 (piano roll and clips)**
- The piano roll has 3 tools. The select tool: pressing a note selects it, pressing and moving moves it, and pressing in an empty place and moving selects with a box (Shift adds). The draw tool: pressing an empty place makes a note one grid step long, pressing and moving right makes it longer, and on a note it works like the select tool. The erase tool: pressing a note, or dragging over notes, deletes them. With any tool, the right 6px of a note change its length, and the left 6px move its start (the end does not move). When a note is short and the left and right 6px overlap, each side is 1/3 of the width of the note. If there are selected notes, all of them change by the same amount.
- While dragging, the original position stays as an accent border, drawn above the other notes (so that it is not hidden when there are many notes). The quantize border of 「そろえる前の位置」 is also drawn above the notes: accent for the selected notes, gray.7 for the others. The original position of a clip in the arrange stays a gray.7 border (clips are large and are not hidden). Horizontally it snaps to the grid; with Shift it does not snap. Esc goes back to before the drag. The value being moved (position, pitch, length, velocity, CC) is shown both in the 「その場の読み値」 near the cursor and on the status line.
- The box for selecting is the same accent 1px line as the 「見えている範囲の枠」 of the overview. Notes touched by the box turn accent while the box is still moving.
- While the erase tool is over notes, the notes that will be deleted turn gray.7, the same as 「使えない」.
- When scale lock is on and the draw tool is over a row outside the scale, the cursor has the OS 「できない」 shape. Pressing does nothing, and the status line says so.
- Cursors use the OS shapes (arrow, left-right, up-down, move, copy, not allowed). Only the shapes of the draw tool and the erase tool are not in the OS, so meloet's own parts (pencil, eraser) are used.
- Velocity band: press the top end of a bar and move up or down. If there are selected notes, all the selected ones move by the same amount; if not, only the one pressed. It stops at 1 and 127.
- CC band: pressing with the draw tool adds a point. The select tool moves points. The erase tool or Delete deletes them. Points are 6×6 gray.10, and accent when selected. An on/off CC, like sustain, stops only at 0 or 127.
- Note list: pressing a field twice, or selecting a row and pressing Enter, turns it into an 「入力の枠」. Enter decides, Esc stops, Tab goes to the field on the right, and ↑ ↓ change it by 1 (by 10 with Shift; only in the pitch field, Shift gives 12 semitones = 1 octave, the same as Shift ↑ ↓ in the piano roll).
- Clips in the arrange: press and move (also to other tracks; over a track where the clip cannot be placed, the 「できない」 shape). Holding Alt (Option on Mac) copies. Mod D copies it right after itself. The 6px at the left and right ends trim the length. The trimmed part is not deleted: it is kept inside a gray.7 border and comes back when the clip is made longer again. The arrange grid is 1 bar by default.
- Horizontal zoom in and out: − ＋ on the heading row of the arrange and on the toolbar of the piano roll (Mod −, Mod ＋, the wheel with Mod held). The horizontal bar is a 「スクロールの棒」 (scroll bar) laid on its side, drawn over the very bottom of the track rows or the note area. It takes no space, so the band height calculation does not change. It is not shown when everything fits.
- In the overview of a shrunken arrange, the 「見えている範囲の枠」 follows the range that the piano roll is showing now. When the piano roll opens, it scrolls to the start of that clip (with the loop on and overlapping the clip, to the start of the loop; round 10). Dragging the border scrolls the piano roll sideways.
- When a clip with 2 or more takes is opened in the piano roll, the same switching tab (「◂ テイク 2 / 3 ▸ T」) is shown next to the clip name on the toolbar.
- Piano roll during play: the look of the notes does not change. The notes sounding now light up in accent in the keyboard column on the left (the same way as the keys of the input meter). Keys you play light up in the same column too. The play line is accent.
- Piano roll during recording: held notes grow to the right in the rec color. When released, they become the normal note color. The play line is rec. The tab says 「● テイク 3 / 3」.
- **Rules added in version 5 (after round 3)**
- Contents of the piano roll lists. Grid: 「1 小節」, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 (triplets are a separate button; when on, it says 「1/8 三連符」). Scale: under the heading 「種類」, 「メジャー」 and 「マイナー」; under the heading 「主音」, the 12 from C to B. The names are 「C メジャー」「A マイナー」 (minor is the natural minor scale). What to align: 「はじまり」, 「はじまりと終わり」.
- The scale lock button stays an on/off button. The ▾ on its right opens the scale list. Choosing from the list turns it on. When it is off, the reading is 「オフ」.
- Pressing 「そのほかの CC」 lists the CC numbers in the take other than 1, 64 and pitch bend (「CC 7」「CC 11」). Choosing one opens one band for that number (the tab says 「▾ そのほかの CC CC 7」). If there are no other CCs, it is 「使えない」. No band is made for a number that is not in the take (to write a given number yourself, use the automation of a plugin parameter).
- Opening 「CC の一覧」 lists all the CC points of the take in order of position, in 3 columns: position, kind, value. Values are written the same way as the band readings (「踏む（127）」「OFF（0）」「+2048」). The fields can be typed in, the same as in the note list. The number in the heading is the total number of points. The on/off CCs are 64 to 69.
- 「長さをそろえる」 and 「ベロシティをまとめて変える」, when opened, give one 「まとめて変える行」 row. Enter or 「そろえる」 decides. The length is typed as 「拍.ティック」 (the same as in the note list). If no notes are selected, they are 「使えない」.
- When the piano roll opens where there is no clip, the note area shows only the grid, and a hint is shown at the top left (「ここにはクリップがありません。」 and 「書く道具（2）で押すと、そこから 4 小節のクリップを作って書きます。鍵盤で弾いて録っても作られます。」). The name on the toolbar is 「1 ピアノ ・ クリップなし」. Pressing with the draw tool makes a clip on the track of 「弾くと鳴る先」, from that bar for 4 bars (up to the next clip if there is one), and draws in it. Making the clip and drawing are 1 step together. The name of the clip is the name of the track.
- When nothing is selected, the values of the 「選んだノート」 panel are empty, and the count in the heading is 「0」 in gray.8. The quantize button says 「かける」 for a take not yet quantized, and 「かけなおす」 after it is quantized. 「もとの位置へ戻す」 can be pressed only after quantizing.
- Notes drawn with the draw tool have velocity 100 (if velocity lock is on, its value).
- The width of the 「CC の一覧」 panel is size.panel.list, 216. The points are grouped by kind (sustain, pitch bend, modulation, CC 7, and so on), and each group folds by its heading (height 20, ▾ ▸, the kind name, the number of points). There are 2 columns: position (76) and value (the rest).
- In the slim form of the transport, the 「dB」 of the master value is left out (「−6.2」「−∞」). Hovering shows 「マスター ・ −6.2 dB」. 「∞」 is not in the number font (IBM Plex Mono), so it is drawn as one character of the Japanese font (1em). When deciding widths, count 「∞」 as 1em.
- When stopped, if any track is muted, the left of the status line shows 「 ・ 4 パッド はミュート」. For 2 or more: 「4 パッド ・ 5 ベース はミュート」.
- The status line shows the note names of the selected notes, up to 4. For 5 or more, the count, the first 4, and 「…」 (「選択 7 ノート ・ C3 E3 G3 C4 …」).
- Menu bar: height 24, above the transport. 「ファイル」 (「新しい曲」, 「ひらく…」, 「保存」, 「名前をつけて保存…」, 「書き出し…」, 「音の設定…」, 「meloet を終わる」); 「編集」 (「取り消す」, 「やりなおす」, 「切り取り」, 「コピー」, 「貼りつけ」, 「複製」, 「消す」, 「ぜんぶ選ぶ」, 「クリップを切る」, 「クリップをつなげる」); 「表示」 (「アレンジへ」, 「ピアノロール」, 「ミキサー」, 「取り消しの履歴」, 「棚」, 「パラメータ」, 「横に拡大」, 「横に縮小」, 「縦に拡大」, 「縦に縮小」, 「拡大率 ▸」); 「再生」 (「再生」, 「停止」, 「録音」, 「先頭へ」, 「ループ」, 「メトロノーム」, 「カウントイン ▸」); 「ノート」 (「道具 ▸」, 「クオンタイズ」, 「もとの位置へ戻す」, 「グリッド ▸」, 「三連符」, 「スケール固定 ▸」); 「トラック」 (「ミュート」, 「ソロ」, 「録音待機」, 「次のテイク」, 「前のテイク」, 「トラックを足す」, 「名前を変える」, 「色 ▸」, 「上へ動かす」, 「下へ動かす」, 「トラックを消す」); 「ヘルプ」 (「meloet について」). The keys are on the right of the rows. Things not there yet are 「使えない」. The band rows of 「表示」 and the on/off rows have a check mark on the left. 「縦に拡大」 and 「縦に縮小」 can be pressed only while the piano roll is open.
- F10 enters the menus at the first name. ← → move to the next one; then ↑ ↓ and Enter. One press of Alt does not enter them (because Alt + drag is used for copying).
- 「名前をつけて保存」 uses the PC's save window (title 「名前をつけて保存」; the first name is the name of the current song). It makes a folder named 「名前」 in the chosen place and puts 「名前.meloet」 in it. The song name also becomes that name.
- Context menus. Notes: 「切り取り」, 「コピー」, 「貼りつけ」, 「複製」, 「消す」 / 「クオンタイズ」, 「もとの位置へ戻す」. An empty place in the note area: 「貼りつけ」 (to the pressed place), 「ぜんぶ選ぶ」. Keyboard clips: 「ピアノロールでひらく」 / 「切り取り」, 「コピー」, 「貼りつけ」, 「複製」, 「消す」 / 「ここで切る」, 「つなげる」, 「名前を変える」 / 「次のテイク」, 「前のテイク」. Audio clips: the same rows without 「ピアノロールでひらく」 and 「つなげる」. Track heads: 「ピアノロールでひらく」 (keyboard tracks) / 「ミュート」, 「ソロ」, 「録音待機」. An empty place in the arrange: 「貼りつけ」 (to the pressed place), 「ここにクリップを作る」 (keyboard track), 「音のファイルを入れる…」 (vocal track). An empty place of the marker row: 「ここにマーカーを足す」. (The rows 「ここで切る」 to 「ここにマーカーを足す」 were added in round 10, user:2026-11-23; see the round 10 rules below.) Text boxes: 「切り取り」, 「コピー」, 「貼りつけ」, 「ぜんぶ選ぶ」. Anywhere else, nothing is shown.
- Right-clicking something that is not selected selects only that thing, then opens the menu. With any tool, right-click opens the context menu.
- Setting the play position: pressing the ruler of the arrange snaps to the lines visible on the ruler (finer as you zoom in, down to beats). Pressing the ruler of the piano roll snaps to its grid. In both, Shift stops the snapping. The loop range stays in whole bars. Dragging on the ruler of the piano roll also sets the loop range.
- Pressing 「1. 1. 000」 in the transport turns it into an input box right there, and you can type in the forms 「5」「5.2」「5.2.240」.
- The arrange also draws beat lines, but only when it is zoomed in enough to snap to beats (bars gray.6, beats gray.4). When there are no beat lines, the bar lines are gray.4. 「グリッド 1 小節」 on the heading row is the grid for 「クリップを動かす」 (moving clips), so it does not change with zoom.
- Playing sounds in the piano roll. The keyboard on the left: it sounds only while pressed. The strength is 100 (if velocity lock is on, its value). Pressing and sliding up or down plays one key after another. Sounding keys light up. It sounds during recording too, but it is not put into the take. Notes: a note sounds when pressed (until released; stopped at the length of the note), each time its pitch changes while dragging, and when it is moved with ↑ ↓ (for about 0.3 s). Nothing sounds when selecting with a box, on Mod A, when choosing a row in the list, or during play and recording. There is no switch for whether to sound. The keyboard picture in the input meter does not sound when pressed.
- Vertical zoom of the piano roll: the row height has 5 steps, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 (12 at first). − ＋ at the top corner of the keyboard column, Mod Shift + wheel, and the 「表示」 menu. The band height rules do not change; only the number of visible rows changes. Key names are only on the C rows.
- Velocity lock: 「固定」 at the right of the velocity reading on the input meter. While it is on, the reading shows the fixed value, and pressing the reading lets you type it. The first value is 100. Notes you play, recorded takes, notes from the draw tool, and sounds from pressing the keyboard of the piano roll all get that value. The original strength is not kept. It is remembered as a meloet setting, not in the song.
- Selecting a track: pressing a heading selects that track. Pressing a clip also selects its track. The selected track becomes 「弾くと鳴る先」.
- Mute and solo: M and S can be pressed. Shift M and Shift S work on the selected track. Mute is saved in the song and is one undoable step. Solo is a switch only for listening; it is not saved and is not a step. Even on a muted track, notes you play sound (mute removes only the playback sound). A track with both mute and solo on does not sound in playback (mute wins).
- Selecting many clips: Shift adds, Mod A, or a box from an empty place. They can be moved, deleted and copied together. Paste goes to the play position on the selected track (moved to the start of the bar). Paste from the context menu goes to the pressed place. When the clips cannot be placed because they would overlap other clips, they are not placed, and the status line says so.

- Mixer meters: always drawn at 30 Hz (whatever the number of tracks; changed in version 11; the rules of the look are the same). The key light and the play line stay at 60 Hz. The input meter band and the level bars of the track heads stay as they are.
- Bypassing an effect (「エフェクトをとばす」; 86-1; choice 13: 13-A): a check mark at the left of a filled slot. On is an accent dot; when bypassed, the mark is empty and the name is gray.7. Press the mark, press B on the chosen slot, press 「とばす」 in the heading of the params panel (while bypassed it says 「とばしている」, and the button has the 「えらんでいる」 form), or choose 「とばす」 in the context menu. One undoable step, saved in the song. The parameters can still be moved while bypassed. A stopped plugin (red border and 「止」, no mark) cannot be bypassed. Bypassed by you = gray name and empty mark; stopped = red; the color tells them apart. The name area of the slot becomes 16 narrower, so a name that does not fit is cut with 「…」, and hovering shows all of it.
- Reordering inserts (86-2): press a slot and move it up or down. An accent 2px line shows where it will go. Only within the same strip (moving to other strips is not done yet). Esc stops. Also with 「上へ動かす」 and 「下へ動かす」 in the context menu. One undoable step.
- Replacing an insert (86-3): 「入れかえる…」 in the context menu of a filled slot opens the shelf with the 「エフェクト」 list and the heading 「（トラック）のインサート（番号）を入れかえる」. The current row shows 「いまのエフェクト」. Choosing a row shows under it 「（トラック）のインサート（番号）の（いま）を、（新しい物）に入れかえます。いまの設定は残りません（取り消せます）。」 and 「入れかえる（Enter）」「やめる（Esc）」. Replacing is one undoable step, and undoing it brings back the earlier settings too.
- Effect favorites (86-4): effect rows also have the same star as instruments. Above the 「エフェクト」 list on the shelf, 「すべて ・ お気に入り」. The key for the mark is F. At first, 「リバーブ」 and 「ディレイ」 are marked. Plugins can be marked too.
- The params panel when nothing is chosen (86-5): under the heading, 「ミキサーでインサートの枠をえらぶと、ここにパラメータが出ます。」 and, in gray.8, 「音源の枠をえらぶと、音源のパラメータが出ます。」.
- Typing values in the params panel (86-6): choose a row and press Enter, or press the value twice. The value area becomes an input box (background gray.0, border gray.10), and the value can be entered without the unit. Enter decides (one undoable step), Esc stops. A value out of range is set to the end, and the status line says so. For a value with steps, Enter opens the list of steps. An on/off value switches with Enter.
- Fader, pan and send with keys (86-7): give them focus with Tab. Faders and sends move by 1 dB with ↑ ↓, by 0.1 dB with Shift. Pan moves by 1 with ← →, by 10 with Shift. Home gives the default value (fader 0.0 dB, pan C, send the amount used when the track was added). Enter to type a value. Everything until 1 second after the key is released is put together as one step.
- When there is no output (86-8): the output slot of the master shows 「出口なし ▸」 (gray.8). Pressing it opens the sound settings. The meter does not move (because no sound is flowing).
- Status line for effects (86-9; 86-10): the status line says when an effect is put in, removed, reordered, replaced, bypassed, or turned back on (chapter 6). When a parameter is chosen: 「（トラック）・（エフェクト）の「（パラメータ）」をえらんでいます」. For returns, as now: 「A リバーブの「響きの長さ」をえらんでいます」 (because the name of the return has the name of the effect in it).
- Hundreds of plugins (Y12; choice 14: 14-A): the order is 「最初から入っているもの」 → 「あとから入れたもの」. Inside 「あとから入れたもの」: the SF2 file headings (instruments only) → the headings of plugin kinds. Inside a kind, in name order, with the maker on the right of the row. LV2, VST3 and CLAP of the same plugin are put together in 1 row (the mark is the 1 format used and 「＋数」). The headings all start folded. The search box matches name, maker and kind, shows and opens only the headings with matches, and the count is 「合う数 / 全部」. Headings without matches are not shown.
- Kinds (14-A): effects, in the order 「EQ」, 「ダイナミクス」, 「リバーブ」, 「ディレイ」, 「モジュレーション」, 「ひずみ」, 「フィルター」, 「ピッチ」, 「空間」, 「分析」, 「そのほか」. Instruments, in the order 「シンセ」, 「サンプラー」, 「ドラム」, 「ピアノ ・ 鍵盤」, 「そのほか」. The kinds that a plugin gives itself (the LV2 class, the VST3 category, the CLAP features) are put together as follows. 「EQ」 ← EQ, Parametric EQ, Multi EQ (LV2); Fx|EQ (VST3); equalizer (CLAP). 「ダイナミクス」 ← Dynamics, Compressor, Limiter, Gate, Expander; Fx|Dynamics; compressor, limiter, gate, expander. 「リバーブ」 ← Reverb; Fx|Reverb; reverb. 「ディレイ」 ← Delay; Fx|Delay; delay. 「モジュレーション」 ← Modulator, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser; Fx|Modulation; chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo. 「ひずみ」 ← Distortion, Waveshaper; Fx|Distortion; distortion. 「フィルター」 ← Filter, Lowpass, Highpass, Bandpass, Comb, Allpass; Fx|Filter; filter. 「ピッチ」 ← Pitch; Fx|Pitch Shift; pitch-shifter. 「空間」 ← Spatial; Fx|Spatial; surround, mono, stereo. 「分析」 ← Analyser; Fx|Analyzer; analyzer. Instruments: Instrument|Synth, synthesizer → 「シンセ」; Instrument|Sampler, sampler → 「サンプラー」; Instrument|Drum, drum-machine → 「ドラム」; Instrument|Piano, piano → 「ピアノ ・ 鍵盤」; others → 「そのほか」. An item that gives several kinds is put in only 1 place, the first match in the order above. An item that gives none goes to 「そのほか」.
- When to scan (Y13): at the first start, everything is checked in the background (a band under the shelf; what has been checked can be used right away). From the second start, usually nothing is shown, and only the things in the plugin places that changed (added, updated, deleted) are checked in the background. Only when there is something new does the status line say so.
- 「もう一度スキャン」 is at the right of the 「あとから入れたもの」 heading on the shelf, and under 「音の設定 ▸ くわしい設定 ▸ プラグインの場所」. It checks everything again. Things that stopped in the middle of checking are skipped from the next scan on (「もう一度ためす」 checks only that one again).
- 「止まった 4 個を見る」 (Y13): the dialog 「スキャンで止まったプラグイン」 (width 640) opens. At the top, 「この 4 個は、調べている途中で止まったので、棚に入れていません。meloet と曲は無事です。次のスキャンからは、とばします。」. Each row has the name, the format mark, why it stopped (「読みこむと止まりました」「30 秒たっても終わりませんでした」「作りがこわれています」), the place, and 「もう一度ためす」. At the bottom, 「プラグインの場所…」 and 「閉じる」.
- Plugin places (Y13): besides the usual Linux places (\~/.lv2, /usr/lib/lv2, /usr/local/lib/lv2, \~/.vst3, /usr/lib/vst3, /usr/local/lib/vst3, and the places written in LV2_PATH and so on), the user can add places. The places are in 「プラグインの場所」 in 「音の設定 ▸ くわしい設定」. The usual places cannot be removed. Adding or removing a place checks only that place.
- Tracks of plugin instruments (Y14): the track name is the plugin name (「Surge XT」). If the same name already exists, 「Surge XT 2」 (the same form as 「ベース 2」 of the built-in sounds). If it does not fit, it is cut with 「…」.
- The sample ▶ of plugin instruments (Y14): it is shown. If the kind the plugin gives is 「ドラム」, the drum phrase; if 「ピアノ ・ 鍵盤」, the phrase of 「ピアノ ・ 鍵盤」; otherwise the phrase of 「シンセ」. It is played with the current sound of the plugin. The first press loads the plugin, so the time area shows 「読みこみ中…」.
- Audition of plugin instruments (Y14): choosing a row loads that plugin (meanwhile, 「Surge XT を読みこんでいます…」), and when it is loaded, the audition text appears. The window does not open by itself. A 「窓をひらく」 button is added to the audition box; pressing it opens the plugin window (for people who want to choose a sound). If the plugin cannot be loaded, 「Surge XT を読みこめませんでした。」 is shown, and no track is made.
- Plugin windows and undo (Y15; choice 15: 15-C): the changes made while you were in a plugin window are put together as 1 step when you come back to meloet (also when the window is closed, or when you move to another window). When you go into the window and when you leave it, the whole state of the plugin is copied, and if there is a difference, 「（プラグイン）の窓で変えた所」 is added to the history (with the place 「（トラック）・ プラグインの窓」). If there is no difference, nothing is added. Mod Z goes back to the state before you went into the window (both the knobs and the preset chosen inside the plugin). When something is moved in meloet's params panel while the window is open, the changes in the window up to that time first become 1 step, and then the drag in the panel becomes 1 step. Mod Z while the window has the input goes to the plugin (it is not meloet's undo). Moving things in meloet's params panel is, as now, 1 step per drag.
- **Rules added in version 9 (after round 5; 88–91; Y16–Y19)**
- CLAP (89-3): until the round that adds CLAP, 「プラグインの場所」 does not show the CLAP folders. The format mark on the shelf does not show CLAP either (the mock's 「＋2」 is 「＋1」 until that round).
- When an instrument plugin stops and the user presses 「外す」 (89-5): the track's instrument becomes a sine wave. The notes stay. It is one undoable step. The instrument name is 「サイン波」 (not 「代わり」, because the user removed it). The track name does not change.
- Song dialogs (89-6): the names are 「保存していない変更」「保存できませんでした」「ひらけない曲」. The status line says 「「（ダイアログの名前）」のダイアログをひらいています」 (the song name is not shown).
- When several files are dragged in (89-10): all SF2 files are added, in the order they came. Files that are not SF2 are skipped. The box in the middle says 「3 つのファイル ・ SF2 は 2 つ」.
- The 「最初から入っているもの」 heading (89-11): from round 5, it is a heading that folds, the same as the other headings (▸ ▾ and a count). While searching, it is not shown if nothing in it matches. Read the row of F5-SF2Shelf that does not fold as this form. In round 4 (no plugins), the heading is not shown (as now).
- A plugin that could not be loaded (89-12): on the shelf, under the chosen row, the box that tells about missing items (a 2px gray.8 line on the left). The text is 「Surge XT を読みこめませんでした。こわれているか、この PC では動かないのかもしれません。」 with the button 「やめる（Esc）」. When a plugin of the song is there but could not be loaded, that track plays with a sine wave, and the instrument row says 「サイン波で代わり」.
- The text on hover over 「サイン波で代わり」 (89-9): for the sound data, as now. For SF2: 「FluidR3_GM.sf2 が見つからないので、サイン波で鳴らしています。」. For a plugin: 「Surge XT が見つからないので、サイン波で鳴らしています。」; when it could not be loaded: 「Surge XT を読みこめなかったので、サイン波で鳴らしています。」.
- Return names (90): if the top insert is a plugin, the name of its category from 14-A (「A リバーブ」). If the category is 「そのほか」, the plugin name (「A MVerb」).
- When the mixer opens (90): if there are bypassed slots or stopped slots, the status line tells about them together, only once, when it opens (chapter 6).
- Plugin windows (90, 91-1, 91-2): the status line also tells when a window opens. For one window: 「Surge XT の窓がひらいています」; for two or more: 「プラグインの窓が 3 つひらいています」. When a window could not be shown, the status line tells, and the params panel can still be used (chapter 6).
- Parameter values (90): a row whose value is unknown shows 「—」. A list of stepped values whose names the plugin does not give is shown by number (「1」「2」…). The place of a window opened during audition is 「棚 ・ ためし弾き」.
- Parameters that only receive the keyboard's knobs (91-3): the ones a plugin sets up like 「MIDI CC 0|0」 to 「MIDI CC 15|129」 (in VST3, the ones only for receiving the keyboard's knobs) are normally not shown. They are not counted in the params panel either. Only when searching are they shown, inside the group 「鍵盤のつまみを受ける所」. Under the list: 「ほかに、鍵盤のつまみを受けるだけの 2080 個（MIDI CC 0|0 〜 15|129）は出していません。さがすと出ます。」. Read 「2855」 in F-ParamMany as 「774」 in F6-ParamMany.
- Right-click and windows (91-4): 「押すと」 in 「パラメータ」 of chapter 3 means a left click. A right-click does not open the window.
- What can have automation (Y16): volume, pan, send amount, mute, parameters of the built-in effects, plugin parameters (instruments too). Bypass cannot (bypass is a switch for when making the song).
- Automation points (Y16; the same in every option): pressing an empty place in the band adds a point (with the value of the line at the pressed position). Dragging a point: up and down for the value, sideways for the position (snaps to the grid). The original point stays as an accent border. While it moves, a reading appears in place: 「+0. 1. 000 ・ −2.1 → −0.6 dB」. Dragging from an empty place selects the points it encloses (a 1px accent border). A point is deleted by pressing it twice, or by selecting it and pressing Delete. Esc goes back to before the move. One drag is one step.
- Between points, the line is straight. Stepped values, on/off, and mute use a stair shape (they switch at the point).
- Bands (Y16): added from the 「▸ パン ▸ プラグインのパラメータ」 row under the track head, or with the button on the right of a row in the params panel (「オートメーションに書く」). The band heading shows 「オートメーション ・ （何を）」, the current value, and the number of points. ▾ folds it. 「帯を消す」 in the right-click menu deletes the band together with its points (one undoable step).
- When a clip moves (Y16): automation points belong to the track, so they do not move with the clip. What moves with the clip is the CC band of the piano roll (the keyboard's movements inside the clip). Their shapes also tell them apart: the CC band uses bars, the automation band uses a line.
- How automation is written (Y16; choice 16: 16-B): points can be drawn on the band at any time. To record, turn on 「● 書く」 in the band heading, play, and move that parameter by hand. Values are written over only while it is held (for a fader, while its handle is held); on release, writing stops and the value goes back to following the line. The part being written over is shown with a 1px border in the rec color. When playback stops, 「書く」 turns off again. One pass of writing (from press to release) is one step. Moving it by hand during playback while 「書く」 is off leads to 「休み」 below.
- Moving by hand (Y16; choice 17: 17-A): when a parameter that has automation is moved by hand (stopped or playing), the automation of that parameter goes to 「休み」. The value stays where the hand put it and does not go back by itself during playback. The band's line is gray.7, and the lower line of the heading shows 「もどす」 and 「休み ・ （いまの値）」. In the mixer, the knob border goes back to normal, and under the value are 「休み」 and 「もどす」. Pressing 「もどす」 goes back to moving along the line. Resting and 「もどす」 are each one undoable step, and are saved in the song. While automation is moving it, the knob border is accent, and under the value is 「オートメーション」.
- Gradual tempo (Y17; choice 18: 18-C): the right-click menu of a tempo point has 「次の点まで、だんだん」 (a check item). When it is on, the tempo changes in a straight line from that point to the next point (the line slopes, with 「だんだん」 above it). A time signature cannot change gradually (it only switches at the start of a bar). During a gradual change, the time display, grid times, metronome, and export are counted by following the changing tempo (the ruler numbers stay in bars). The transport tempo is the current value where the play line is (for a gradual change from 104 at bar 5 to 120 at bar 9, it is 112.00 at bar 7). How to add: pressing twice on the lower line (tempo) of the opened band puts a tempo point at the start of that beat; pressing twice on the upper line (time signature) puts a time signature flag at the start of that bar. Move a point up and down for the tempo; move a point or flag sideways for the position. Press it twice to type a value. Select and press Delete to delete. The band headings are 「テンポ 96.00 ほか 3」「拍子 4/4 ほか 2」.
- Tempo and time signature changes (Y17; the same in every option): a time signature changes only at the start of a bar. A tempo can change at the start of a beat. After a change, the ruler numbers stay in bars, and the grid and metronome follow the new time signature (the strong sound on beat 1 too). The time display and export are counted by following the tempo changes. The tempo and time signature of the transport are the values where the play line is. Adding, moving, changing the value of, and deleting a change are each one undoable step.
- Markers (Y18): K adds a marker at the play line (snaps to the beat). The name is 「マーカー 4」 (the number is the number of markers in the song), and it is added with its name box open. Rename: press it twice, F2, or right-click. Move: drag sideways (snaps to the beat; the reading is the position). Delete: select and press Delete, or right-click. Pressing a marker goes to its position. Alt → goes to the next marker, Alt ← to the previous one. The right-click menu has 「ここから次のマーカーまでをループに」 (for the last marker, up to the end of the song). The names are also shown in the ruler row of the piano roll (they cannot be pressed). Each of these is one undoable step (going to a marker is not a step). Also (round 10, user:2026-11-23): pressing an empty place of the marker row twice, or 「ここにマーカーを足す」 in its context menu, adds a marker there (snapped to the beat), with its name box open, the same as K.
- The undo history band (Y19): the opened band looks as in F-History. Pressing a row selects it (background gray.4, with 「ここまで戻す Enter」 on the right). 「ここまで戻す」 or Enter makes that step 「いまここ」. The steps above it stay, in gray.8 with 「やりなおせます」. Rows above the current step work the same: it redoes up to there. Doing something new removes the steps that could be redone (the text of the band heading stays as now). Keys: ↑ ↓ select, Home goes to the current step, Enter goes back, Esc folds the band.
- Step names (Y19): a history row is 「「（手の名前）」・ （何を）」. The step names are 「ノートを書く」, 「ノートを消す」, 「ノートを動かす」, 「ノートを複製する」, 「ノートの長さを変える」, 「ベロシティを変える」, 「クオンタイズ」, 「CC の点を書く」, 「CC の点を動かす」, 「CC の点を消す」, 「録音」 (a take), 「テイクを消す」, 「クリップを書く」, 「クリップを動かす」, 「クリップを複製する」, 「クリップを切る」, 「クリップの長さを変える」, 「クリップを消す」, 「トラックを足す」, 「トラックを消す」, 「トラックの名前を変える」, 「トラックを並べかえる」, 「トラックの色を変える」, 「音源を入れかえる」, 「エフェクトを入れる」, 「エフェクトを外す」, 「エフェクトを並べかえる」, 「エフェクトを入れかえる」, 「エフェクトをとばす」, 「エフェクトをもどす」, 「パラメータを変える」 (「コンプレッサーの「しきい値」を変える」), 「音量を変える」, 「パンを変える」, 「センドを変える」, 「ミュート」, 「（プラグイン）の窓で変えた所」, 「オートメーションの点を書く」, 「オートメーションの点を動かす」, 「オートメーションの点を消す」, 「帯を消す」, 「テンポを変える」, 「拍子を変える」, 「テンポ ・ 拍子の変更を足す」, 「テンポ ・ 拍子の変更を消す」, 「マーカーを足す」, 「マーカーの名前を変える」, 「マーカーを動かす」, 「マーカーを消す」, 「ループの範囲を変える」 (92-8; turning the loop on or off is not a step), 「テイクを切りかえる」, 「ノートを貼りつける」, 「もとの位置へ戻す」, 「クリップを貼りつける」, 「見つからない物を曲から外す」, 「なおすためのファイルから戻す」, 「戻した変更をすてる」, 「音源を外す」, 「テンポ ・ 拍子の変更を動かす」, 「だんだんにする」, 「だんだんをやめる」, 「帯を足す」, 「オートメーションを書く」 (one pass of 「● 書く」), 「オートメーションを休ませる」, 「オートメーションをもどす」 (92-7; all still the developers' provisional names). For a step typed into a field, the name depends on the field: 「ノートを動かす」「ノートの長さを変える」「ベロシティを変える」. Solo is not a step (a rule since version 5; 92-4).

- Where the sound data can be fetched later (Y26): 「音の設定 ▸ くわしい設定」 has a 「音の素材」 row (「ぜんぶあります ・ 240 MB」 or 「まだ 12 / 31 ・ 取りにいく」, and the location). The shelf's missing-item notice (85) also has 「取りにいく」. When there is a new pack, the status line tells once at start (「取りにいく」). The location is shown in the advanced settings row, and under the 「音の素材」 heading on the second level of 「meloet について」.
- The reason text given to the PC when meloet asks it to wait before shutting down (logind) stays in English (「Writes unsaved changes to the recovery file」). It is not shown on the screen.
- The scale is chosen with 「100 %」 at the far right of the status line. It can also be changed with keys (Mod Alt 1, 2, 3). A scale at which the smallest window (853×448 times the scale) does not fit in the usable area of the current screen cannot be chosen. Pressing the key does not change it either, and the status line gives the reason. When the current window would be smaller than the smallest size at the chosen scale, the window is widened to that size. If the screen becomes smaller and the current scale no longer fits, it is lowered to the largest scale that fits, and the status line tells.
- When the width is not enough, the right side of the status line hides items in this order: 「入口」 → 「出口」 → 「バッファ」. 「窓」 and 「拡大率」 are not removed. If the text on the left still does not fit, its end is shortened with 「…」.
- An earlier take is chosen again with the switch tab on the clip. Switching with ◂ ▸ or T replaces the content of the clip with the earlier take. Switching can be undone. To listen, switch and play. 「いいとこ取り」 (choosing a different take for each place) is not made yet (its place stays as the band under the track).
- When recording during a loop, each pass becomes a new take. It does not record over the earlier pass. A key held across the boundary between passes goes into the take of the pass where it was pressed, and its length is cut at the end of the loop. It does not go into the take of the next pass. If the sustain pedal is held down across passes, "held down" is written at the start of the next pass's take. A pass with no notes does not become a take. When stopped, the take shown is the take of the last pass that has notes. With 「重ねる」 on (round 10), a keyboard track adds every pass to the take instead (see the round 10 rules).
- Inputs are handled the same as outputs. Inputs that cannot be chosen from meloet are grouped under the heading 「いまは使えない入口」, with a text that gives the reason.
- In a window so low that even a single open lower band does not fit, the band is not folded. It gets the remaining height; the band's toolbar does not move, and only the content scrolls vertically. The height is never made 0.
- 「棚」 in the Esc order means the whole right panel. Whether it shows the shelf or the parameters, it closes at the same turn. The 「窓」 list in the status line closes at the same turn as menus.
- When two or more keyboards are connected, meloet listens to all of them. The name in the input meter is the name of the keyboard played last, followed by the number of the others, like 「+1」. Hovering shows all the names.
- Outputs that cannot be chosen from meloet (jacks inside the same device that the PC switches between, outputs turned off in the PC settings) are grouped under the heading 「いまは使えない出口」, which starts folded. The heading shows the count. When it is opened, a reason text appears on each line. When outputs with the same name are listed, the device name is added next to each to tell them apart.
- When the PC does not report whether something is plugged into an output, neither a mark nor a text is shown. The order is: plugged in, unknown, not plugged in.
- While sound is not running yet, the status line shows 「出口: まだ」 and does not show 「バッファ」. While there is no keyboard: 「入口: まだ」.
- The output chooser (flow 2) also works like a dialog when it does not fit in the window. The question line and the 「ここから鳴らす」 line do not move; only the list between them scrolls vertically. When the width is not enough, 「あとから「音の設定」で変えられます」 is hidden.
- Hint texts do not tell the user to press a part that is disabled now. A text that mentions a disabled part is not shown until that part can be used.
- The keyboard in the slim input meter also draws black keys (width 5, height 7). A pressed black key lights up in accent.

- **Rules added in round 10 (the owner's answers to the improvement notes, user:2026-11-23)**
- Clip names: a clip's name can be changed from four places: pressing the name on the clip twice (pressing the waveform of an audio clip twice still makes its row 160 high, as now), F2 while a clip is chosen (with only a track chosen, F2 renames the track), 「名前を変える」 in the clip's context menu, and the name at the top of the params panel 「選んだクリップ」. An input box opens right there, the same as for the track name: Enter decides, Esc stops, an empty name keeps the old name. One undoable step 「クリップの名前を変える」. The look of the box follows the mock P10-ClipName once the owner has chosen.
- Overdub on keyboard tracks: the switch 「重ねる」 on the transport. While it is on, the notes recorded on a keyboard track are added to the clip there instead of making a new take; in loop recording, every pass adds its notes. The result is an edit of the current take: the number of takes does not grow, and undo goes back to before. Vocal tracks always record takes. The switch is a meloet setting (not saved in the song) and not an undoable step. The look follows the mock P10-Overdub once the owner has chosen.
- Splitting at a place: 「ここで切る」 in a clip's context menu cuts that clip where it was right-clicked, snapped to the lines the ruler shows (finer as you zoom in, down to beats). S and 「編集 ▸ クリップを切る」 cut at the play line, as before.
- Joining clips (keyboard clips only): with two or more keyboard clips of the same track chosen, Mod J, 「つなげる」 in the context menu, or 「編集 ▸ クリップをつなげる」 makes one clip from the start of the first to the end of the last. The gaps become empty parts of the clip. The clip keeps the id and the name of the first one. Only the takes shown now are joined; the other takes are not kept (undo brings them back). One undoable step 「クリップをつなげる」. With audio clips, clips of different tracks, or only one clip chosen, these rows are disabled.
- A clip on an empty place: pressing an empty place of a keyboard track twice, or 「ここにクリップを作る」 in its context menu, makes an empty clip from that bar for 4 bars (up to the next clip if there is one), chooses it, and opens it in the piano roll. The name is the track name. One undoable step 「クリップを書く」.
- 「ピアノロールでひらく」 in the context menu of a keyboard track head selects the track and opens the piano roll. When there is no clip at the play line, the piano roll shows the hint 「ここにはクリップがありません。」 as described above.
- 「先頭へ」 (Home, the transport button, 「再生 ▸ 先頭へ」): with the loop on, it goes to the start of the loop; when the play position is already there, to the start of the song. With the loop off, to the start of the song.
- Opening a clip in the piano roll with the loop on: when the loop range overlaps the clip, the horizontal zoom is the largest step where the loop range fits, and the view starts at the start of the loop. Otherwise the zoom fits the clip, as before.
- Chord help in the piano roll: (1) the chord stamp: a chord shape is chosen for the draw tool, and pressing writes the notes of that chord with the pressed key as the root, as one step 「ノートを書く」; (2) the chord table of the key: with the scale fixed, the chords of that key are listed by name; pressing one plays it, and it can be placed with the draw tool in the same way. The look and the list of shapes follow the mocks P10-ChordStamp and P10-ChordTable once the owner has chosen.
- Keyboard buttons (Mackie Control; checked with the Keystation 49 MK3): notes that come in on the second and later ports of a keyboard are buttons. They never sound and are never recorded. Play 94, stop 93, record 95 (the same as R), rewind 91 and fast forward 92 (one bar), cycle 86 (loop on / off), up 96 and down 97 (select the track above / below), left 98 and right 99 (move the play position one bar back / forward, to the start of a bar), centre 100 (loop on / off). The keyboard's fader (CC 7) sets the volume of the selected track: it starts to move only after the fader has passed the current volume (again after another track is selected), changes only the sound while moving, and becomes one step 「音量を変える」 about 0.5 s after it stops. CC 7 is not recorded and not sent to the sound. CC 123 (all notes off) and CC 121 (reset all controllers) stop the notes that keyboard is holding; 121 also centres the pitch bend, sets the modulation to 0 and lifts the sustain. Settings made inside the keyboard (octave, transpose, channel, and so on) send nothing to meloet. The HUI and plain MIDI modes of the buttons are not read.
